## What you see

Above the books and lot cards of each account, a block "Ledger" with two tabs, "By month" and "By company". It counts the lots that have ended (closed, expired, assigned, exercised) and never an open one: a month figure is of lots that ended in that month, so it does not move after the month is over. A lot belongs to the month it ended in, whole (its premium and every day of its split are in that month). Every figure has its lot count beside it. With fewer than 20 split lots the group says "no conclusion" and its figures are grey. Numbers are invented here and add up the way the code adds them (the by-month group below has 5 split lots and 1 lot with no stored quotes).

Labels, numbers and currency words are English; every explanation, hint and the no-conclusion sentence is Chinese, held as string constants in `zh_texts.go` (Decision 7); the Chinese words above are written as English glosses so that this design file shows the figures. The sample's figures add up as the code adds them (5 split lots, 1 lot with no stored quotes; 537.80 = 525.80 + 12.00).

A month with no ended lot is not listed, except the month of the page day, which reads `noEndedMonthZh` (Chinese: no ended lots this month). Numbers older than the first stored quotes (2026-11-07) are "no stored quotes: no split" and have a ledger figure only, as on the lot cards.

## Goal

The plan's question for the month end: how much of what I earned selling puts was rent (time) against the cost of moves, per month and per company, and is the sample big enough to say so. The page answers with the ledger's own figures first and the split beside them, and says "no conclusion" when the sample is small, so a lucky month is not read as a method. No new page, no migration, no write.

## Decisions

1. **The ledger is a block of the same page, not a new page** (plan 月底 section: no separate review page). `Account` gets an optional field `ledger` (`Ledger` or null), added to `page.option_pnl_page.v1` without a version change, as `underlyings` was added to `option_data_page.v1`. A payload without it stays valid; the builder writes it for every account, null for an account with a gap. The route, order and TopNav label are unchanged; `cmd/ot` is not edited: `Config` is unchanged (the window is a constant, Decision 4).
2. **What a ledger counts.** Short puts of the alias that have ended (status closed, expired, assigned or exercised), never an open lot (an open lot's figure is a mark that reverts; the cards below show it; the block says how many open lots it leaves out). The month of a lot is the month of its `ClosedOn` (text `YYYY-MM`); the company of a lot is `Underlying`. Currencies are never added: each group has one book per currency, in order of first appearance. The month is whole-lot: a lot opened in October and closed in November is in November, with its October days (named in Left out; splitting by day is a compute change).
3. **Two views, one set of lots.** By month: the month of the page day and the five months before it (`ledgerMonths = 6`, trial), newest first (built from the page day, by year and month number with day 1, never by subtracting months from a day: no end-of-month shift); a month with no ended lot is not listed except the month of the page day. By company: one group per `Underlying` over the same six months, in order of first appearance in the replay (pages sorts nothing; `purity_test.go` forbids `sort`). **Decided: by company is over the six months, not over the page-day month alone.** One company seldom has 20 split lots in one month, so a one-month company group would always say "no conclusion" and say nothing else; over six months it can reach 20, and the 20-lot rule of the plan (appendix A) is what keeps a thin company group from being read as a method. The plan says only "按月/按公司切换" and gives the sample rule "fewer than 20, no conclusion"; the two views use one window and one rule. Quotes are stored only since 2026-11-07, so until about February most company groups will read "no conclusion" and show the ledger line only; that is the rule working, not a gap. One constant (`ledgerMonths`) moves both views if the replay (plan step 12) shows another window is better.
4. **The lots of the ledger are read in a wider window; the cards stay as they are.** `selectLots` keeps a lot when it is open, ended within `RecentDays`, or ended on or after the first day of the oldest ledger month (`ledgerMonths`, a constant in the package). A `pnlLot` gets `card bool`, true for the lots of today's rule. Cards, the books of the account, `roll`, the sample note and the notes of skipped calls and skipped bought puts (`gathered.calls`, `gathered.longs`) use only the lots with `card` true (a call or a bought put that ended before the 30 days is in no count and in no ledger group; the ledger counts short puts only), so the output above the ledger is what it is today for the same data, card ids included (the id counter runs over card lots only, never renumbered). The data read is wider: the oldest open day, and with it the DGS3MO window (`ratesOf` from 14 days before it), move back; the filter covers every consumer of the lot list (`ended`, `results`, `roll`, `bookCards`, `statusCounts`, `lotCard`) and the two counters of `selectLots`. The rows of `option_day` are read from the oldest open day of all kept lots (more rows: Decision 11).
5. **Pages groups, compute sums.** The pages builder puts each attributed ended result in the bucket of its month and of its company (a map lookup on a text key) and calls `Booker.Combine` once per bucket; it adds no figure and no count (`purity_test.go` stays as it is). One thing is missing in compute: the ledger P&L of a bucket over every lot that has one, and the count of those lots. `Book` gets `AllLots` (= `Lots` + `NoQuoteLots` + `UnreconciledLots`) and `LedgerAllPnL` (= `LedgerPnL` + `NoQuoteLedgerPnL` + `UnreconciledLedgerPnL`), filled by `Combine` with exact decimal addition (Interface, entry 1). A lot given to no `Combine` (a gap lot: no close, no ledger P&L, input refused) is in neither; the group's `left_out` names it with its ledger text as the books do today (`leftOut` is reused). A bucket's `Combine` of results equals the sum of the `Combine` of any partition of them, which a compute test proves (`TestCombineBucketsSumToAll`).
6. **Sample count beside every number, "no conclusion" under 20.** The line "Ledger P&L, every ended lot counted" follows the headline and is never muted or gated (its n is `AllLots`, which can be 20 or more while the split lots are few). Every line of a ledger book ends with ` · n lots` (`1 lot`), where n is `Book.Lots` for every split figure (time, move cost, rent minus move cost, direction, IV re-marking, not split, entry, exit, commissions, total, assignment intrinsic, ledger says) and `Book.AllLots` for the line "Ledger P&L, every ended lot counted". When `Book.Lots` is under 20 (the constant `ledgerMinLots = 20`, trial, plan appendix A) the book's first line, label `Sample`, has the text `noConclusionZh` (Chinese: no conclusion, the sample is too small) followed by ` (n split lots, fewer than 20)`, with tone `muted`, and the headline "Rent minus move cost" and every split line of the book have tone `muted`; with 20 or more the `Sample` line is absent; the figures stay visible, a sample is never hidden. The count that decides is of split lots (the lots the figures are sums of), not of ended lots; the account's `sample_note` on the card list keeps counting ended lots of the last 30 days, and the ledger says which count it uses.
7. **Texts** (pages formats, web shows). Money: `$` for USD, `JPY 1,234.50` for another currency, thousands separators, two decimals half away from zero, U+2212 for minus; percent one decimal (the ledger has no percent). **Chinese texts** (owner constraint 2026-11-17): the hint of every jargon label, the note of the `Ledger`, the no-conclusion sentence and the empty-month summary are Chinese and are string constants of `internal/pages/option_pnl_page/zh_texts.go` (names `ledgerNoteZh`, `noConclusionZh`, `noEndedMonthZh` and one `ledgerHint<Name>Zh` for each of rent, ledger-all, time, move cost, direction, IV re-marking, entry, total, assignment intrinsic, ledger says), each hint at most 200 characters; the hint constants are not the English `hints` of `strings.go`, and `ledger.go` maps a line id suffix to its constant. Labels ("Ledger", "By month", "By company", "Sample" and the line labels of the cards), the figures, the currency words, the group label (`2026-11` or the company name) and every count are English. A constant holds one Chinese sentence and no number; a number is appended in English by `ledger.go`. The cards' own hints stay English in this design (Left out).
8. **Schema.** `Ledger` = `title`, `note` (`ledgerNoteZh` followed by the open-lot count as English text, "2 open lots"), `views` (array of `LedgerView`). `LedgerView` = `id` (enum `by_month`, `by_company`), `label`, `groups` (array of `Group`). `Group` = `id` (`^grp-[A-Za-z0-9:_-]{1,96}$`), `label`, `summary` (English counts, or `noEndedMonthZh` for an empty page-day month), `books` (array of the existing `Book`). The `Book` of a group is the existing def: its `id` is `book-lg-<view>-<group>-<currency>` (cut to 96), its `counts` reads "USD, 5 split lots of 6 ended", its `left_out` is the line of Decision 5, its `headline` is "Rent minus move cost" with its figure and lot count, its `lines` are the `Sample` line when the book is under 20 split lots, the ledger-all line, the split lines and the "Ledger says" line. The ledger book is built by its own function in `ledger.go` (not `bookCards`, whose empty-book branch tests `Total == ""` and `Combine` gives "0"); it reuses `splitLines`, `ledgerLine` and `leftOut`, then replaces each line's hint by its Chinese constant. Ids carry indexes, never names: book `book-lg-<view>-<g>-<c>` and line `lg<view><g>-<c>-<line>` with view `m` or `c` and g, c the group and book index, so no id passes 64 characters (Line ids are limited to 64) and none collides. All figures are text; there is no number field.
9. **Gaps.** An account with a gap (`no_reader`, `no_flex_rows`, `no_sessions`, `no_quote_reader`) has `ledger` null and the existing gap card. An account with no ended short put in the six months has a `Ledger` whose views each have the one group of the page-day month reading "no ended lots this month". Quote and close reads are those of the cards (Decision 12 of `options-pnl-page.md`): an ended lot with no stored close is a gap lot, named in `left_out`, never in a sum.
10. **Web** renders and computes nothing. The tabs are a switch between two lists the payload already holds, not a filter or a sort; the default tab is `by_month`. A tab, a group and a book render texts only; a book with `left_out` shows it under the headline; a group with no books shows its `summary`.
11. **Cost.** The wider window reads the stored `option_day` rows of every short put that ended in six months, from its open day, in chunks of 500 instrument ids, as the cards do now, and attributes each lot once (the same result is used by the card and by the ledger). Trial: six months; the size is named, not measured (the page is built per request, as today). If it proves slow the fix is `ledgerMonths`, not a cache (no cache table, plan 不做的事).
12. **No migration, no write, no clock in compute, no `cmd/ot` change.** `docs/contracts/ot/pages/option_pnl_page.md` says nothing about the window and is not edited; `docs/contracts/pages.md` gets one paragraph (entry 3).

## Interface

Entry 1 (module compute, interface `internal/compute/optionattrapi/api.go`, declarations only; the sums of lots are a compute rule, so they get an interface entry first, as Decision 4 of `options-pnl-page.md` did): `Book` gains two fields and nothing else changes.

    // AllLots is Lots + NoQuoteLots + UnreconciledLots: the lots given to Combine that have a ledger P&L.
    // LedgerAllPnL is LedgerPnL + NoQuoteLedgerPnL + UnreconciledLedgerPnL, exact, not rounded.
    // Neither includes a lot that was not given to Combine.
    AllLots      int
    LedgerAllPnL string

and the rule of `Combine` stated in the doc: the `Book` of a set of results is the sum of the `Book`s of any partition of the set (per currency), for every sum field and for the two new ones, so a caller may bucket the results by any key and call `Combine` once per bucket; `Book` has no day and no company, the caller keeps the key. `NewBooker()` fills the two fields. There is no new type and no new method: a `Bucketer` would only move a map lookup out of pages (Adversarial 3).
