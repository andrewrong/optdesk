Goal: for each account, one page that lists every open short option the owner holds, with its moneyness and distance from the strike, days left, the premium taken and how much of it is kept at the mid mark, the IBKR model delta and IV, the next results date and ex-dividend date inside its life, and the early-assignment guard of a short call; below it the short options closed in the last 30 days (expired, assigned, closed) as Flex tells it, and a short check table of Flex against the live account positions. Nothing is stored, nothing is pushed.

## Decisions

1. A second page, board 21 untouched. The new page is `page.option_tracking_page.v1` (title `OptionTrackingPage`), route `/options/tracking`, package `internal/pages/option_tracking_page`. The system-outcome page of board 21 keeps its schema `page.tracking.v2` and gets, when its history exists, its own route (`/options/system-tracking`, a later design, named so that the two addresses never collide). The word "tracking" in the pages of this repository therefore means two things; the page titles say which (`系统期权跟踪` and `我的卖方期权追踪`).
2. Truth and scope. A row is a position of the Flex replay (`compute.OptionLedger`, per account alias, nothing stored; `option-ledger.md` Decision 16) with a negative quantity: a sold put or a sold call. Flex is the truth for assignment, exercise and expiry, so a status other than `open` comes only from Flex. Long legs are not rows; the page says how many it left out ("N 个多头腿未列出,见期权持仓页") so nothing is silent. Spreads are not grouped (each leg is a row, as in the ledger). One account group per alias that `StoredAliases` returns; the page works with one alias (the main account) and shows the second as a group of its own or a gap `no_flex_rows` when it has none.
3. Day-late Flex is shown, not hidden. Flex arrives after the close, so a contract sold or assigned today is not in it yet. A small check table per account compares the open short options of the replay with `AccountReader.Positions` of the newest day with rows (issue 5) and lists the differences with a code: `not_in_flex` (the account holds a short option that no open ledger position has, with its terms and quantity only, no premium, no figure) and `not_in_account` (an open ledger position the account no longer holds: probably assigned or expired today, Flex pending). The check never changes a row or a figure; a missing account reader or capture gives the table the gap `no_account` and leaves the rest. The match key is underlying symbol, expiry, strike and right. Spread legs of the account are matched leg by leg for the same reason.
4. One rule, in compute, pure. A new interface `trackapi.OptionTracker` with `Track(TrackInput, TrackParams) TrackResult` (declared in the declarations-only package `internal/compute/trackapi` (Decision 14), implemented by `internal/compute/optiontrack` with `math/big`, no float, no clock, no I/O; the pattern of `compute.OptionLedger`). All numbers of the page are made there: DTE, moneyness, distance, mid, extrinsic, premium kept, event flags. The builder only formats. The mark rule is the ledger's rule (mid of bid and ask, bid not negative, ask positive, at most `MarkMaxAgeSessions` session days old, else `no_mark`), and a test checks that the kept amount of `optiontrack` equals premium income minus the buy-back value of `optionledger`'s own `UnrealizedPnL` plus commissions for the same marks (the two rules never drift).
5. Fields of a row. `dte` is the calendar-day difference between the expiry and the as-of day (the owner's `option-candidates.md` DTE is the same count). `moneyness` is `itm`, `otm`, `atm` (only when spot equals strike exactly) or `unknown` (no spot); `otm_percent` is signed text with two places, half even: for a put `(spot - strike) / strike x 100`, for a call `(strike - spot) / strike x 100`, positive out of the money, negative in the money (an owner-readable "how far from the strike"). `mid` and `mark_day` come from the newest `option_eod` row within the age limit; `extrinsic` is `max(0, mid - max(0, spot - strike))` for a call and `max(0, mid - max(0, strike - spot))` for a put, shown for both and used by nothing. `premium_income` is the ledger's. `kept` is `premium_income + quantity x multiplier x mid` in the contract currency (`quantity` is negative, so the product is the negative buy-back value and is subtracted; the ledger's `unrealized_pnl` is `premium_income - commissions + value`, `premium.go`), before commissions; `kept_percent` is `kept / premium_income x 100`, two places. Both are empty with the gap `no_mark` when there is no fresh mark, never 0. Premium and assigned basis stay separate (owner): an assigned row shows its premium and the strike text, and the page never subtracts one from the other.
6. Greeks and IV are IBKR model values from the same `option_eod` row (delta, IV), shown as stored; a missing one is a gap cell, never computed, never 0.
7. Events in the life. The next results date and the next ex-date with its amount come from the stored candidate rows of the same underlying (any kind, newest day with rows within 7 days, the lookback of `option-candidates-page.md` Decision 3), because the candidate job already reads the watch list and the event stores; the page opens no second path to them. `earnings_in_life` is true when the next results date lies between the as-of day and the expiry, inclusive; `ex_in_life` likewise. A company with no candidate row has the gap `no_event_row` in both cells (shown, not hidden). The ex-date of a short put is not shown (it does not drive a put's assignment; owner 2026-11-09, Decision 13).
8. Early-assignment guard. For a short call the guard is the stored verdict of the row of kind `held_call_guard` that matches alias, underlying, expiry and strike (`option-candidates.md` Decision 12: `extrinsic < dividend x div_guard_factor` is `block early_assignment_risk`; `no_dividend_info` and `stale_dividend` are `unknown`); the page shows its status as a badge and the reason once, and writes no verdict of its own. A short call with no matching row has the guard `unknown` with the code `no_guard_row`, never `ok`. A short put has no guard (the cell is empty with the text "不适用"); the page computes no second guard rule, so there is one rule in the repository. The guard's own extrinsic uses the bid (Decision 12 of the candidates design), the page's `extrinsic` column uses the mid (Decision 5): the column header says "时间价值(中间价)" and the guard cell says its basis, so the two numbers are not read as one.
9. Recently closed. A second table per account lists the short positions whose status is not `open` and whose closing day is within `RecentDays` (30) days of the as-of day: expired, assigned (with `Early` as a badge "提前"), exercised or closed, with premium income, commissions and realized P and L as the ledger has them. Totals, strategy sums, the P and L chart, structures and roll credits are the page of `option-positions` (seam below); this page has no totals row and no sum of any column.
10. No push. The page is served by `ot serve` on the local machine as the other account pages are. The builder, the rule and the route import no `notify` and no Telegram code (grep-checked), and no row or field holds the margin cushion or the assignment cash (strike x quantity x multiplier is not a field). `option-notices` may call `trackapi.OptionTracker` for its own lines; what it sends and to where is its decision, and ADR 0010 stands.
11. Empty and partial states, each named. No ledger reader is the page gap `no_reader`; an alias with no Flex rows is the group gap `no_flex_rows`; open rows exist but no fresh mark gives the row `no_mark` cells and the page `stale` label; no guard row, no event row and no account capture are their own gaps; a store error fails the build (the route answers 500, as `/market` does). A group with no open short option shows the sentence "没有未平仓的卖方期权" and still shows its recent table.
12. Frontend stack (ADR 0034): shadcn components from `components/ui`, `@tanstack/react-table` through `components/data-table/data-table.tsx` for every table, lucide icons; no chart, no new dependency. 
13. Reaching the page and the owner's four answers (owner 2026-11-09). (a) The top bar gets no tenth item of its own: `option-positions` (design PR 747, issue 12 `option-positions-nav`) turns the bar into one `期权持仓` dropdown (the existing `期权` item at `/options` stays; the bar has ten items) (shadcn dropdown-menu), and this design's small web issue 9 (`option-tracking-menu-link`, not the view issue) adds the link `追踪` (`/options/tracking`) into that menu in `web/src/shell/TopNav.tsx` and its test; issue 9 is cut only when issue 8 (the route) and `option-positions-nav` are on main, so the menu never links to a 404 and no issue of this design waits for another design's unmerged work. (b) A short put has no ex-dividend date (Decision 7 stands, no reader is added). (c) A short put has no guard (Decision 8 stands, no threshold, no badge). (d) The recent window is 30 days (`RecentDays` 30, Decision 9).
14. Where the interface lives (owner decision 2026-11-09, answer "a"). The types `OptionTracker`, `NewOptionTrackerFunc`, `TrackInput`, `TrackParams`, `TrackResult`, `TrackedOption`, `TrackQuote`, `TrackEvent`, `TrackHeld` and `TrackRecon` are declared in the new package `internal/compute/trackapi` (`api.go`, declarations only, and its contract test), not in `internal/compute/api.go`. Reason: that file is 835 lines on main under the ratchet ceiling of 840 (`docs/design/ratchet.json`, owner-only; an agent never raises it), so these declarations (about 100 lines) cannot be added to it. `trackapi` imports `internal/compute` for `LedgerPosition` (a sub-package may import its root; the root never imports `trackapi`) and `internal/contracts` for `contracts.Day`; `compute.LedgerPosition` and `compute.OptionLedger` stay where they are. The import rule is the paragraph on declarations-only packages of `docs/design/modules.md` (main's text); this design adds `trackapi` to the entry cell of the compute row and to the two lists that paragraph and the `depends` paragraph name, and issue 2 adds it to `declarationsOnly` in `scripts/arch/main.go`. `docs/design/settings-form.md` makes the same move with `internal/compute/settingsapi`, and `docs/design/builders-wave.md` (open) with `internal/compute/blockapi`; the three share the compute entry cell and the lists, and the later pull request rebases that text.

Seams with the two parallel designs. `option-positions` (structures, close cost, roll credit, max loss, P and L, the `option_result` page) owns every aggregate and every closed-trade result; this design owns the list of what is open now and its risk state, and shows no sum. They share two things and nothing else: `compute.LedgerPosition` (this design adds no field to it; it reads `Right`, `Expiry`, `Strike`, `OpenedOn` and `ClosedOn`, which `docs/design/option-positions.md` entry `### 2 option-positions-ledger-terms` fills, and the entries that read them wait for it) and the reader `store.OptionLedgerReader`. `option-notices` (morning report, option notices) owns what is sent; this design owns the rule `trackapi.OptionTracker` and the page; it adds no job, no notify call and no push text. `option-positions` is on main as a design; if its field names change, the rule (3) is rebased, not redesigned.

Named risk: the join from a position to its quote. It is made on terms, never on `LedgerPosition.Contract` (a conid or a text key): underlying symbol to underlying id by `InstrumentNames`, then expiry, strike and right by `OptionTerms` (issue 5, `TestQuoteMatchedByTerms`). A position that finds no row is `no_mark`, shown, and the page `stale` label says so; a wrong join cannot show a wrong number, only a gap.

- Schema `contracts/schemas/page.option_tracking_page.v1.json`, `schema` const `option_tracking_page`, `schema_version` const 1, `additionalProperties` false, under 600 lines, `$ref`s to `common.v2.json` (`Gap`, `SourceTime`, `StaleLabel`, `RunStatus`, `Timestamp`) as `page.market_page.v1.json` does. Envelope as `market_page`: `run_id, build_id, scope, generated_at, data_times, run_status, stale (StaleLabel or null), title, notes (strings), gaps (Gap list)`. Page fields, all required: `as_of` (date text), `mark_note` (string 1..200: "中间价,收盘 bid/ask 的中点;不是成交价"), `accounts` (1 to 4 `Account`).
  - `Account`: `alias` (`^[a-z0-9_-]{1,16}$`), `title`, `mark_day` (date or null), `guard_day` (date or null), `long_legs_left_out` (integer), `open` (`Table` or null), `recent` (`Table` or null), `recon` (`Table` or null), `gap` (`Gap` or null); `gap` is non-null exactly when `open`, `recent` and `recon` are all null (schema `oneOf`).
  - `Table`, `Row`, `Cell` as in `page.option_candidates_page.v1` (`Column {id, label, align, hint}`, `Row {id, cells}`, `Cell {text, value, badges, tone}` or a gap cell `{kind: gap, reason, text}`), written out again in this file (no cross-file `$ref` between page schemas) so a change of the candidates page cannot break this one; the web data-table takes both. Row ids match `^pos-[A-Za-z0-9:_-]{1,96}$` (the ledger `position_id`) in `open` and `recent`, `^chk-[A-Za-z0-9:_-]{1,96}$` in `recon`.
  - Columns, as data and not as an enum: `open`: company, contract, qty, dte, moneyness (badge `价内`, `价外`, `平价`, with `otm_percent` as the text), spot, mid, premium, kept, kept_percent, delta, iv, results (next results date, badge `在存续期内`), ex (date and amount, calls only), guard (badge `ok`, `阻止`, `未确认`, `不适用`, with the reason). `recent`: company, contract, qty, status (badge, `提前` when early), closed_day, premium, commissions, realized. `recon`: company, contract, qty, code (badge) and note.
- Generated type `web/src/generated/option_tracking_page.v1.ts` (title `OptionTrackingPage`), committed with the schema.
- compute, new declarations-only package `internal/compute/trackapi`, file `api.go` (`package trackapi`; all new; text only, no float; it imports `internal/compute` for `LedgerPosition` and `internal/contracts` for `contracts.Day`, and no function is declared in it). Not in `internal/compute/api.go` (Decision 14):

  `TrackQuote {Underlying string; Expiry contracts.Day; Strike, Right string; Day contracts.Day; Bid, Ask, UnderlyingClose, Delta, Iv string}` (the newest `option_eod` row of a contract, Delta and Iv empty when absent);
  `TrackEvent {Underlying string; Expiry contracts.Day; Strike, Right string; Kind string; Day contracts.Day; NextEarnings contracts.Day; EarningsState string; NextExDate contracts.Day; ExAmount, GuardStatus, GuardReason string}` (one stored candidate row, `Kind` as stored, `Day` its run day; the guard fields are set only on a `held_call_guard` row);

  `TrackInput {Alias string; AsOf contracts.Day; SessionDays []contracts.Day; Positions []compute.LedgerPosition; Quotes []TrackQuote; Events []TrackEvent; Held []TrackHeld; HeldKnown bool}` (`HeldKnown` false means no account capture: the check is skipped, not empty);
  `TrackParams {MarkMaxAgeSessions, RecentDays int}`;
  `TrackedOption {PositionID, Underlying, Right, Strike, Expiry, Quantity, Multiplier, Currency, Status string; Early bool; DTE int; Moneyness, OtmPercent, Spot, Mid string; MarkDay contracts.Day; MarkStatus string; Extrinsic, PremiumIncome, Commissions, Kept, KeptPercent, RealizedPnL, Delta, Iv string; NextEarnings, NextExDate contracts.Day; ExAmount string; EarningsInLife, ExInLife bool; EventStatus string; Guard, GuardReason, GuardBasis string; OpenedOn, ClosedOn contracts.Day}` (an empty string is "no value": the builder turns it into a gap cell with the reason of `MarkStatus`, `EventStatus` or `Guard`);
  `TrackRecon {Underlying, Right, Strike, Quantity string; Expiry contracts.Day; Code string}` (`not_in_flex` or `not_in_account`);
  `TrackResult {Open, Recent []TrackedOption; Recon []TrackRecon; LongLegsLeftOut int}`;
  `type OptionTracker interface { Track(TrackInput, TrackParams) TrackResult }` and `type NewOptionTrackerFunc func() OptionTracker`.
