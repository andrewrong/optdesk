# statement account: an account with no gateway appears on the positions and household pages from the daily Flex statement

Author: the optdesk designer (draft)
Owner approved: (pending)
Date: 2026-11-13
Revision: 2

Source: owner 2026-11-13 (ask 3843): one family account at IBKR has no gateway; its trades, positions and value come only from the daily Flex statement; it should appear on the positions and household pages like the main and second accounts, a day old and saying so; the owner agreed to classify accounts by source. Advisor proposal (binding unless code proves it wrong): store the statement as a capture in the existing `account.*` tables, one capture per statement day with id `<alias>:<reportDate>:flex`, written by a new small job; the source is declared in `accounts.toml`; the capture is filed under the report date; the page says "from the broker statement of <date>"; no new table and no source column.

Goal: an alias declared statement-sourced gets, every day after the Flex statement is stored, one capture of positions and of net liquidation and cash, plus the statement's mark price of each position, so that `positions_page` and `household_page` show it with no change of the readers. Both pages say that the data is from the statement of a named day. The gateway job does not touch the alias and logs no failure for it, and no other job that walks the book treats it as a gateway account.

Existing design: `account-identity.md` (the book, `accounts.toml`), `account-data.md` (the capture tables, `account_capture`), `position-marks.md` (the mark table), `option-ledger.md` (the stored Flex sections), `account-flex-fx.md` (EquitySummary and the conversion rate are stored). `internal/jobs/cutover/accountimport.go` is the precedent of a second source that writes the same tables.

## Decisions

1. Source is declared. `accounts.toml` gets one optional line `statement = ["<alias>", ...]` (an array of strings, each an alias of the file, not repeated); a file without it loads as before and every alias is gateway-sourced. `statement` joins `main` as a reserved key. The main alias cannot be statement-sourced (a refusal naming the line). `internal/accounts` gets a second interface, `Sourced` with `Statement() []string` (the aliases in the order written), beside `Book`; `Book` itself is not changed, so `demoBook`, `fixedBook` and `fakeBook` still compile; the toml book implements both, and a caller asks `b.(accounts.Sourced)`; a book that is not one has no statement alias. (The proposal said the book gains one method; a method on `Book` would force edits to `cmd/ot/accounts.go` and `accounts_test.go`.) `docs/contracts/accounts.md` has no version history; it is edited in place (its responsibility line, its format section, its line 22 that now forbids "tables, dotted keys and non-string values" and must make the one exception, and its errors section).
2. Where. The existing tables, no migration. One capture per statement day: id `<alias>:<YYYY-MM-DD of reportDate>:flex`, `SessionDay` the report date, `Status` `ok`, `At` the newest `RecordedAt` of the rows it was made from. `WriteCapture` writes nothing for an id that exists, so a statement that is revised after its day was captured is not applied (Left out). The capture holds `Positions` and `Values` (a statement collects both).
3. Guard. The job writes only for aliases declared statement-sourced, because readers take every row of (alias, day) and two captures of a day would be mixed. The aliases are the job's own input (`Config.Aliases`). The gateway job is kept away from those aliases by the wiring (Decision 8), not by a change of `accountcapture`.
4. Mapping. The job is a new package `internal/jobs/statementcapture`. It reads `OpenPosition`, `EquitySummaryByReportDateInBase` and `ChangeInNAV` (for the base currency) through `flexsection.Reader`. For one report date `D` it makes a capture when both sections are `present` in the newest statuses, the EquitySummary has a row of `D` and the OpenPosition has at least one row of `D` (an account that holds nothing writes no capture; Doubt 3).
   - Dates. `reportDate` and `expiry` are read in either form, `YYYYMMDD` or `YYYY-MM-DD`, and both are written as `YYYY-MM-DD`; any other text refuses that day.
   - Levels. Per conid, the `SUMMARY` row is used alone when it exists and its `LOT` rows are ignored; a conid with only `LOT` rows is combined: `quantity` is the sum of `position`, the unit cost is the average of `costBasisPrice` weighted by `position`, rounded to 6 places (the one division of the job; a sum of zero skips the conid, counted); the other attributes come from the first lot row in `RowKey` order, and lot rows of one conid that differ in `currency` or `markPrice` refuse the day. A row of any other level is skipped and counted. A statement of lot-level detail therefore gives the same capture as one of summary level.
   - Categories. `assetCategory` `STK` gives sec_type `stock`, `OPT` gives `option`, any other category is skipped and counted in a log line (as the gateway job does).
   - Fields. `symbol`: for a stock the attribute `symbol`; for an option the attribute `underlyingSymbol`, not `symbol`, because the readers take `Position.Symbol` as the underlying and `symbol` of an option is the whole contract text (an option row without `underlyingSymbol` refuses the day). `conid`, `currency`, `multiplier` as stored (stock: 1), `quantity` = `position` (signed text), `strike` as stored, `putCall` `P` or `C` as `put` or `call`, exchange empty. `avg_cost` = unit cost (`costBasisPrice`) for a stock and unit cost times `multiplier` for an option (the broker's per-contract cost, which the positions page divides back, `positions_page/rows.go:126` `perShareCost`); exact decimal arithmetic, no float.
   - Values. Tag `NetLiquidation` = EquitySummary `total`, tag `TotalCashValue` = `cash`, both in the base currency, which is the one `currency` of the `ChangeInNAV` rows (none, or two different, refuses the day).
   - A required attribute that is missing or not a decimal refuses that day: nothing is written for it and the job result carries a Permanent error text naming the day and attribute, never a value.
5. Report dates. The pages read XNYS session days only, so the job writes a day only when `clock.Calendar` says `D` is an XNYS session day; a report date that is not (a Saturday statement, a holiday) is skipped and counted, not failed, and its data is the data of the session day before it. The job considers every stored report date up to the run day and not yet captured, newest first; a first run therefore also fills the stored history, one capture per day, all insert-only; the month-start capture and the two mark days that `household_page` needs for the month change and the movers exist as far as earlier statements are stored, and a page without them shows its gaps, not a failure.
6. Marks. For each position written, one mark row of the same capture: `Attempt` 1, `Basis` `close`, `Price` and `Close` = `markPrice`, `Currency` the position's, `Status` `ok`, `MarkedAt` = `At`, `SessionDay` `D`. They are written with `mark.Writer.WriteMarks` after the capture; a retry repeats the same keys, which are left as they are. `position_marks` does not run for her (Decision 8), so no live mark races with the statement mark and the statement mark is the one stored.
7. Staleness. The capture is filed under the report date, so the existing age mechanism shows the day (`data_times`, `stale` with "Account data day <day>", `stale_input` after 3 sessions). Each page adds one note when its alias is statement-sourced and a capture was found: `From the broker statement of <data day>; this account has no live gateway.` (English, under 200 characters, appended to `notes`; the schemas are unchanged, no version). The gap pages (`no_data`, `stale_input`) get no such note. The proposal's note on a household total for accounts with different data days has nothing to attach to: `household_page` is one scope per account and has no total across accounts (Doubt 1).
8. Statement aliases are filtered out in `cmd/ot`, at the places that take the book as a list of gateway accounts; `internal/jobs/accountcapture` is not changed. One file, `cmd/ot/statementalias.go`, holds `statementAliases(book accounts.Book) []string` (nil for a book that is not `accounts.Sourced`) and `withoutStatement(book accounts.Book, aliases []string) []string` (the list in its order without the statement aliases). The places: `accountCapture` in `cmd/ot/account.go` deletes the statement aliases from the map `readers` before `accountcapture.New` (the sockets are still dialled; her alias gets no reader, so no row, no failed row and no "degraded" line, and `Plan`'s hash covers the aliases left); `markSettings` in `cmd/ot/positionmarks.go` returns `withoutStatement(book, bookAliasList(book))`; `candidateAliases` in `cmd/ot/candidates.go` sets `f.candAliases` to `withoutStatement(f.book, f.bookAliases())` (a nil `f.book` stays nil); `noticeAliases` in `cmd/ot/notices_site.go` returns `withoutStatement(book, bookAliasList(book))` (main is never a statement alias). `siteAliasList` and the page configs keep her: that is how she reaches the two pages. The held-name lists keep her (Decision 12).
9. Schedule and order. `statement_capture` is an any-day job (job console row, no force: an existing capture is never replaced), manifest `[schedule]` XNYS `session_close` offset `95m`, `run_bound` `10m`, after option_ledger's 60 minutes and its 30 minute bound; registered with `order: 55`, so in a run it follows `option_ledger` (50) and precedes `quote_snapshot` (60). It is wired only when the book has a statement alias and the three stores open; otherwise the family logs "job left out" with the missing line and builds nothing.
10. Roles. Reads: the option-ledger store opened with `OT_PG_DSN_OPTION_LEDGER` (`store.DSNEnvOptionLedger`, `internal/store/api.go:243`; role `ot_w_optionledger` holds SELECT on `rd.flex_section_row_r1` and `rd.flex_section_status_r1`), opened the way `sectionsStore` of `cmd/ot/optionledger.go` does through `openFlexSections`. Writes: `ot_w_account` (INSERT, SELECT on the account tables, `store.DSNEnvAccount`) for the capture and `ot_w_posmark` (INSERT, SELECT, `mark.DSNEnvPositionMark`) for the marks. No grant, no role and no migration changes.
11. Contracts unchanged: no file under `contracts/schemas`, `contracts/datasets` or `contracts/proto` changes. `docs/contracts/accounts.md` gets the `statement` line and `Sourced` (Decision 1).
12. Held names. Her held stocks reach `optioneod` and `optionopen` through the existing book-wide held-name readers, with the rules they have today (US jobs take USD stocks only); nothing is added or excluded for her (Doubt 4). Her option rows carry the underlying as `Position.Symbol` (Decision 4), so `optioneod`'s held-call match by `p.Symbol` and the ledger premium match see the underlying, as for a gateway account.

## Doubts

1. The advisor proposed a note on the household total when accounts have different data days; `household_page` is per alias and has no total. The note is built on the per-alias page only. If the owner means the household figure on the home page (`home_household`, main alias only today), that is another design.
2. `strike`, `putCall`, `cash`, `underlyingSymbol` of an `OpenPosition` row and the values of `levelOfDetail` are not verified in the repo, and the owner check listed the attributes of `OpenPosition` only up to `open...`. A wrong name refuses the day and writes nothing, so the account would show `no_data`. The first deploy check is a read of one stored row of each section for the alias.
3. A statement day with an `EquitySummary` row and no `OpenPosition` row (the account holds nothing) writes no capture, so the page shows `no_data` and not an empty account (an older capture shows for up to 3 sessions, then `stale_input`). A flat account is unlikely for this one; the fix would be to treat `present` with `row_count` 0 as empty positions.
4. Her held stocks now reach the held-name lists of `optioneod` and `optionopen` (book-wide). The rules are those of today; whether the owner wants her names in those lists is not asked.
5. The base currency comes from `ChangeInNAV`, because `EquitySummaryByReportDateInBase` carries none in the testdata; if the real query does not store `ChangeInNAV` for her the day is refused. The alternative, the currency of the positions with `fxRateToBase` 1, is not verified either. Positions keep their own currency with marks in the same currency, as the gateway path does; the household figures convert only JPY, so another base gives gaps.
6. The offset `95m` is the designer's choice (after option_ledger's 60m and its 30m bound); under the single-runner design (#1936) the run order is the family order 55, and the offset only matters for the scheduler's own slot.
7. Whether an `OpenPosition` row of a window that ends on a Sunday carries the Friday as `reportDate` is not verified; a report date that is not a session day is skipped, so the worst case is the missing day, not a wrong one.
