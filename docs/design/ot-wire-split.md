# ot wiring split: registering a job or a page in ot edits files of its own

Author: the optdesk designer (draft)
Owner approved: (pending)
Date: 2026-11-10
Revision: 1

Source: the queue ran six issues one at a time because each edits `cmd/ot/wire.go`, `cmd/ot/wire_test.go` and `docs/contracts/ot.md`: #1084 (bars_refresh and fx_refresh), #1164 (dividend_capture, landed), #1175 (POST /owner-settings), #1188 (position_marks), #1195 (report blocks, landed), #1203 (session-day reader of the tracking page). `AGENTS.md` (modules, size, tests), owner 2026-11-08 ("own glue code sits behind an interface so a library can replace it").

Read on origin/main at f001a47: `cmd/ot/wire.go` (432 lines), `wire_test.go` (595), `runonce.go` (731), `site.go` (451), `pagelist.go`, `depsext.go`, `optionledger.go`, `candidates.go`, `account.go`, `preflight.go`, `scheduler.go`, `docs/contracts/ot.md` (218 lines, paragraphs of 1500 to 2500 characters), `docs/design/modules.md` (row ot: `cmd/** docs/contracts/ot.md docs/contracts/ot/**`), `docs/design/ratchet.json`, the `Files:` lines of the six issues.

On main: subcommands register in an `init()` through `register(name, fn)`; `depsext.go` gives a command its deps through `extDeps[name]` and `depsOf[T]`, so a new command adds no field to `deps`; `optionledger.go`, `account.go` and `candidates.go` already keep one job family each in a file of its own. Not yet split: the list of jobs (`wireJobs` in `runonce.go`, a fixed sequence of `if job != nil { append }`), the fields of `jobFactory`, `runOnceConfig` and `wiring` that name single jobs (`earn`, `earningsOff`, `cand`, `candidatesOff`), the fields of `deps` and their lines in `newBaseDeps`, the page routes of `extraPageBuilder` (`site.go`) and the page names of `pageNamed` (`pagelist.go`), and every test and every contract paragraph.

## Goal

Adding a job, or a page route, to `ot` is a new file of its own plus tests of its own plus one document of its own, so that issues that register different jobs or pages edit disjoint paths and run in parallel; the behaviour of every command is the same before and after.

## Decisions

1. The collision is three shared edit points, not one: the sequence in `wireJobs` with the single-job fields of `wiring`, `jobFactory` and `runOnceConfig`; the fields of `deps`; the shared test and contract files. The split removes all three, or a queued issue still collides on whichever is left.
2. Job registry, the same pattern as `register` and `extDeps`. A new file `cmd/ot/jobreg.go` holds `type jobFamily struct { id string; order int; startOff func(cfg runOnceConfig, brokerUp bool) string; build func(ctx context.Context, a *app, jc *jobCtx) (contracts.Job, string, error) }`, `registerJob(f jobFamily)` and `sortedJobFamilies()`. `startOff` is the reason the family is disabled before any store is open (what `startDisabled` in `preflight.go` hard-codes now for `fred_series`, `earnings_refresh`, `option_eod`, `option_eod_jp`, `quote_snapshot`); nil means never. `jobCtx` carries what the families share and nothing named after one family: `cfg runOnceConfig`, a `*jobFactory` (families that set its fields, as `candidateJob` and `dividendJob` do, see them in the loop; `wiring.factory` is copied once after the loop), the dialled broker and `brokerUp`, the calendar, the `closerSet`. A family file calls `registerJob` in an `init()`; a second family with the same `id` or the same `order` panics at start (same rule as `register`), and `TestJobOrderUnique` runs the init. `wireJobs` becomes one loop over `sortedJobFamilies()`.
3. Order is the number each family states, in steps of ten, and it reproduces today's sequence exactly: `fred_series` 10, `option_eod` 20, `option_eod_jp` 30, `dividend_capture` 40, `option_ledger` 50, `quote_snapshot` 60, `compute_figures` 70, `calendar_refresh` 80, `earnings_refresh` 90, `account_capture` 100, `option_candidates` 110. Why a number and not append order: Go runs the `init()` of the files of a package in the order of the file names, so an append order would change when a file is renamed; `option_ledger` must still follow `option_eod`. Numbers are reserved by this design so that parallel issues cannot pick the same one: `bars_refresh` 120, `fx_refresh` 130, `position_marks` 140, and 150 to 190 for the next five jobs a design names (the design that adds a job names its number from that range and the next free one after it; two designs never share one). A clash still fails `go test` at once, in the branch that adds the second family.
4. What `build` returns: `(job, "", nil)` the job is wired; `(nil, "", nil)` the family is absent (the family logs its own `job left out` line before, as `dividendJob` does now: the line and its text stay); `(job, reason, nil)` the job is held in `wiring.jobs` and dropped from the `run-once` and `backfill` list with one `job left out` line carrying the reason, while the scheduler keeps it (this is what `earnings_refresh` and `option_candidates` do now with `earningsOff` and `candidatesOff`). `wiring` replaces `earn`, `earningsOff`, `cand` and `candidatesOff` by `off map[string]string` (job id to reason) and `held map[string]contracts.Job`; `runnable`, `candidateProbe` and `schedulerDisabled` read those. `startDisabled` of `preflight.go` becomes a loop over the registry calling each `startOff` (same ids, same reasons); `earningsOff()` and `candidatesOff()` of `runOnceConfig` stay as the `startOff` of their families so that there is one source of each reason. The probe (`prober.probe`) does not become a registry loop in this cut: it needs a freshly dialled broker (`p.broker(ctx)`, not the broker of `jobCtx`, which can be the stand-in) and the XTKS check that `swapReconciler.Probe` compares with `reasonNoXTKS` (`TestJPProbeKeepsJobOutWhenXTKSRunsOut`), and `build` has no return for an absent job with a reason. Its `switch` keeps the five ids it has (`fred_series`, `earnings_refresh`, `option_eod`, `quote_snapshot`, `option_eod_jp`; the earnings line reads `w.off["earnings_refresh"]`) and its text; its `default` returns `(nil, w.off[id])` for an id of a registered family without a probe case, so a new family stays left out until the process restarts (the rule of the earnings lines), and `"unknown job"` for any other id. A probe hook in `jobFamily` is added later by the issue that needs it (an edit of `jobreg.go`, owned by this design). `printOutcomes` keeps its signature and `runonce.go` (`reconcileDay`) passes `w.off["earnings_refresh"]`; `backfill.go` does not call it: the output names the same one left-out job as today, and no other job is printed as left out. The log lines, the statuses and the `printOutcomes` output stay byte for byte.
5. Lines of `.env` and keys: `runOnceConfig` keeps its fields as they are (it gains none, `env` included); a new family reads its own line through `a.envFile()` (as `optionLedgerJob` does; the values are never printed or logged) and states its own `reason...` constants and its own line name in its file. `env.go`, `loadConfig` and `runOnceConfig` are not edited for a new job. Failing for a missing line stays the existing rule; a new job is left out, never failed, without its line (as `option_ledger`).
6. Stores and constructors of a new family: its `build` opens its own stores with `jc.extra.addCloser` (the pattern of `optionLedgerJob`), and its constructors come from `extDeps[<family>]` and `depsOf[T]` (the pattern of `optionLedgerDeps`). A new family adds no field to `deps`, no field to `jobFactory` and no line to `newBaseDeps`. The existing fields stay where they are in the refactor (moving them is a later step, Left out). Own implementation, because `extDeps` is the glue the owner asked to sit behind an interface and no dependency-injection library fits; `registerJob` is a map and a sort.
7. Where the code of a family lives: the seven families still in `runonce.go` move to `cmd/ot/wire_<family>.go` (`wire_fred.go`, `wire_optioneod.go` with `option_eod_jp`, `wire_dividend.go`, `wire_quotesnapshot.go`, `wire_computefigures.go`, `wire_calendar.go`, `wire_earnings.go`); `optionledger.go`, `account.go` and `candidates.go` keep their names and gain the `registerJob` call. A new family is `cmd/ot/wire_<family>.go`, or the file its design already names (`positionmarks.go`). One family, one file under 600 lines, one `init()`.
8. Page registry, the same shape. A new file `cmd/ot/pagereg.go` holds `type pageFamily struct { name, route string; build func(ctx context.Context, a *app, clk clock.Clock, dayFlag string, rd siteReaders) ([]byte, error); named func(ctx context.Context, a *app) (pages.Page, func(), error); order int }` and `registerPage`; a clash of a non-empty `name`, of `route` or of a non-zero `order` panics. `extraPageBuilder` (`site.go`) and `pageNamed` (`pagelist.go`) read the registry for the four routes and names they switch on today (`options/candidates`, `options/tracking`, `options/notices`, `settings`; `option_candidates_page`, `option_tracking_page`, `option_notices_page`); `candidates_site.go`, `tracking_site.go`, `notices_site.go` and `settings.go` each register theirs in their own `init()`. The "known pages" text keeps its order and is the same text: the list of `deps.pageList`, `macro_page`, `report_page`, then the registered names with a non-empty `name`, sorted by `order` (`option_candidates_page` 10, `option_notices_page` 20, `option_tracking_page` 30, the order of today's text; the number is explicit because `init()` order follows file names). `settings.go` registers the route `settings` with an empty `name`, nil `named` and order 0: `pageNamed` and the known-pages text skip a family with an empty name, so `ot page --name settings_page` stays unknown and `settings_page` stays out of the list (Decision 13); the document `settings_page.md` documents the route only. Market, positions (many routes) and report (they read the query string; `page.go` switches on `reportName` and `marketName`) are not table-driven by this design (Left out): `pageFamily.build` has no query parameter and one `route`.
9. Readers a page family needs beyond the shared ones (`series`, `instruments`): `siteReaders` gains `ext map[string]any` filled by `siteOpeners[key]` (a registry of `func(ctx, *app, track func(io.Closer, error) bool) (any, bool)`, the shape of `openOwnerReader`, so it shares the closers and the pages DSN handling of `openSiteReaders`) and read through `siteReaderOf[T](rd, key)`, which answers the zero value and false when `ext` is nil (the demo profile returns early with no readers and runs no opener). A page family adds an opener in its own file. The existing fields of `siteReaders` stay in the refactor.
10. Tests, one file per family: a family's tests go to `cmd/ot/wire_<family>_test.go` (`<family>_test.go` for the three existing families), all with `t.Parallel()`, `t.TempDir()`, a fake clock and no sleep, using the shared helpers `testApp` (`main_test.go`), `fullEnv` (`runonce_test.go`) and `rig` with `newRig` (`runonce_option_test.go`), which a new family does not edit; a family whose job needs a line that `fullEnv` lacks leaves the job out in `TestRunOnceWiresSixJobs`, so that test keeps its exact six ids. The tests of `wire_test.go` move to the file of their family; `wire_test.go` ends with the shared run-once tests and helpers listed in issue 3 (`TestNewDepsFilled`, `TestRunOnceWiresSixJobs`, `TestRunOnceNeedsLoaderDSN`, `TestRunOnceRefusesBadWatchList`, `TestDemoProfileOpensNoStore`, `TestSetupLineEqualsOwnersetConst`, `TestEarningsRefreshRegistered`, `jobIDs`, `errorString`) and never grows again; the registry tests are in `jobreg_test.go` and `pagereg_test.go`. `TestJobSetUnchanged` holds the eleven ids of Decision 3 and checks that they are present in that order, with other ids allowed between or after them; a later family is not added to that list (it would be a shared edit) but states its own order number in its own test, and `TestJobDocsExist` (Decision 11) checks that every registered id has its document.
11. Contract documents, one per job and one per page: the long paragraphs of `docs/contracts/ot.md` that describe a single job or page (the `run-once` paragraph, the route lines, the `--name` lines) move to `docs/contracts/ot/jobs/<id>.md` and `docs/contracts/ot/pages/<name>.md`, the paths the row ot already owns. Each holds: the order number, the window, the lines it reads, the reasons it is left out, and a `## Version` section. `docs/contracts/ot.md` keeps the rules common to all jobs and a table of two columns (job or page, document); it is edited by the refactor only. A later issue adds its document and never edits `ot.md`: the `v<N>` list of the `## Version history` of `ot.md` is linear and every issue appended to it, so it is frozen at v20 and later versions are written in the document of the family. `TestJobDocsExist` reads `docs/contracts/ot/jobs/<id>.md` for each registered id through `os.DirFS` of the repository root found from the test file (read only, no write).
12. Size: `runonce.go` (731 lines) and `scheduler_test.go` (784) are below 800 and not in `docs/design/ratchet.json`; the refactor shrinks `runonce.go` and no later issue grows either; a family test never goes to `scheduler_test.go` or `runonce_test.go`. A file that would reach 800 is reported, not split, as in `AGENTS.md`.
13. No behaviour change: the same jobs, in the same order, with the same ids, log lines, statuses, off reasons and the same `run-once` output; the same pages, routes and error text. No contract file under `contracts/` changes: `Contracts: unchanged`.

## Modules

One module, `ot` (`cmd/**`, `docs/contracts/ot.md`, `docs/contracts/ot/**`), no new package, no new import: the imports of `cmd/ot` stay the list of the row ot in `docs/design/modules.md` and `docs/design/modules.md` is not edited. The refactor moves code between files of the same package `main`.

## Interface

None. `ot` is the composition root and one package; `jobFamily`, `pageFamily`, `jobCtx` and the registries are unexported and are tested in `cmd/ot`. A change of these types is an edit of `jobreg.go` or `pagereg.go`, owned by the issues of this design only.

## Issues

Every test calls `t.Parallel()`, writes files only under a temporary directory, uses a fake clock, never sleeps, opens no port and touches no live broker or database. The names of the tests this design adds are under 32 characters (moved tests keep theirs). Acceptance is checked offline by reading, grep and git; test runs are under "Checked by CI".

## Adversarial

Answers to the read-only attack, kept in the dispatcher scratchpad (ot-wire-split.adversarial.md, not in the repository), items 1 to 6.

1. Gap vs goal: adopted. `jobFamily` gains `startOff` and `startDisabled` of `preflight.go` becomes a loop over the registry (Decision 4), so #1084 needs no `preflight.go` (its jobs stay left out until restart through the probe `default`, Decision 4); `printOutcomes` keeps one string, passed by `runonce.go` as `w.off["earnings_refresh"]`, output unchanged; the page registry covers the four routes of `extraPageBuilder` only, market, positions and report stay (Decision 8, Left out); `TestJobSetUnchanged` checks the eleven ids as an ordered subsequence, so later jobs do not edit it (Decision 10). The `PriceBars` part of #1084 and the history broker are named as not fitting and cut out of it.
2. Unnamed risk: adopted. Order numbers are reserved by this design (Decision 3) so parallel issues cannot choose the same one; `jobFactory` is shared by pointer and `wiring.factory` is copied once after the loop (Decision 2).
3. Simpler way: adopted in part. `runOnceConfig.env` is dropped, families read `a.envFile()` (Decision 5); the page part shrinks to the table-driven routes and is its own issue 2; the rest is kept because a plain slice in one file is the same collision.
