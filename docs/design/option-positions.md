Goal: the owner opens `/options/structures` of `ot serve` and sees, for one account, the option structures he holds (short put, covered call, put and call spread, long option) in the order of their risk, each with moneyness, distance to the strike, premium earned so far, maximum loss and gain, the profit now, and for a single short leg the cost to close and the net of rolling to the next monthly; one click opens the structure page (legs at mid, expiry payoff with break-even, IBKR Greeks, key levels, fills, timeline). At `/options/result` he sees what his closed sold options returned, by month and by way of ending (closed, expired, assigned, exercised), with the premium kept share, and a reliability block that says what was not counted. Every figure is an exact decimal computed in `internal/compute`, from the stored Flex rows replayed by `compute.OptionLedger`, from stored `obs.option_eod` quotes and from the stored account capture; where an input is missing the cell is a named gap, never a zero.

## What main holds today

A `LedgerPosition` carries `Contract` and `Underlying` but not the right, the strike, the expiry, nor the open and close day: a structure cannot be recognised from it, and a result by month cannot be cut.

## Decisions

1. Three pages on the carried schemas, none changed. The builders emit `structures_list` v1, `structure_detail` v3 and `option_result_page` v1 exactly as the web views already read them; no schema, no generated type and no web view is edited (the contract issue of a candidates-style page is not needed). A field a schema marks as nullable stays null when this design has no value for it (`ex_dividend`, `assignment`, `protection`), and `notes` says so in one line. `positions_page` (board 6) stays as it is (owner 2026-11-08) and `ledger_page` (board 20) is Left out (owner 2026-11-09, answer A: its own design later).
2. Source of positions: the Flex replay only. Open structures are built from `LedgerPosition` rows with status `open`; assignment, exercise and expiry are the statuses the replay got from Flex (owner decision). The account capture is never a source of a figure. It is compared: `compute.OptionStructures` takes the held option rows of the newest capture and returns `CaptureDiff` (contracts held by the capture and not open in the ledger, and the reverse); the structures page shows the count and the contracts as a note ("尚未进入 Flex 的持仓: ..."), never in a total. A capture older than 3 session days gives no diff and a note.
3. The ledger position gets its terms (interface change, issue 1, filled by issue 2): `Right` (`P` or `C`), `Expiry` (`YYYY-MM-DD`), `Strike` (decimal text), `OpenedOn` and `ClosedOn` (days; `ClosedOn` empty while open), `OpenPrice` (the Flex `tradePrice` of the opening row, per share, text) and `Fills []LedgerFill{Day, Action, Quantity, Price, Commission}` (one per row of the piece, text), `BrokerRealized` (the sum of the Flex `fifoPnlRealized` of its closing rows as text, empty when a row has none). The events already hold the right, expiry and strike; the opening price and the rows are kept from the same Flex attributes (issue 2 adds them to the piece; the current rule keeps `price` for stock rows only), so no page parses raw Flex attributes. No other field changes, so no caller of `Replay` breaks.
4. Recognition is a pure rule, one place: `compute.OptionStructures.Evaluate` in `internal/compute/optionstruct`, per alias, no I/O, no clock, `math/big.Rat`, decimal text at every boundary, no float (the rule of `option-ledger.md` Decision 11). Order: (a) a sold and a bought piece of the same underlying, right and expiry whose open quantities match pair (the unit is the piece a FIFO close leaves, `position_id` `<alias>:<openTx>:<n>`; a piece closed in part pairs by its remaining quantity, and a pair that no longer matches is `other` until it matches again, never forced) into `put_spread` (sold strike above bought strike), `put_debit_spread` is named `other`, `call_spread` (sold strike below bought); (b) a sold call with shares in the alias cycle of the underlying (`LedgerCycle.Shares` at least 100 per contract, first in first out among the calls) is `covered_call`, else `naked_call`; (c) the rest are `short_put`, `long_put`, `long_call`. Anything else (diagonal, calendar, straddle, collar, three or more legs) is `other`: its legs are listed, it has no max loss and no total in any sum, and the page says "未识别的结构, 逐腿显示". A position is never split. This is a port of the idea of option-trader `flex/structures.py` (which recognises more kinds and links chains), cut to what the owner trades: sold puts, covered calls, vertical spreads.
5. Structure identity. `structure_id` is `<alias>:<position_id of the leg with the lowest strike, ties by id>` (the position id of a whole lot is stable, `option-ledger.md` Decision 12; the `n` of a part closed in steps is assigned in close order, so the id of a part can change when a later close is stored and the page says so by the gap page of this Decision); the detail route is `/options/structures/<structure_id>` path-escaped, and a list row's `href` is that path. An id of a structure that is no longer open is a gap page "结构已不在持仓中", not a 404.
6. The figures, one function each, the ids of the register: `structure.max_loss` and `max_gain` (the corners of the expiry payoff per `register.toml`; short put: strike times multiplier times contracts minus the premium; spread: width minus the net credit; covered call has no max loss number and max gain is strike minus basis plus premium, with the basis being the strike or purchase price of the cycle and the premium shown apart, never deducted from the basis; naked call: unbounded, a gap `no_rule` with text "无上限"), break-even, `structure.unrealized_pnl` and `structure.leg_pnl` (mark minus opening price, signed, times multiplier), `structure.earned_share` (premium kept now divided by the opening net premium, from the mid), `structure.expiry_distance` and `level_distance` (percent of the underlying close, text moved two places, no rounding of the stored digits), `structure.close_cost` (the mid of the short leg times multiplier times contracts; the owner's rule "mark at mid" replaces the snapshot price of version 1), `structure.roll_credit` (the bid of the same right and strike at the next monthly expiry, a third-Friday date by date arithmetic in the rule, minus the close cost; gap `no_data` when the quote set has no such contract) `Compare.cash_after` (premium income minus commissions minus the close cost, of the single short leg; a gap when the close cost is one), and `structure.recent_commission` (the commissions of the last 30 days of the legs, from the ledger rows). Each is a gap with a named reason when an input is absent: `missing_quote` (no mark), `stale_input` (older than `MarkMaxAgeSessions`), `unsupported_currency` (a JPY structure: no FX is designed, figures are in the contract currency and the schema's `Usd` type is for USD only), `no_rule` (`other`, naked call). Version 2 of these ids is written by issue 11.
7. Marks and Greeks come from stored IBKR rows. `StructureQuote` carries the bid, ask, underlying close, day and the four IBKR model values of one contract, as text, matched to a leg by (underlying, right, strike, expiry), never by an id. The mark is the mid of bid and ask, rounded half-even to 4 places, and a quote with a bid of zero or no ask has no mark. The Greek of a structure is the signed sum of quantity times multiplier times the leg value; `model_text` names the source ("IBKR 模型值, 取自 <day>"). Optdesk prices nothing, solves no IV and bumps no input.
8. The payoff chart data (`Payoff.points`, band, break-even, spot) is the piecewise linear expiry payoff over the corners of the legs plus a margin of 20 percent of the top strike on both sides, built in `optionstruct` (the corners only, so at most 2 plus legs points). The schema carries the break-even and band; drawing a reference line is the web chart layer's open item (`tracking-household-reports.md` Left out) and is not touched.
9. The option result is a second pure rule: `compute.OptionResults.Summarise` in `internal/compute/optionresult` over the closed `LedgerPosition` rows of one alias and currency: per month of `ClosedOn` the realized P&L (premium income minus commissions, the ledger's `RealizedPnL`), the running sum, the endings by `Status` (closed, expired, assigned, exercised) with counts and sums, the premium kept share (realized divided by opening premium of the sold positions, as the sold-side share), the assigned premium and shares apart (the cost basis stays the strike, never reduced by premium), and the reliability counts: positions whose `BrokerRealized` equals the replayed figure, positions that differ, positions with no broker figure, `Unresolved` and `Review` ids (never in a figure). Currencies are never added: one section per currency and side (`sold`, `bought`), keys `sold_usd`, `bought_usd`, `sold_jpy`. A month that is not over has `muted` true. Nothing is stored: the figures are computed at build time, as `option-ledger.md` Decision 2 says.
10. Per account, main first. Every page is for one alias. `ot serve` takes `?account=<alias>` and shows the main alias when the query is absent; an alias not in the list is a gap page "未知账户". The alias list is given by `ot` (the gateways file, `account-data.md`; the first entry is main), each page names its alias in `scope`, and no page, no function and no figure pools two accounts. The second account failing (no capture, no Flex rows) shows its own gap and never affects the main account's page.
11. Privacy and delivery. The pages are served on the local machine as the other account pages are. The builders and the `cmd/ot` route import no `internal/notify` and no Telegram sender (grep-checked in issues 7 to 10): the margin cushion and the assignment cash (a short put's strike times multiplier is the obligation, shown as the max-loss figure on the page only) are on no Telegram path, as ADR 0010 and `option-ledger.md` Decision 14 say. Notices about structures (an exit rule hit, an assignment) are `own design option-tracking` and are not part of this design.
12. Pages compute nothing. A builder reads through the interfaces (`OptionLedgerReader`, `AccountReader`, `OptionEodReader`, `SessionDayReader` and `OptionTermsReader`, which gives the right, strike and expiry of an `option_eod` instrument so a quote is matched by terms), calls `compute.OptionLedger`, `OptionStructures` and `OptionResults`, and writes text: it moves a decimal point two places for percents, copies money text, never rounds, never adds, never sorts a figure (the order is the order `Evaluate` returns: the most negative max loss first, gaps last, then `structure_id`). Its test fails on `float32`, `float64`, `math/big`, `sort` and any import of `internal/notify`, `internal/jobs`, `os` or `database/sql`.
13. The parameters of the compare row (`captured_param`, `exit_dte_param`) are two named constants of `optionstruct`, `DefaultCaptureTarget = "0.50"` and `DefaultExitDTE = 21` (the owner's sold-option exit habit as the boards show it, "已赚 ≥ 50%"), passed through `StructureParams` so a later settings table can replace them (owner 2026-11-09, answer B: constants 50 percent and 21 days; a settings table may replace them later). The compare row is filled only for a structure with exactly one short leg and no long leg; for the others `compare` is null.
14. Frontend stack (ADR 0034): no UI code in this design except the top-bar menu (Decision 15, issue 12); the existing views use shadcn/ui components, the shared data table and recharts, and no dependency is added, so `frontend-whitelist.json` and `web/deps-allowlist.txt` do not change.
15. The top bar gets one new item (owner 2026-11-09, answer C): the label `期权持仓` (the existing item `期权` at `/options` stays unchanged; the bar never shows two items with one label; the bar then has ten items), a menu made with the shadcn `dropdown-menu` over `radix-ui` (ADR 0034: the component `web/src/components/ui/dropdown-menu.tsx` is added in the shadcn way; `radix-ui` 1.8.1 is already in `web/package.json`, `frontend-whitelist.json` and `web/deps-allowlist.txt`, so no dependency changes). It holds the links `结构` (`/options/structures`) and `盈亏` (`/options/result`); the designs `own design option-tracking` and `own design option-notices` add `追踪` and `提示` to it after issue 12 and carry Order lines on it. The menu sits after the item `期权候选` that `option-candidates-page.md` issue 4 adds. Issue 12 is no longer conditional.
