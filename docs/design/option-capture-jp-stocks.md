## What the probe of 2026-11-10 showed

- Seven of the eight names (4063, name A, name B, 6981, 7974, 8766, 9501) have options: exchange `OSE.JPN`, trading class equal to the code, option symbol equal to the code, every expiry regular. 278A has none (stock section only, no exchange, no expiry).
- The seven share the same four expiries on that day: 2026-11-12 (2 days away), 2026-12-10 (30), 2027-03-11, 2027-06-10: two near months, then quarters.
- Strike steps differ per name and per price level: 250, 100, 100 up to 5000 and 250 above, 25 up to 500 and 50 above; for 8766 the step is 16.67, alternating 17 and 16 in the printed values (483, 500, 517, 533, 550...).
- The options are thin. A last price was in several cases only the prior close (the tool's flag `is_close`, no trade that day, no time, volume 0), bid and ask were absent, one-sided (ask only) or placeholder wide (bid 10, ask 800 against a last of 829.5), open interest in the tens or less.
- Not shown by any tool: the multiplier (the ADR says 100), the tick table, the session hours and any night session, the quote after 15:45.

## What main holds today

- `option_eod_jp` is built by `optioneod.NewJP` from `job_jp.toml` (zone `Asia/Tokyo`, window and slot calendar `XTKS`, slot 15:50 JST, `freeze_after` 1550, `stop_after` 1700, `chunk` 50, `budget_minutes` 8, `ledger_dataset = "obs.option_eod_jp"`, `trading_class = "225"`). One `[[underlying]]`, N225 (`IND`, `OSE.JPN`). It is wired in `cmd/ot` (`deps.newOptionEodJP`, `jobFactory.optionEodJP`, `startDisabled`, the prober, the XTKS left-out rule of Decision 13 of the first design).
- `watch.toml` is loaded once into `[]jobs.WatchEntry` (`cmd/ot/watch.go`); `jobFactory.watch` holds it. A JP entry has `market = "JP"`, `symbol`, `sec_type = "STK"`, `exchange = "TSEJ"`, `currency = "JPY"`, `con_id` and the optional `buy_price`. `optioneod.NewWithWatch` keeps only `Market == "US"` and `SecType == "STK"` (`watchUnderlyings`), so a JP entry is ignored today.
- The ot dry run now builds `option_eod` over the watch list through `buildOptionEod` (`cmd/ot/runonce.go`), called by `jobFactory.optionEod` and by `dryRunBuild` (`cmd/ot/dryrun.go`) with the watch list passed to `dryRunJob`; `option_eod_jp` still goes through `d.newOptionEodJP` in both.
- Chain and contract path: `ListChains` is `reqSecDefOptParams` and each row is `(exchange, trading_class, multiplier, expirations, strikes)`; strikes leave pyside as decimal text (`_decimal_text`, `Decimal(repr(float))`), `Chain.Strikes` is `[]string`, `selectContracts` copies the text of the chosen strike to `Contract.Strike`, `rangeStrikes` compares with `big.Rat` on the text, `ResolveContract` turns the text into a float for the TWS contract. Nothing on that path rounds a strike. `osiSymbolWith` (`map.go`) is the only place that rounds: strike x 1000 to 8 digits for the `osi` identifier.
- Chain filter: `chainOfRoot` (`run.go`) takes the first chain whose `Exchange` equals the underlying's exchange (for a stock `TSEJ`, never the `OSE.JPN` of the chain) and whose class is the manifest `trading_class`.
- ADR 0004 amendment 2026-10-05 (lines 176 and 178) holds an observation by the earlier collector: "JP standard series: `OSE.JPN`, trading class equal to the stock code, multiplier 100", and that multiplier enforcement is open. The probe of 2026-11-10 confirms exchange, class and option symbol, not the multiplier.
- Row mapping: `statusOf` (`map.go`) gives OK and PARTIAL status 1 (2 when the quote has no bid, ask, last and implied vol at all), NO_DATA 2, the errors 5; `mapRow` copies bid, ask, sizes, last, volume, open interest and the model values as the quote has them and leaves a missing one nil. `broker.Quote.Close` exists but `mapRow` does not copy it; `obs.option_eod.r1` has no close and no is-close column; its `status` enum has 3 not listed and 4 stale, which the job never writes. Kind `OPTION_EOD` (`spec.py`) wants bid, ask, close, volume, open interest and the model values and releases the line only when bid, ask, close and the model values have all arrived, else waits 20 s and answers PARTIAL (or NO_DATA when nothing wanted arrived); its `last` is not a wanted field.
- The runner calls `Job.Run` with one key at a time, so `budget_minutes` is spent per key and one failing name does not stop the others. A `Permanent` error leaves the key missing and the cursor holds on it (`runner.md` line 22); a `NoValue` error is a final `no_value`.
- `us_hash_test.go` pins the plan hash of the US manifest. `canonical()` prints each underlying with `%+v` and appends the optional settings only when set; `buy` is an unexported per-symbol map filled by the watch constructor.

## Decisions

1. One job, more keys. The JP stocks are keys of `option_eod_jp`, next to N225, not a second job: a second job needs a second id in `startDisabled`, the prober, `swapReconciler.Probe`, `dryRunJobs` and the tests that count jobs. Slot, window, zone, calendar `XTKS`, `freeze_after` and ledger dataset are the same. The key is `{obs.option_eod_jp, <code>}`; the table written stays `obs.option_eod`; no contract changes.
2. A new constructor `optioneod.NewJPWithWatch` of the existing type `jobs.NewOptionEodWatchFunc`; `internal/jobs/api.go` does not change. It builds from `job_jp.toml`, keeps the N225 underlying and adds one underlying per watch entry with `Market == "JP"` and `SecType == "STK"`, sorted by symbol, with the `buy_price` text of the entry. `NewJP` keeps its signature and plan. No translator argument: JP codes have no separator and the identity is what `OSIRoot` gives. An empty JP watch list gives the N225-only job, not an error. A repeated code is an error, as in the US constructor.
3. Underlying of a stock: the watch entry gives `symbol`, `sec_type`, `exchange`, `currency`, `con_id`; a `[stock]` table of `job_jp.toml` gives the rest: `mic = "XTKS"`, `calendar_id = "XTKS"`, `lot_size = "100"` (the TSE trading unit), `price_increment`, the option side `chain_exchange = "OSE.JPN"`, `multiplier = "100"`, `option_mic = "XOSE"`, and the selection values of Decision 6 (`min_dte`, `max_dte`, `max_strikes`, `max_strikes_per_side`). No field is added to the `underlying` struct; the table is a new field of `manifest` read by `parseManifest`, and `canonical()` appends its values only when the manifest holds a stock underlying, so the hash of `NewJP` and of the US manifest do not move.
4. Chain of a stock: the first chain with `Exchange == chain_exchange`, `TradingClass == root(symbol)` (the code; observed on 2026-10-05 and again on 2026-11-10) and `Multiplier == "100"`. The multiplier filter is the enforcement ADR 0004 left open. A multiplier the tools did not confirm is a risk the filter would turn into a silent loss of every name, so the two failures have two texts (Decision 5): no chain of the exchange and class is "no listed options"; a chain of the class with another multiplier is "no chain of multiplier 100". The live test of issue 1 asserts that the 7974 chain has multiplier "100" (open point 1). N225 keeps `trading_class = "225"` and its own exchange; a stock always takes its root as class.
5. Absence is final, not stuck. For a stock key: `ListChains` answering no chain, or none of exchange and class, ends with `NoValue` "no listed options" (278A is this case in the probe of 2026-11-10, so a real name exercises the path); a class chain of another multiplier ends with `NoValue` "no chain of multiplier 100"; a selection with no expiry in the window or no strike ends with `NoValue` "no contract in window" (Decision 6). Each text names the job and symbol only. An error of `ListChains` itself stays `Permanent`. Reason: a `Permanent` key is a failed run that keeps the key missing, and the cursor waits for it until the window close (`expireIfClosed` turns it into `no_value` there, about two hours after the XTKS close); a known absence needs no failed run, no retry and no failure line every day, so it is `NoValue` at once with its text. N225 keeps `Permanent` for a missing chain and an empty selection.
6. Selection for a stock follows the owner's goal (valuation and buy price set the strike) and the US watch job: expiries are the calendar days to expiry `min_dte..max_dte`, strikes come from `rangeStrikes` when the entry has a `buy_price` (0.80 x buy to the larger of 1.05 x buy and the price, at most `max_strikes`), else from the manifest strike band, whose per-side cap the `[stock]` table overrides. The `[stock]` values are `min_dte = 14`, `max_dte = 75`, `max_strikes = 12` (the `buy_price` path) and `max_strikes_per_side = 6` (the band path, `strike_band = 0.10` unchanged), so both paths give at most 12 strikes and every stock key holds at most 12 x 2 rights x 2 expiries = 48 contracts; N225 keeps `max_expiries = 3`, `strike_band = 0.10`, `max_strikes_per_side = 8`. Checked against the chain of the probe of 2026-11-10 (expiries 11-12, 12-10, 2027-03-11, 2027-06-10):
   - Today (DTE 2, 30, 121, 212) the window picks one expiry, 12-10; the 2-day series is skipped on purpose (a series about to expire is not the month the owner sells) and the quarters are too far. A window of 21..45 held the same single expiry today but would pick none on every day when the next monthly is closer than 21 days and the one after is a quarter, so the lower bound is 14 and the upper 75, which also takes a next monthly if the exchange lists one after 11-12 expires (the probe shows none yet; it would be about 62 days away) and never a quarter 121 days out: at most two expiries, at most 12 strikes x 2 rights x 2 expiries = 48 contracts per name (the range 0.80 to 1.05 x buy is about 12 steps at the steps of the probe).
   - When the exchange lists no series between 14 and 75 days (the chain of the probe has a gap of about three months between 12-10 and 2027-03-11), the key is `NoValue` "no contract in window" for those days, a final hole with an honest text, never a stuck cursor. The days of such holes are not known until the first weeks of runs; this is acceptable for the capture and is read from the `no_value` lines (open point 6). The window values are manifest values, changed without code.
   - The DTE rule needs no change in code: `dteExpiries` and `rangeStrikes` exist; the change is that the stock's values are passed instead of the manifest-wide ones (the manifest-wide `min_dte`, `max_dte`, `max_strikes` stay 0 for N225, and its `max_strikes_per_side = 8` is not touched).
7. Strikes are decimal text end to end. The chain gives them as decimal text, `selectContracts` and `rangeStrikes` carry and compare the text and never round, `ResolveContract` gets the text as the float it spells. Non-integer steps (8766, 16.67) therefore reach the gateway as the exchange lists them. The only rounding is the `osi` identifier of `osiSymbolWith` (strike x 1000, 8 digits), which is one-to-one for steps of 0.001 or more; the identifier stays stable day to day as long as the gateway spells a strike the same way, which the live test records for 8766 (open point 3). The refusal at a strike of 100000 yen or more is unchanged and not met by any name of the probe.
8. Thin options are mapped honestly, with the contract as it is. The quote path keeps `Last` of an option nil (`last` is no wanted field of `OPTION_EOD`), copies `bid` and `ask` when they arrived and leaves a missing side nil, so a one-sided book is a row with one side set and a placeholder-wide book is copied as given: nothing is fabricated, nothing is filtered. The row status follows `statusOf`: 1 when the quote is OK or PARTIAL (a thin option waits the 20 s of the kind and ends PARTIAL), 2 when nothing wanted arrived or the quote has no bid, ask, last and implied vol at all. The prior close of the broker (`Quote.Close`) is not stored: `obs.option_eod.r1` has no column for it, and the dataset's status 4 "stale" is not written by the job. This design does not change that, because it would be a contracts change of its own; it is named in Left out and open point 5, because a reader of a thin row cannot tell a day without trade from an absent quote except by `bid`, `ask`, `volume` and `event_ts` being nil or old. `lot_size` of an option instrument is the chain multiplier (100); `option_price_increment` is the manifest value 1; the stock underlying is stored as `Type stock` with `mic = XTKS`.
9. Time. Slot 15:50 JST, `freeze_after` 1550, `stop_after` 1700, the day is the JST date, no backfill: as in the first design. The keys of one slot run one after the other in plan order; `NewJPWithWatch` orders the plan with `N225` first and the codes after it, so the stocks can never starve the index (the plan of `NewJP` is N225 alone and does not change); a key that starts at or after 17:00 is `NoValue` "after session", so the nine keys must finish before 17:00. The cost is bounded and not measured: `listed()` resolves each pair one after the other through `ResolveContract` (up to 48 per name, the latency of one is unknown), a thin option then waits up to 20 s in a chunk of 50 under the 70 market data lines of pyside, and the stock's own snapshot waits 20 s (no bid and ask after the close either); `budget_minutes = 8` is per key, so nine keys can in the worst case use 72 minutes, more than the 70 between 15:50 and 17:00, which is why N225 is first and why the stock keys are capped at 48 contracts on both strike paths (Decision 6). The dry run of Decision 11 prints the elapsed time of each key (wall clock; its job clock is fixed, so it does not trip the budget) and the owner sets the values from it. The session of the stock options, a night session and the close against 15:50 are not known (open point 7); the probe was made before 15:45, so the quote after 15:45 is not known either.
10. What is written about the owner. The codes come from `watch.toml`, which stays in `$OT_HOME`; the manifest `job_jp.toml` holds no code and no stock `con_id`; fixtures use synthetic codes (`1111`, `2222`) and made-up `con_id`; the live test names the two names the owner decided on, 7974 (options) and 278A (none), and no price, position or buy price. A named line holds the job id and symbol only.
11. The dry run covers the stock keys. `dryRunBuild` calls a new `buildOptionEodJP(d, b, r, w, watch, clk)` (as `buildOptionEod` for the US job), which `jobFactory.optionEodJP` also calls, so run-once, the scheduler and the dry run share one path; the field `deps.newOptionEodJPWatch` (default `optioneod.NewJPWithWatch`) is used when set, else `newOptionEodJP` (a test literal of `deps`). `ot run-once --dry-run --job option_eod_jp` then runs every stock key against the live gateway with the in-memory sink, prints one line per key with its wall elapsed time and its `no_value` text, and writes nothing; 278A shows "no listed options" there. The real run-once is the owner's step and holds the scheduler fence.
12. Reuse before a parallel pipeline: one `[stock]` table, one filter in `chainOfRoot`, one constructor, one factory function in `cmd/ot`. No second table, store, writer, broker client, scheduler, calendar or job id.

## Left out

- `option-candidates-jp`: gates, covered-call and cash-secured-put candidates for JP stocks, `ot candidates` JP output and the page. This design only stores the chain. Because the options are thin, that design must treat a nil bid or ask as no quote and a wide spread as no price.
- Strikes above the larger of 1.05 x buy and the price, and puts below 0.80 x buy: the range is the one of the US job (`rangeStrikes`), so a covered-call strike far above the price is not captured; without a `buy_price` the job takes the strike band of the manifest (0.10 around the price, at most 6 strikes per side). A wider range is a change of the shared rule and its own design.
- A close or no-trade column, or the writing of status 4 (open point 5).
- The early-assignment guard (JP single-stock options are American style) and the JP dividend and earnings calls for the stocks.
- Per-name caps by liquidity, open-interest filters, a cap on the number of expiries in the window beyond the two the window yields today, and any rule that drops a name by its price: a strike of 100000 yen or more is refused by the OSI check, which fails that key as `Permanent`; no stock of the list is known to be above it, and a wider identifier is a separate design.
- Weekly or mini classes of a stock series (the filter takes the class equal to the code and multiplier 100 only).
- A market column on `obs.option_eod`, keying the ledger by job, the translator wiring in `cmd/ot`, and `calendarrefresh` for XTKS: unchanged from `option-capture-jp.md` Left out.
- Backfill of past JP days, any account position, any Telegram text, and any list of names or a stock `con_id` in the manifest.
