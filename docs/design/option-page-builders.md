Goal: after the candidates job has stored a run, the owner opens `/options` of `ot serve` and sees board 5 with real rows: one card for the best ok short put of each company (contract line, annualized yield, IV line, liquidity, size), the full candidate table below it, and the strike by expiry chain of the first card; and opens `/options/stock/<symbol>` and sees board 18 for one company: top bar, strike by expiry matrix with the chosen contract, one card per ok contract, a price map per expiry and the table of all its contracts with Θ, Γ and Vega (rounded to 4 decimal places, "n/a" when null). Every number is a stored column printed as text (the three Greeks rounded to 4 decimal places), or one of two percentages computed from stored columns. What nobody stores yet (HV30, IVP, probability of finishing in the money, the excess over cash) is shown as a gap ("n/a"), never computed in the page. Strike % and spread % are computed by the builder from stored values (decided by the dispatcher 2026-11-10, Decision 6).

This design changes no schema and no view: `Contracts: unchanged`.
- `option_page.v2` needs `markets` (1 or 2 panes: `cards`, `candidates` table of at most 200 rows, `all_link` whose `target` is an in-page anchor id, `chain` or null), `stale`, `notes` (at most 20), `gaps` (at most 20) and `refresh` (`enabled`, `symbols`, `notice`, `fresh_count`, `total_count`), besides the run keys; a card has `verdict` (key `worth`, `marginal`, `pending`, `skip`), free text lines and at most 10 tags; a `Gap` reason is one of an enum of 13 values that holds `no_data`, `no_reader`, `missing_quote`, `stale`, `not_built` and `missing_input`. `stock_option_page.v2` is almost all strings and requires `stale` (a `StaleLabel` or null), `top`, `notice`, `shared_gap`, `maps` (at most 30), `matrix` (strikes at most 100, expiries 30, cells 2000), `cards` (a ladder of exactly 3 pairs), `no_card`, `held` (at most 50), `contracts` (a block of exactly 11 column texts and rows of at most 2000), `footer` and `refresh`; a price map has marks of kind `spot`, `cap`, `ma50`, `ma200`, `breakeven` or `strike` (this design writes `spot`, `breakeven` and `strike`) and at most 2 bands.

- `full_scan.v1` is not the IBKR scanner: its sections are `short_put`, `covered_call` and `put_credit_spread` tables of candidates. It is left out below for another reason.

## Decisions

1. One package, `internal/pages/option_boards`, holds both builders. They share the candidate lookback, the gate JSON, the decimal text and the envelope, and `modules.md` does not let one pages sub-package import another. Own implementation, because no library reads our gate text or shifts a decimal point in text; the small helpers of `option_candidates_page` (`shiftPoint`, `parseGates`, `daysBack`) are written again here and a later design may move both copies to one place (Left out).
2. Sources. Only `store.CandidateReader` (kind `short_put`), `store.OptionEodReader` (one call per distinct `UnderlyingID` of the rows shown, on the data day), for the one-stock page `openquote.OptionOpenReader` (`internal/store/openquote/api.go:33`; the design now records the `Quotes` field that `internal/pages/option_boards/page.go:48` already holds, wired at `cmd/ot/site.go:95`) and, for the one-stock top bar, `store.InstrumentNameReader` (nil gives a null name). Precedence of the contracts table and of the matrix (`internal/pages/option_boards/stock_contracts.go:51-54`): when a live row (the open or refresh row of `Quotes`) exists for the instrument, it replaces bid, ask, delta, IV, Theta, Gamma, Vega and volume; otherwise the `OptionEod` row is used. `OptionEod` takes one underlying id and may hold several captures of one instrument and day: the builder makes one call per distinct `UnderlyingID` of the rows shown (board 5 with rows of two companies gives two calls, the one-stock page gives one), reads `from` = `to` = the data day, keeps for each instrument id the row with the greatest `capture_id` (the reader's order makes it the last), and matches it to the candidate by instrument id. The rows hold `*float64`; `eodtext.go` turns them into text without a parse, in one rule per kind: volume and open interest with `strconv.FormatFloat(v, 'f', -1, 64)` (the stored text, unchanged), Theta, Gamma and Vega with `strconv.FormatFloat(v, 'f', 4, 64)` in one Greek-only function `greekText` of `eodtext.go` (owner decision 2026-11-11; the same call as `greekCell` of `internal/pages/option_data_page/format.go`), called for the `OptionEod` row and for the live row alike, while `floatText` keeps volume and the other live columns as they are; a null Greek, volume or open interest prints "n/a", the one `gapText` of `eodtext.go` (`eodtext.go:11`), as every other missing field of the board does. Amounts that the schema wants as numbers are written as `json.Number` of a decimal text, never through a float. Nil `Candidates` gives the page gap `no_reader`; a value is "n/a" only when neither row holds the value (a nil `Eod` and a row with no live row give "n/a" for volume, open interest and the Greeks; a field that a live row does not hold is "n/a", never filled from `OptionEod`, `livePrices` of `quote.go`); the live rows are the rows of `OptionOpen(ctx, day)` on the build day whose status is ok or partial, keyed by instrument id; a nil `Quotes` gives none and an error of the reader is an error of `Build`. The builders read no clock and no file, parse no float; exact decimal arithmetic uses `math/big.Rat` of the standard library in one file (`ratio.go`, Decision 6, as `option-candidates.md` Decision 1 does), and import no `notify` (grep-checked); the margin cushion and the assignment cash have no row (ADR 0010).
3. Day and lookback, the rule of `option-candidates-page.md` Decision 3 for one kind: the builder reads the build day and, when it has no row, the earlier calendar days back to 7 (day subtraction on the date text, no calendar); the first day with rows is the data day. `option_page.v2` requires `stale` (a `StaleLabel` with `data_time`, `stale_after` and `text`, both times in the owner-zone form `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS+08:00`, written in `Zone`), so `option_page` writes it in every case: fresh day (data day is the build day): `data_time` the run's `ComputedAt`, `stale_after` the end of the build day (`T23:59:59`), text "数据日 YYYY-MM-DD"; stale day (data day before the build day): `data_time` the run's `ComputedAt`, `stale_after` the end of the data day, text "数据日 YYYY-MM-DD,早于今日", and a `stale` gap; no row in 7 days: `data_time` and `stale_after` both the start of the build day (`T00:00:00`), text "没有候选行,候选任务尚未存入", and the gap `no_data` with the same text, `data_times` empty, an empty page that is still valid. `stock_option_page.v2` allows a null `stale`: the one-stock page writes null on a fresh day and for an unknown key, and the stale label above on a stale day.
4. `option_page`, one pane, US (`currency` USD). The Japanese pane is a fixed note "日本期权:后续设计 (option-candidates-jp)" as on the candidates page; it is never silent. Cards: the ok rows whose `RankInUnderlying` is 1, in the stored order, at most 6 (`maxCards`). Verdict from the stored status only: `ok` is `worth` ("值得看", tone good), `unknown` is `pending` ("数据不全", warn), `blocked` is `skip` ("不够格", muted). `marginal` is never written: it needs a judgement or a score and the owner dropped composite scoring (2026-11-09).
5. Card text, all printed from stored columns. `contract_line` "sell put 62 · 11-13 周五 · 22 天 · Δ −0.15" (strike as stored; month-day and weekday of the stored expiry; DTE as stored; delta as stored with "−" for the sign, "Δ n/a" when null). `iv_line` "IV 38% · HV30 n/a · IVP n/a" (IV by moving the decimal point two places in the stored text, no rounding; HV30 and IVP are always n/a). `annualised` is `PercentAmount` from the stored yield by the same shift, unit `percent`. `liquidity_line` "成交 V · 持仓 OI" from `OptionEod` of the row's instrument on the data day, "n/a" for a missing value. `size_line` "1 张 · 行权价 62 · 乘数 100" (printed, not multiplied; `size_usd_approx` null). `premium_line` "权利金 1.20 (收盘 bid 估) · 盈亏平衡 61.38 · 到期在价内概率 n/a · 比现金高 n/a". Tags: "E MM-DD" for a listed date, "E 无" for `absent`, "E n/a" otherwise, and one badge text per stored mark ("财报日期未确认", "已过财报,60 天内无财报"). The rich-premium tag has no producer and is not written. `reason` "各关卡通过" plus the mark texts. `headline` "N 个值得看。", N the number of cards (so the headline and the cards agree); `all_link` still counts all stored rows. `all_link` is "全部 M 个机会", target the id of the candidate table's section.
6. Candidate table, the 12 columns of board 5 in the fixture order (12 is the shape of the fixture; the schema allows more): symbol, verdict, annualised, strike_pct, dte, delta, bid_ask, spread_pct, volume, open_interest, ivp, earnings. All stored rows of the kind in the stored order (ok rows first, as the reader gives them), at most 200 (a note says how many were cut). `ivp` is a gap cell (`no_data`, text "n/a"). `strike_pct` is strike over the stored spot minus 1 and `spread_pct` is (ask minus bid) over mid, mid being (bid plus ask) over 2, both computed from the stored decimal texts with `math/big.Rat` (decided by the dispatcher 2026-11-10; an exception to `docs/contracts/pages.md` "computes nothing", which entry 1 records there), shown as a percent rounded half away from zero to one place ("−8.2%", "6.1%"); the sort `value` is the percent times 100 as an integer. A missing or zero input (spot, ask, bid, mid) gives a gap cell with reason `missing_input` and text "n/a spot" or "n/a ask", never a guess; the strike % is the strike's distance from the underlying close, the same figure the card shows no other way. The E cell is "E MM-DD 在期内" when the gate `events` of the row is `block`, else the tag text of Decision 5. A sort key `value` is an integer made by moving the decimal point in text (DTE as stored, delta times 100), and null when the text is not exact.
7. Chain card of the first card's company: rows are its stored contracts sorted by strike ascending (decimal text compare, no float), columns its expiries ascending; each cell is the stored bid (`UsdAmount` text), a pair with no stored row is a gap cell `no_data`; summary "已选:SYM MM/DD 行权价 $K Put". No row for the company gives `chain` null (the view shows "没有链数据").
8. `stock_option_page` for one company. The key is the `Issuer` text of a stored row (`^[A-Za-z0-9_-]{1,64}$`); the route is `/options/stock/<key>` and a bare `/options/stock` uses the issuer of the first ok row of the data day, else of the first row. A key with no row gives `no_card` ("没有该标的的候选行"), an empty matrix with `empty` text, `shared_gap` text and `run_status` `partial`.
   - `top`: `symbol` the key; `name` from the name reader or null; `spot` "现价 X" (stored spot of the first row); `day_change`, `day_tone`, `quote`, `refresh_expiry`, `refresh_strike` null (no stored source); `iv` "IV 38% · HV30 n/a · IVP n/a" (IV of the first ranked row, "IV n/a" when null); `earnings` "财报 MM-DD" or "财报 无" or "财报 n/a" from the stored date and state; `trend` "趋势 n/a"; `policy` the fixed text "策略 sell put,买价线内才卖".
   - `matrix`: expiries ascending, strikes ascending (decimal text compare), one cell per stored row, key `c<instrument id>`. `yield_text` "年化 12.3456%", `premium` "权利金 1.20" (stored bid), `delta` "Δ −0.15", `volume` "成交量 N" or "成交量 n/a". `shade` `worth` for status ok, else `none` (`marginal` never written, Decision 4); `star` when `RankInUnderlying` is 1; `chosen` for the one ok row with the lowest stored `Rank`; `thin` when the gate `quote` is not `pass`; `earnings` when the gate `events` is `block`; `out` is always false because no delta band is defined anywhere (Left out). Cells and strikes over the schema limits are cut in the stored order and `notice` says so.
   - `cards`: one per ok row, at most 10, in the stored order (`in_matrix` true, `chosen` as in the matrix): title "put 62 11-13", `pass_tag` true, `yield_line` "年化 12.3456% · 22 天" (the stored yield shifted, no rounding, as `yield_text`), a ladder of three pairs (买价 bid, 中间价 n/a, 卖价 ask), `advice` "建议 卖出 1 张 put (收盘 bid 估,次日才卖)", `amounts` ["行权价 62 · 乘数 100", "盈亏平衡 61.38"], `risk` "风险 股价低于 62 会被行权", `liquidity` "流动性 成交量 N · 持仓 M".
   - `maps`: one per expiry (at most 30): marks `spot` (stored spot), `breakeven` and `strike` (stored texts of the ok rows of that expiry), `bands` empty and `description` "波动区间 n/a (compute 尚无)".
   - `contracts`: the 11 columns "到期, 行权价, 买价, 卖价, Δ, IV, Θ/天, Γ, Vega, 成交量, 年化"; one row per stored row of the company in the stored order (at most 200); Θ, Γ and Vega from the live row when there is one, else from `OptionEod` (Decision 2), rounded to 4 decimal places as the data page prints them (number format only: `internal/pages/option_data_page/format.go`, `greekCell` = `strconv.FormatFloat(v, 'f', 4, 64)`; owner decision 2026-11-11), "n/a" when null (the board's one `gapText`, not the gap text of the data page); a tiny negative, and a stored negative zero, prints "-0.0000" exactly as the data page does (`format.go` has no negative-zero rule, so none is added here); for the archived row and for a live row alike. `held` is an empty list and `notice` points to `/options/tracking`; `footer` "由存档收盘报价与候选表生成".
9. Shared envelope. `run_status` is `ok`, or `partial` when any gap exists; `data_times` has one source "candidates" with the run's `ComputedAt` in the owner zone; `scope` is `options` or `stock-options`; `run_id`, `build_id` and zone as in `option_candidates_page`; `at` is given by the caller in UTC and `generated_at` is written in `Zone` (`at.In(Zone)`, form `+08:00`, as `market_page` does). A store error fails the build (the route answers 500 with its fixed text).
10. Routes. `/options` registers the family `option_page` (name `option_page`, order 40: `ot-wire-split` reserves 10, 20 and 30) and replaces the sample route of that path. `/options/stock/<key>` registers one family with a key; it has no name, so `ot page --name` does not know it (it needs a key). The stock family needs a key in its route: `pageFamily` is owned by `ot-wire-split` and its `## Interface` now names this design's entry 6 as the one amendment (the optional field `buildKey`). Both are registered through `registerPage` in their own files of `cmd/ot` with their own page documents under `docs/contracts/ot/pages/`; `ot.md`, `wire.go`, `runonce.go` and `pagelist.go` are not edited.
11. Frontend stack (ADR 0034): the views exist and are not touched, so no UI line is added here. A payload that does not match its schema is a bug of the builder: each builder test validates its output against the schema file.
