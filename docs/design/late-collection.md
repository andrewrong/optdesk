Goal: every pull of a snapshot dataset (option_open, option_eod, quote_snapshot; US and Japan) stores its rows whenever it is made, however late and however often; every page that shows a slot's value (a quote point) shows when it was observed, and a close chain shows the newest time of its rows; the pages and computations read one row per contract by one rule (a row counts for its day only if it was received before the next session starts; then the priced, complete and newest row wins); the window, its close, its gap and its alert stay exactly as today and are the health mark; after the close a key that is missing, partial, empty or no_value is still collected, one key per pass, until the next session of the market starts (for the US; Japan's end of the close chain, 17:00 JST, is before its window close at 17:30, so Japan is retried only by the walk inside its window and has no late-phase day, Decision 5; the next open for the open dataset; for the close datasets the end of the close chain, the start of the next session of any kind (the next regular open for the US, the manifest's `stop_after` 17:00 Asia/Tokyo for Japan), which is the instant after which a row no longer counts for its day: for the US the reader applies it too, for Japan only the job's write guard applies it and the reader's own cut is the later next regular open, Decision 2).

## What main holds today, against the owner's words

1. A retry is a no-op by key. The three tables key on a capture id (`migrations/0006_option_eod.sql:42`, `0007_quote_snapshot.sql:28`, `0029_option_open.sql:47`) and the capture id is a SHA1 of known text: `day|dataset|subject` (`internal/jobs/optioneod/map.go:57-59`, used at `optioneod/run.go:210`), `day|slot|instrument` (`optionopen/core.go:477-480`), `day|dataset|subject` (`quotesnapshot/run.go:77-79`, used at `run.go:122`). The inserts are `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` (`snapshot/optioneod.go:28`, `snapshot/quotesnapshot.go:24`, `optionopenpg/queries.go:19`). A second pull of a key adds nothing; the first answer stays, even an empty one. Three texts say so: `internal/jobs/api.go:10-12`, `contracts/datasets/obs.option_open.r1.json:14` and `docs/contracts/datasets/obs.option_open.md:10` ("deterministic ... meets the primary key").
2. Guards turn a late run into a refusal: "wrong day" `NoValue` when the key's day is not the local date (`optioneod/run.go:118-120`, `optionopen/pull.go:222-224`, `optionopen/jp.go:261-263`, `quotesnapshot/run.go:99-101`); "missed slot" `NoValue` ten minutes after a slot (`quotesnapshot/run.go:108-109`); `stop_after` as `NoValue` "after session" (`optioneod/run.go:123-124`, `option_eod_jp` only, 1700) or `Permanent` "not started" (`optionopen/pull.go:246-247`, `jp.go:284-286`; `job_pull.toml` 1000 ET, `job_pull_jp.toml` 0930 JST); `freeze_after` returns an empty result when the local time of day is before it (`optioneod/run.go:126-128`, 1615 US, 1550 JP), by the clock of now and not the key's day, so a late pull of an earlier day's key before that time of day does nothing. `quotesnapshot/run.go:150` ends `NoValue` "no quote" at the first empty slot. The slots of `quote_snapshot` are 0945, 1230 and 1530 but its one scheduled instant is the close minus 30 minutes (`quotesnapshot/job.toml:5` and its `[schedule]`), so today only the 1530 key produces; the other two end `NoValue` "missed slot".
3. The runner marks and skips. A `NoValue` result is marked `no_value` (`runkey.go:207-208`) and a marked key is skipped by `Reconcile` (`reconcile.go:266`). A counted run ends a key after two (`reconcile.go:344-378`). A key missing at its window close gets one more run after the close (reason `late`, `reconcile.go:347`), and then `closeRow`/`expireIfClosed` (`reconcile.go:383-438`) rewrite the latest run to `expired` or `partial` and mark `no_value`; `gaps.go:139` (`closed`) opens one `ops.job_gap` row per job, dataset and day (`migrations/0012_scheduler.sql:15`), which the notifier reports once. The window closes at the session close plus `lateness_hours` (`window.go:27-38`). `Backfill` and the console run a closed key that is missing or `no_value` (`backfill.go:60-100`, `backfillKey` at 282-300).
4. Readers take all rows. `OptionEod` and `QuoteSnapshots` return every row of a range ordered by day, instrument and capture id (`snapshot/optioneod.go:31-39`, `snapshot/quotesnapshot.go:27-31`); `optionopenpg` takes the newest row per instrument (`optionopenpg/queries.go:32-37`; contract text `openquote/api.go:35-37`). Consumers pick again by their own "latest" (`optioncandidates/calls.go:252`, `input.go:159`, `option_boards/load.go:85`, `option_data_page/builder.go:219-297`, `cmd/ot/live_quotes.go:230,486`, `companies_inputs.go`).
5. What a page shows of time: `option_boards/quote.go:98-106` shows the open row's `ReceivedAt` (or `EventTs`) as `AsOf` with the source `open_pull` whatever the row's time; `option_data_page` prints per point `Slot`, bid, ask, last and no time (`builder.go:110-116,236-239`), and its `data_time` is the maximum over all points (`builder.go:243-244`).

## Decisions

The rule over all of them, from the owner: **a late run never creates a gap or an alert, and never changes the kind the close wrote; it can only resolve one.**

1. **A pull's capture id is its own, and the key does not change.** The capture id becomes `uuid.NewSHA1(OID, day|dataset|subject|start)` (option_open: `day|slot|instrument|start`) on one line each, where `start` is the instant the run began, `time.Time.UTC().Format(time.RFC3339Nano)`. Same-key pulls then have different capture ids, so a re-pull never meets the primary key and always appends; the primary keys, tables and base views stay, and `x-primary-key` stays. The three texts of item 1 above say "one per pull" instead (`Contracts: changed`; they are in contracts files). `capture_id` stays a column; no query decides by it except as the last tie key of a rule. Rows are only appended. What bounds the copies: a late run is one key per pass, at most one an hour per key and one in six hours after an empty answer (Decision 5), and it stops at the collect-until time; inside the window a run is started as today. A late pull that returns only `no_data` rows appends them (about a chain of rows per run, `optioneod/run.go:377-380`): for a list of 30 names at most about 21 thousand unusable rows a weekend, which the reader hides and the table keeps (Doubt 4).
2. **One rule, in one place, with a final tie key, per day.** One SQL fragment in `internal/store/shared` (allowed by `docs/design/modules.md:37`; `scripts/arch/main.go:102-104` has `sharedSub`; the package is new) is used by the three readers; each reader keeps its column list and signature. The rule picks one row per subject **and session day** (contract and day; for `quote_snapshot` contract, day and slot), because `OptionEod` and `QuoteSnapshots` are range reads (`risk_page/load.go:151`, `structures_page/load.go:145`, `companies_inputs.go:124,149`, `option_data_page/builder.go:185,233`) and a partition without the day would collapse history. Every ordering ends with `capture_id DESC` as its last tie key; the full `ORDER BY` of each rule is written once below.
   - `option_eod` and `quote_snapshot` (the close datasets), in two steps, with one instant for collect-until and the write guard (the reader reads it for the US only, below): **the end of the close chain** of a key's day, the start of the next session of any kind of that market. Its source: when the job's manifest carries `stop_after` (`HHMM` in the manifest `zone`, `optioneod/manifest.go:83,167`), that local time on the key's day, which is `option_eod_jp`'s `1700` `Asia/Tokyo` that main already has (`optioneod/job_jp.toml:7-8`: "a run at or after 17:00 JST never writes night-session quotes"); otherwise the next regular open of the market's calendar, `NextSession(...).OpenAt` (`internal/clock/api.go:16-31`, from `master.session_day.open_at`, `migrations/0001_master_session_day.sql:5`; `XNYS` for `option_eod` and `quote_snapshot`, quotes before it are the close). The calendar has no night-session start (`clock.Session` has `OpenAt` and `CloseAt` only, `api.go:17-24`), and a per-day night start is not built (Left out). **Eligibility:** a row counts for its day key only when its `received_at` is before that instant. It is held by two cuts that differ for Japan. For `XNYS` the instant is the next regular open: the late run is cut at it (Decision 5) and the reader drops a row received at or after it (`rd.session_day_r1`, `migrations/0028_session_day_view.sql:5`). For a job with `stop_after` (`option_eod_jp`) the tight cut is the job's write guard alone (taken on the key's own day and not on today, 17:00 JST): the reader cannot know the manifest time, and its own cut for such a job is only the next regular open of the calendar, later than 17:00 JST, a backstop and not the same instant. The market of a row is the `calendar_id` of its instrument (`master.instrument.calendar_id`, `migrations/0008_master_instrument.sql:13`; `internal/store/api.go:61`, `internal/store/instrument/read.go:11-13`), joined on `instrument_id` through `rd.instrument_r1`: `ot_ro_pages` can use nothing on schema `master` (it has no `USAGE`; its `SELECT` on `master.instrument`, `0008_master_instrument.sql:63`, is unusable, and `store.md:45` saying it has no grant on a base table is already false on main, corrected in the same edit), and one SQL fragment serves both `ot_ro_pages` and `ot_runner`, so both read the calendar id through the view (`ot_runner`'s own `USAGE` and `SELECT` on `master`, `migrations/0004`, `0008:65`, is not used by the rule), and `rd.instrument_r1` (`migrations/0010_instrument_names_view.sql:4-10`, granted to `ot_ro_pages`) has `instrument_id, instrument_type, name, currency` and no `calendar_id`, no other `rd` view carries a calendar per instrument (`rd.underlying_r1`, `migrations/0033_underlying.sql:39`, has `market` `US`/`JP` per settings row, not a calendar id and not keyed by `master.instrument`; `rd.option_terms_r1`, `0009:21`, and `rd.session_day_r1`, `0028:4`, have none), so entry 4's migration adds the column to that view (`CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW`, the column last, the grant kept; `rd.instrument_r1` is granted to `ot_ro_pages` only, but `ot_runner` also reads `rd.option_eod_r1` and `rd.quote_snapshot_r1` (`docs/contracts/store.md:45`; the pg tests `TestOptionEod*PG` and `TestQuoteSnapshot*PG` open as `ot_runner`), so the migration also does `GRANT SELECT ON rd.instrument_r1 TO ot_runner` after the view is replaced: ot_runner gains read on rd.instrument_r1 (reference data only: four columns and the calendar id, no identifier or personal data), `docs/contracts/store.md` says so, and a pg test reads through the rule as `ot_runner` (`TestRuleReadsThroughInstrumentViewAsRunner`); its two readers `instrument/names.go:15` and `index_ids.go:16` name their columns and are unaffected). It is the same calendar id the job is planned with (`runner.WindowRule.CalendarID`, `internal/runner/api.go:26`; `Slot.CalendarID`, `api.go:166`; `sched/ticker.go:418`). The instrument join is an inner join: every row of the three tables has an instrument row by foreign key (`migrations/0008_master_instrument.sql:41-44`, `0029_option_open.sql:15`) and `calendar_id` is NOT NULL, so no branch for a missing instrument exists. A row past the cut is dropped by the rule, not deleted. When the row's calendar has no next session loaded (`calendar_id` is NOT NULL, but a session day can be missing), the reader applies no cut to that row: it counts, because a row dropped for a missing calendar day would leave the key silently missing; the missing day is a gap of the calendar data, named in Doubt 10 (`TestRuleCalendarWithoutNextSessionHasNoCut`). **Rank among the eligible rows of a key:** two-sided priced first, then complete, then the NEWEST `received_at`, and only then `generation DESC` as a tie-break (`generation` is the broker quote's `Generation`, `broker/api.go:64`, set at `optioneod/map.go:75`, column `migrations/0006_option_eod.sql:14`; it may restart from zero when the gateway restarts, so it must never outrank time), then `capture_id DESC`. The full order of the close datasets is: priced desc, complete desc, `received_at DESC`, `generation DESC`, `capture_id DESC`. Priced is a two-sided quote: `bid > 0`, `ask > 0` and `ask >= bid`; a `last` alone, a zero side or a crossed quote is not priced, so a stale last never wins. Complete, for the option tables, means `delta` not NULL; `quote_snapshot` has no `delta` column (`migrations/0007_quote_snapshot.sql`), so for it every row is complete and the step ranks nothing. An unpriced or incomplete row never displaces a priced complete one, and a newer priced complete row displaces an older one: every pull has its time, nothing is overwritten and the newest usable one is read (owner ask 3023). This replaces the earliest-wins rank of revision 5, which would have kept a first row with a stale last, a zero or crossed quote, or a pre-close pull for ever, because every repair path (`resolve`, `store_once`, `Backfill`, the console press) only appends. A key whose eligible rows are all one-sided (bid 0, far strikes) is present, final, not a gap and not pulled again; the reader returns its newest eligible row as it is, and a zero bid means no bid, which is data (`TestRuleAllOneSidedKeyReadsNewestEligibleRow`). There is no "live before frozen" step, on purpose: the cut keeps the next session out of the day. The test names are in entry 4. The close chain of `option_data_page` shows one data time, the newest `received_at` over the rows it holds (Decision 4), not a time per row.
   - `option_open`: the open is the earliest live priced row (the open keeps its earliest-in-session rank on purpose: it is a point in time, and the owner's ask 3023 is about the close datasets, where the newest usable pull is wanted; priced here is as above, a two-sided quote; its full order is: in-session live priced rows first, by `received_at ASC`, then `capture_id DESC`; when a contract has no such row the fallback group by `received_at DESC`, `capture_id DESC`) at or after the slot within its session (`market_data_type = 1`, price present, `received_at >=` the slot instant, which the job guarantees by not quoting before its slot, and `NOT late`). The greeks of the open row are its own; a page may show greeks of another row only when it says so. "Newest" applies to the day's best row only: when a contract has no such row the reader returns its newest row, and that row (stored after the session, or without a live price) is never presented as the open: it carries `late` and its time (Decision 4). `late` is a new column of `rd.option_open_r1` and a new property `late` of the dataset contract, a derived flag: true when its day has an option_open window and `received_at` lies in none of them (the view adds `EXISTS (window rows of that day) AND NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM ops.dataset_window w WHERE w.dataset_id LIKE 'obs.option_open%' AND w.session_day = o.session_day AND o.received_at BETWEEN w.open_at AND w.close_at)`, with `o` the alias of the view's own table (an unqualified `session_day` would resolve to `w`'s column and make the clause a tautology); a day with no window row at all (older history, a window never written) is not late, so history does not read late; the US and Japan sessions do not overlap in time, so no currency mapping is needed). One migration, numbered the next free number on main when the entry is built. A row of the old table has `late` false when its time is in a window: no data migration.
   A later row for one contract appends and is read with no change of the consumers that pick "latest" (they tie on the capture id and the rule decides first). Two consumers change meaning and are named in Decision 4.
3. **The guards stop refusing; the window and its marks stay.** For the snapshot jobs the "wrong day" guard goes (the reconciler passes the key's day), "missed slot" goes, `stop_after` goes from `option_open` and `option_open_jp` (`optionopen/job_pull.toml:11`, `job_pull_jp.toml:11`); `option_eod` (US) and `quote_snapshot` never had it, and the key is removed from the manifests that had it, its field goes from the structs where no manifest uses it (the decoders, `toml.Unmarshal` of go-toml v2, ignore unknown keys, so nothing rejects a leftover and no test claims it does), and it stays for `option_eod_jp` with a new meaning: it is no longer a refusal of the whole key but the end of the close chain (Decision 2): a run at or after it, on the key's day, ends `NoValue` "after session" as on main (unmarked before the window close, Decision 5), and the walk inside the window collects Japan from the first run up to it. The check is also made while the run stores, not only at its start (main checks once, `optioneod/run.go:116-125`, so a run started at 16:55 JST could store a quote received at 17:03 and it would stand as the close): a quote whose `received_at` is at or after the stop is not stored under the day key and the run ends there, in the budget check or before each store. A run cut at the stop keeps and counts the rows it stored (`Returned`, `Usable`). "Usable rows for the key" means `Usable > 0`: any one stored status-ok row of the key (a run is one key, `runkey.go:100`). When it stored at least one such row it returns them with no error, even if only some chunks of the chain were stored before the cut (`TestRunCutAtStopWithSomeChunksStoredIsPresent`: the key is present, no gap and no alert), as a finished run, and logs why it stopped; main's `verdict` marks a `NoValue` error first (`runkey.go:207-208`), another error next, and `Usable > 0` present at 221 before the empty-before-close and default cases, so with no error the key is present by the `Usable > 0` case and this case needs no verdict change; the verdict changes only as Decision 5 says, for an unmarked `NoValue` of a `Late` job before its close, which contradicts `docs/contracts/runner.md:22` ("A `NoValue` error is `no_value` at any time"): entry 5, whose Files hold `docs/contracts/runner.md`, rewrites that sentence with the exception (`TestRunCutAtStopAfterUsableRowsReturnsThemWithNoError`). This amends Decision 8 of `option-open-gap-jp.md` ("wrong day ... stays") for the two option_open pulls only. 5. **Late collection after the close, by two plain methods and a phase.** `runner.Late` (new in `internal/runner/api.go` next to `Windowed`, `Bounded` and `Postcondition`, lines 153-187): `LateCollect() string`, standard-library type only, so a job package does not import `runner`; it returns `"resolve"` (a usable late answer resolves the key) or `"store_once"` (one late pull is stored and the key's state is left as the close wrote it). A second method, `LateStop() (hhmm, zone string)`, returns the manifest's `stop_after` and `zone` of a job that has one (`option_eod_jp`) and empty strings otherwise, so the reconciler learns the end of the close chain from the job and a job package still does not import `runner`. A job without the method is unchanged to the line. The window, its close, `closeRow`, the mark, the gap and the alert are not changed; they are the health mark, at today's time, once.
   - **Inside the window.** One change: a `NoValue` result of a job with `Late` before its close is not marked: `verdict` (`runkey.go:203-228`) gives it the reason `empty_before_close` and no mark, as an empty answer gets today, so the key keeps being tried each pass until the close; the run after the close ends it as today (`reconcile.go:347`). `deadlineFor` (`runkey.go:288-296`) and the per-run cut are unchanged.
   - **collect-until**: `shared.CollectUntil(ctx, cal clock.Calendar, calendarID string, day contracts.Day, closeChain bool, stopHHMM, stopZone string) (time.Time, error)` in `internal/runner/shared`, the one function the reconciler uses. For `option_open` (`closeChain` false) it is the open of `cal.NextSession(ctx, calendarID, day)` (`clock/api.go:31`), as before. `closeChain` is not a new method: the reconciler takes it from the mode of the job, `LateCollect() == "resolve"` is the close chain (`option_eod`, `option_eod_jp`, `quote_snapshot`) and `"store_once"` is the open (`option_open`, `option_open_jp`), a rule `TestCollectUntilCloseChainFollowsMode` states, and the stop pair is the job's `LateStop()`.    - **the late phase**, after the normal walk of a `Reconcile` pass (`reconcile.go:111-126`): `late.go` first closes every lapsed late run of the last 14 session days, also of a day whose collect-until has passed (below). Then, for each calendar of the jobs that have `Late`, the session days of the last 14 whose window of the dataset has closed and whose collect-until is after now (for the US close chain on a Monday before the open that is Friday, after the open Friday is out; for Japan's close chain no day qualifies, since 17:00 JST is before its window close of 17:30; the Japan repair time is the walk inside the window, from 15:50 to 17:00), oldest first; the keys of that day's stored plan (`ledger.Expected`) whose dataset belongs to a job with `Late` and whose mark is `no_value`, that is a key the close has already written (the row of `closeRow` or the verdict after the close). A key that is unmarked is never picked: before the close has written its row (`expireIfClosed`, `reconcile.go:413-438`) a late run could race the normal walk and decide a state the close should decide; a present key is never picked. It runs at most one key per pass and returns. A key is skipped: while a run of it is running with a live lease; for `resolve`, when its latest late run started less than one hour ago, or less than six hours ago when that run ended empty (done, no error, no usable row: a permanent empty condition such as no listed options does not take the slot every hour); when its latest late run ended `Permanent` (a fault of the configuration is not time); for `store_once`, when a late run of the key has `UsableN > 0` (done or partial: the key is not retried once a usable late row exists; `option_open` counts every inserted row in `Returned`, `pull.go:272` and `jp.go:307`, and only usable ones in `Usable`, `pull.go:278` and `jp.go:313`, so a pull of all-error rows or a share shortfall has not spent the shot); a `store_once` run killed before it wrote its counts (kill -9: the row stays `running` with `UsableN` 0 and is closed `abandoned`) is not known to have stored usable rows, so its key is pulled again after one hour: accepted, the pull appends and the case is rare (`TestLateStoreOnceAfterKilledRunPullsAgain`); a `store_once` run with no usable row, empty or with a job error, is tried again like `resolve`, after one hour, six after an empty answer, never after `Permanent`. A key is started only when now plus its run bound is before the earliest `NextStart` of `store.SchedulerLedger.Status(ctx).Jobs` (`ticker.go:440`), else the pass logs `late_yield` and ends (a nil ledger has no yield). A calendar error ends the late phase of that calendar for the pass with a logged reason `late_calendar` and does not fail `Reconcile`. The pass cadence is 15 minutes (`ticker.go:31-32`), so N late keys take N passes a round. The console and `ot backfill` run any closed key at any time and are not limited by this phase.
   - **the phase plans the day it runs**: a stored plan holds keys and no jobs, and every late job is a listed job whose `Run` serves a key only when its last `Plan` was for that day (`cmd/ot/listedjob.go:139-141,147-163`: `Plan(day)` sets `l.day`, `Run` answers `Permanent` "no plan for these keys" otherwise). A key whose job is not in the plan (the plan of the job failed and was skipped, `reconcile.go:158-167`, or the list no longer holds the name) is skipped without any state; the stored plan may hold names the list has dropped, which is correct and never retried. Planning day D sets `l.day` to D; nothing else runs after the phase in a pass (one worker, one lease; the normal walk, which plans today for its own runs, comes first, and the next pass plans today again), and the console force and Backfill plan their own day, so today's run is not broken. Why not the close's own reason: a done run with reason `expired` would turn a `partial` gap into `expired` and a gap-less key into an `expired` gap (`gaps.go:172-178`), and a crashed late run would count toward `crashBrake`.

## Left out

- A primary-key change: the capture id per pull makes every pull's rows distinct without it, and a key change would rewrite three hypertables, two of them compressed, and every `ON CONFLICT` and `x-primary-key`, for no reader.
- Views other than the two view columns (`late` and `calendar_id`): the one SQL rule of `internal/store/shared` is the single place a reader picks a row, so a union or a "best row" view would be a second copy of it that can drift.
- A `source` column: only one source (IBKR through the gateway) writes these tables today; a column in compressed hypertables is costly to add and no reader would use it, so it waits for a second source (Decision 10).
- A calendar-based day guard: the reconciler already passes the key's own day to the job, so a guard comparing the key's day with a calendar day has nothing left to refuse (Decision 3).
- A retry count or a tuple of bounds: the owner asked that a stop time never gives up, and a count would give up while the market is still closed; time (collect-until) is the bound, plus the spacing of Decision 5.
- A new gap kind and a notify change: the close already writes the one gap and sends the one alert, which is the health mark the owner wants at today's time; a second signal for a late run would repeat it, and the recovery reaches the owner by the existing morning digest.
- A late reason that readers count: a counted late row would flip `partial` to `expired`, give a gap-less key a gap and push late crashes toward the brake (Decision 5), so late rows are ignored by those rules.
- A widened window: it would move the close, the gap, the alert and the restart blocker, which the owner wants left at today's time; collect-until lives only in the late phase.
- A per-day night-session start in the calendar (a `Session` field, a column of `master.session_day` and its loader): it would replace the constant `stop_after` 17:00 JST that main already trusts (`optioneod/job_jp.toml:7-8`) and unlock nothing, since Japan's close chain already ends at that instant for collect-until and the write guard (the reader's backstop for Japan is the next regular open, Decision 2); a change of the night start would be a change of one manifest line.
- A "late" flag computed by a page: the row already carries `late` from the reader and the time is shown, so a page has nothing to compute (Decision 4; the web front end computes nothing).
- A time per row in the close chain of `option_data_page`: it would add a field to every chain row of the v2 schema for a case (a later pull next to the close pull in one chain) that appears only after a repair, and the newest time of the chain already tells it; the chain shows its newest time (Decision 4).
- New versions of `page.option_page` and `page.stock_option_page` for a `late_pull` source: a larger contracts change than the need requires (Decision 4).
- Repair of a present partial key (Decision 7): the key is present and final by the scheduler's own rule, and the owner's press repairs it; a scheduler rule for it needs a completeness check the three datasets do not have.
- Repair of a missed `option_open` open: the open is a point in time that cannot be pulled later; a late pull is stored and shown as late and never resolves the gap (Decision 9).
- Jobs other than the option datasets: bars, dividends and the others have no slot value that a late pull falsifies and are repaired by their own retry; a global cap of pulls per day for IBKR's lines: the pass cap and spacing already bound it to one key per pass, and no measured limit exists [unverified].
- The console's force rows: a follow-up of `job-console.md` once Decisions 1 and 2 are on main. The removal of the page v1 schema: it waits until no consumer reads it.

## Doubts

1. Whether the gateway answers `market_data_type` 1 for option ticks after the close is unverified (`pyside/src/optdesk/ibkr/spec.py` not read in full); the close datasets do not depend on it (they have no live-before-frozen step: eligibility by the next session start and the newest priced row decide), and the earliest-in-session rule for the open does not either.
2. Whether `rd.option_open_r1` may reference `ops.dataset_window` under the view roles is unverified (one existing ops view at `migrations/0012_scheduler.sql:71`); the pg test of entry 4 settles it, and if it fails the flag moves to a column written by the job.
3. A present partial key is final and nothing lists it but the observed times; the owner may want a page for it (a follow-up).
4. The growth of unusable rows by late pulls and by the in-window retries of empty keys (Decision 5, no longer marked) is estimated, not measured.
5. A calendar with no session within 14 days back logs `late_calendar` and no late run happens for it; the arch check of `internal/store/shared` was not run [unverified].
6. A lapsed late row of a day older than 14 session days stays `running` forever (kill -9 and then no pass for 14 days); harmless to the blocker and to every reader, but it shows on the status and console runs lists.
7. An owner press (`Backfill` or the console force) runs on the same worker and waits behind a live late run, up to its bound of 16 to 25 minutes, and refuses a key with a live row (`backfill.go:77`); a press that ends empty on a key with an `expired` gap clears that gap at the next sync, because a press run has the reason `backfill` and `keyKind` counts it (`gaps.go:173-175`): that is main's behaviour today and not the late run's.
8. A day closed while the scheduler was down is run by the normal walk after the close with the reason `late` (`reconcile.go:344-378`), as today, and is not a late-phase case; a `crashed_repeatedly` key is retried every six hours all weekend, each a real pull, bounded by the pass cap.
