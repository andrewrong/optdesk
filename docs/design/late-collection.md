# Late collection: every pull stores its own rows, one rule reads them, a missing key is tried again until the next open

Author: the optdesk designer (draft)
Owner approved: (pending)
Date: 2026-11-12
Revision: 3 (revision 1 changed the key and added views, a calendar guard, a gap kind and a notify entry; revision 2 per-pull capture ids with a tuple, a run count and a retry_bound reason; revision 3 keeps the per-pull capture id and widens only the window end, time is the bound, the existing expired gap is the mark)

Source: owner asks 3015, 3021, 3023, 3027, 3029, 3032 and 3034, in meaning. Losing the open is fine while the market is still open: collect late, and repair what was missed until the next session opens; do not be rigid. A stop time only judges "normal or late", it never gives up: the owner trades options and needs the data, and repeated option pulls are fine. Storage must be extensible with no hard-coded logic; the owner does not care which capture a row came from, what matters is that collected data is stored and can be repaired; a capture id may be kept and used by logic but must never stop data from being added.
Read on origin/main at 96bbaeb5: `AGENTS.md`, `docs/design/modules.md`, `docs/design/scheduler.md`, `docs/design/option-open-gap-jp.md`, `migrations/0006`, `0007`, `0012`, `0029`, the three writers and readers, `internal/runner/reconcile/*.go`, `internal/runner/sched/{gaps,ticker}.go`, `cmd/ot/listedjob.go`, the manifests of `optioneod`, `optionopen` and `quotesnapshot`, and the advisor's review r1 of revision 1. File:line cites are of that commit; "[unverified]" marks what no read could settle.

Goal: every pull of a snapshot dataset (option_open, option_eod, quote_snapshot; US and Japan) stores its rows whenever it is made, however late and however often; the pages and computations read one row per contract by one rule, the row carries its observed instant; a key with no usable answer is tried again until the next open of its market; a stop time stops refusing and only says "late".

## What main holds today, against the owner's words

1. A retry is a no-op by key. The three tables key on a capture id (`migrations/0006_option_eod.sql:42`, `0007_quote_snapshot.sql:28`, `0029_option_open.sql:47`) and the capture id is a SHA1 of known text: `day|dataset|subject` (`internal/jobs/optioneod/map.go:57-58`, used at `optioneod/run.go:210`), `day|slot|instrument` (`optionopen/core.go:477-480`), `day|dataset|subject` (`quotesnapshot/run.go:77-79`, used at `run.go:122`). The inserts are `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` (`snapshot/optioneod.go:28`, `snapshot/quotesnapshot.go:24`, `optionopenpg/queries.go:19`). A second pull of a key meets the key and adds nothing; the first answer stays, even an empty one. The comment of the type says so too (`internal/jobs/api.go:10-12`: "one per key, deterministic").
2. Guards turn a late run into a refusal: "wrong day" `NoValue` when the key's day is not the local date (`optioneod/run.go:118-120`, `optionopen/pull.go:222-224`, `optionopen/jp.go:261-263`, `quotesnapshot/run.go:99-101`); "missed slot" `NoValue` ten minutes after a slot (`quotesnapshot/run.go:108-109`); `stop_after` as `NoValue` "after session" (`optioneod/run.go:123-124`, `option_eod_jp` only, 1700) or `Permanent` "not started" (`optionopen/pull.go:246-247`, `jp.go:284-286`; `job_pull.toml` 1000 ET, `job_pull_jp.toml` 0930 JST). `quotesnapshot/run.go:150` also ends `NoValue` "no quote" at the first empty slot.
3. The runner then gives up. A `NoValue` result is marked `no_value` (`runkey.go:176-177`) and a marked key is skipped (`reconcile.go:255-257`). A counted run ends a key after two (`reconcile.go:333-364`, `counted >= 2` at 361, the second only after a partial). Three crashed runs stop a key (`crashBrake`, `reconcile.go:311`, tested at 361). A key missing at its window close becomes `no_value` (`expireIfClosed`, `reconcile.go:400-421`). The window closes at the session close plus `lateness_hours` (`window.go:27-38`), the evening of the day. An empty answer before the close and a `Retryable` before the close are not counted and retried (`reconcile.go:352`, `runkey.go:187-197`). A run is cut at `max(close, now) + run bound` (`deadlineFor`, `runkey.go:253-262`), and the ticker finds the end of a window by `windowEnd` (`ticker.go:418-432`).
4. Readers take all rows. `OptionEod` and `QuoteSnapshots` return every row of a range ordered by day, instrument and capture id (`snapshot/optioneod.go:31-39`, `snapshot/quotesnapshot.go:27-31`); `optionopenpg` already takes the newest row per instrument (`optionopenpg/queries.go:32-37`). Two rows of one contract would reach ten consumers (`cmd/ot/site.go:52-53`, `cmd/ot/companies_inputs.go:37-39`, `internal/pages/option_boards/page.go:34`, `internal/pages/option_data_page/builder.go:45`, `jobs/optioncandidates/types.go:19`, `jobs/computefigures/types.go:18`, `jobs/optionopen/inputs.go:46`, and others). Two of them pick a row by `generation` then `CaptureID` (`optioncandidates/calls.go:252`, `optioncandidates/input.go:159`): with random capture ids that tie-break would be arbitrary.
5. The observed instant exists: `received_at` is `NOT NULL` on all three tables and `option_boards/quote.go:98` already shows the open row's `ReceivedAt` on its page. `event_ts` is the exchange's own timestamp when there is one.

## Decisions

1. **A pull's capture id is its own, and the key does not change.** The capture id becomes `uuid.NewSHA1(OID, day|dataset|subject|start)` (option_open: `day|slot|instrument|start`), where `start` is the instant the run of the key began, `time.Time.UTC().Format(time.RFC3339Nano)`. Same-key pulls then have different capture ids, so a re-pull never meets the primary key and always appends; the three primary keys, tables, views and the `x-primary-key` of `contracts/datasets/obs.option_eod.r1.json:11`, `obs.quote_snapshot.r1.json` and `obs.option_open.r1.json` stay as they are: no migration, `Contracts: unchanged`. `capture_id` stays a column; no query decides by it. Rows are only appended; the owner-only rule on UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE is untouched. What bounds the number of copies: a retry runs only while its key is missing (unmarked), and a run is started at most once per reconcile pass (every 15 minutes, `ticker.go:31-32`) until the window ends, so a key has at most four runs an hour, and a crashed run leaves rows that a re-run repeats under another capture id, which the reader of Decision 2 reads as one. The readers of `capture_id` outside the three stores on main are `optioncandidates/calls.go:252` and `input.go:159` (they get one row per contract from the reader of Decision 2, so their tie-break never sees two) and `cmd/ot/demo_option.go:60,79` (unique demo ids); `positionmarks/plan.go:27-40` is the account family. The three `uuid.NewSHA1` of the capture ids change (`optioneod/map.go:59`, `optionopen/core.go:479`, `quotesnapshot/run.go:79`); the `plan_id` hash (`optionopen/plan.go:252`) does not.
2. **One rule, in one place.** The rule is one SQL fragment in `internal/store/shared` (a sub-package of the store row, allowed by `docs/design/modules.md:37`; `scripts/arch/main.go:102-104` has `sharedSub` and `internal/runner/shared` already exists), used by the three readers. For each contract of a day (for `quote_snapshot` each contract and slot) the chosen row is `DISTINCT ON` that subject, ranked by quality before recency: (1) live data (`market_data_type = 1`), (2) a price present (`bid`, `ask` or `last` not NULL), (3) for the two option tables IV and greeks present (`delta` not NULL); then for `option_open` the earliest `received_at` (the open is the first good row), for `option_eod` and `quote_snapshot` the newest, `generation` before `received_at` as the consumers use it today. A frozen or last-only row of a later pull therefore never displaces a live, complete row, and a later row for one contract appends and is read with no change of any consumer: the readers keep their column lists, signatures and the base views `rd.option_eod_r1`, `rd.quote_snapshot_r1`, `rd.option_open_r1` (`optionopenpg/store_test.go:150` asserts that name and stays true; `snapshot/catalog.go:15` reads the same view and is not touched). `observed_at` is the existing `ReceivedAt`; it reaches the page of the open price already (`option_boards/quote.go:98`) and so "late" shows from data. A `quote_snapshot` row of a late pull keeps its slot label and shows its true `received_at`.
3. **The guards stop refusing; the open is bounded by the session.** For the five snapshot jobs the "wrong day" guard goes (the reconciler passes the day of the key; a late run of Friday's key on Saturday is what the owner wants for `option_eod` and `quote_snapshot`), "missed slot" goes, and `stop_after` goes (a manifest with it fails to load; the stop time's judgement "normal or late" is carried by `observed_at` against the slot and by the existing window-close gap of Decision 5, no second meaning). The before-slot guard of `quote_snapshot` stays but builds the slot instant from the key's day, not from the date of now (`quotesnapshot/run.go:105`); `option_open` and `option_open_jp` get the same lower bound at their slot, which they lack today (`docs/design/job-console.md` lists them as listed-only for that reason), so no stored `option_open` row is older than its slot and the reader needs no zone. `option_open` is not widened to the next open: its window stays the session (`optionopen/pull.go:139-141`), a late open is collected while the market is open and none after the close, so an after-close price is never stored as an open. `quotesnapshot/run.go:150` ends `NoValue` "no quote" at the first empty slot; with Decision 5(c) the runner does not mark that key before the window ends. The plan hash of `option_eod` and `option_open` includes `|stop..` (`optioneod/manifest.go:219-220`) and changes with the removal; the contract docs say so.
4. **One plain optional method, as `Windowed` and `Postcondition` are.** `runner.Late` (new in `internal/runner/api.go`, next to `Windowed`, `Bounded` and `Postcondition`, lines 87-121): `LateCollect() (untilNextOpen bool)`. A standard-library type only, so a job package does not import `runner` (the `jobs` row of `modules.md` depends on `contracts clock sources broker store compute symbol`). Presence means "collect late, tried again"; the value says where the window ends: `true` for `option_eod`, `option_eod_jp` and `quote_snapshot`, `false` for `option_open` and `option_open_jp`. A job without it is unchanged to the line. There is no count: time is the bound.
5. **The runner, for a job with `Late`:**
   - (a) with `untilNextOpen` the window of its datasets closes at the open of the next session of the window's calendar (a day-by-day look-ahead of at most 14 days over `clock.Calendar`, which covers Golden Week and Thanksgiving), in `windowFor` (`window.go:27-38`) and in the ticker's `windowEnd` (`ticker.go:418-432`), which must give the same instant;
   - (b) a missing key is started again at every pass while no run is running and the window has not ended; the rule "second run only after a partial" (`reconcile.go:361`) and the crash brake (`reconcile.go:311`, `crashBrake`) do not end a late series: the window end does, and the spacing is the reconcile cadence;
   - (c) a `NoValue` result before the window's end is not marked: `verdict` (`runkey.go:172-198`) gives it the reason `empty_before_close` and no mark, as it does an empty answer today, so `reconcile.go:255-257` does not skip the key; at the window's end `expireIfClosed` (`reconcile.go:400-421`) makes it `no_value` as today;
   - (d) the run bound stays apart from the window's end: `deadlineFor` (`runkey.go:253-262`) for such a job is `now + run bound`, never `window end + run bound`, so a run started on Friday is cut by its own bound;
   - (e) the health and the bound are the marks that exist: at the window's end `closeRow` (`reconcile.go:370-390`) gives a still-missing key the reason `expired` or `partial` and `gaps.go` (`gaps.go:139`, `closed`) opens the existing `ops.job_gap` row, one open gap per job, dataset and day (`migrations/0012_scheduler.sql:15`), which the notifier reports once; no new reason, no new gap kind, `gaps.go` and `notify` are not changed. Until the window ends a missing key shows no gap;
   - (f) who reads the window close, and what each shows while the window is open over a weekend: `reconcile.go:235,302` writes it (`putWindows`); `reasonLate` (`reconcile.go:33,336`) is no longer produced for these datasets, because no run is after the close, and stays for every other job: the run's own `finished_at` and the row's `observed_at` say how late; `backfill.go:287` lists a key inside its window as `InWindow` and does not run it, so the owner's press of a late key on Saturday is listed and not run until the console design runs flagged keys inside the window (named in Decision 7; Doubt 2); `gaps.go:139` shows no gap (above); `ticker.go:436` is item (a); `store/ops/ledger.go:31` only reads;
   - (g) the pass yields to what is due: inside a pass the keys of the turn's day run first and the retry-only keys of earlier days run after them, so Friday's retries cannot run before Monday's open pull is walked; a retry of an earlier day that is already running when a slot turn becomes due holds the lease for at most its run bound (tests below; Doubt 3).
   Load: a retry starts at most one run per missing key per 15 minutes, keys run one after another under the run lease, and a run is bounded by `budget_minutes` 8 and `chunk` 50 of its manifest, so the gateway sees at most 50 lines at once; IBKR's own limits are from memory [unverified].
6. **Partial keys.** A key is present when the pull passes its own check: `option_open` needs `min_ok_share` 0.5 of its planned items (`optionopen/pull.go:259-262`, a shortfall is a `Permanent` error and an unmarked key, so under Decision 5 it is retried until the window ends and the missing share is pulled). `option_eod`, `option_eod_jp` and `quote_snapshot` have no completeness check (a key is present when any row is usable, `runkey.go:151-157`), so a present partial key of theirs is not retried (Left out) and the runs page of `option-open-gap-jp.md` shows it from the counts of its `runview.Row` (returned and usable per key): nothing is silent, and the page that shows it is that design's entry 6. A present `option_open` key between 0.5 and 1.0 of its items is also shown by the counts and is not retried.
7. **Where jobs are wrapped, and the order.** `optioneod` and `optionopen` go through `f.listed(...)` (`cmd/ot/wire_optioneod.go:83,110`, `cmd/ot/wire_optionopen.go:100,145`), which forwards only the optional methods it knows (`cmd/ot/listedjob.go:175-198`; `cmd/ot/wire_underlying.go:136-146` has `listedWin` and `listedPost`); `LateCollect` is forwarded like `RunBound`, with a test. Order against `docs/design/job-console.md` (PR #1732): its entry 0 (runner interface: `BackfillOptions`, `ForceBackfiller`, `KeyRun`) and entry 3 (`backfill.go`, `backfill_force.go`, `reconcile.go`, `runkey.go`, `sched/gaps.go`) edit `internal/runner/api.go`, `reconcile/reconcile.go` and `reconcile/runkey.go`, the files of entry 1 here, so entry 1 here is built after both have landed and is rebased on them; entries 2 to 4 here do not touch those files and may start earlier. Two follow-ups belong to that design, not this one: force rows for `option_eod`, `option_eod_jp` and `quote_snapshot` can flip to yes once Decisions 1 and 2 are on main, and `BackfillWith` should run a `Late` key inside its window when the owner presses.
8. **Extension test, as acceptance.** A new pull time (another entry of `slots` in `quotesnapshot/job.toml`, another `slot` in a `job_pull*.toml`) changes no key, table or job id: the new pull has its own capture id and its own `received_at`. A second source that can observe the same instant is not covered: it would need a source column in the key, and it is not built until a second source exists.

## Doubts

1. Closed by reading: `internal/store/shared` is allowed (`docs/design/modules.md:37`, `scripts/arch/main.go:102-104`, `internal/runner/shared` exists); the arch check was not run [unverified].
2. Closed by reading: the reconcile cursor holds on an incomplete day and later days still run (`reconcile.go:92-95,106-121`, `ticker.go` walks from the cursor), and the pass runs every 15 minutes (`ticker.go:31-32`). Open: until the console design runs `Late` keys inside their window, the owner's press of a late key on a weekend is listed `InWindow` and not run (Decision 5(f)); the owner's repair then waits for the scheduler's next pass.
3. A retry of an earlier day that started before a slot turn became due holds the lease for at most its run bound (25 minutes for the option pulls); order inside a pass puts today first (Decision 5(g)) but cannot preempt a run in progress [unverified how the ticker queues a due slot behind a running worker, `ticker.go:449`].
