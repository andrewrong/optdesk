# Late collection: every pull stores its own rows, one rule reads them, a missing key is tried again until the next open

Author: the optdesk designer (draft)
Owner approved: (pending)
Date: 2026-11-12
Revision: 5 (revision 4 left the window and its close as on main and added the second time, collect-until; revision 5 states what a late run does to a key the close already wrote, as a table, keeps its gap, its kind and its alert untouched, moves the page to a v2 schema, and treats option_open's late pull as one stored pull that does not resolve the gap)

Source: owner asks 3015, 3021, 3023, 3027, 3029, 3032 and 3034, in meaning. Losing the open is fine while the market is still open: collect late, and repair what was missed until the next session opens; do not be rigid. A stop time only judges "normal or late", it never gives up: the owner trades options and needs the data, and repeated option pulls are fine. Storage must be extensible with no hard-coded logic; the owner does not care which capture a row came from, what matters is that collected data is stored and can be repaired; a capture id may be kept and used by logic but must never stop data from being added.
Read on origin/main at 484dbc41: `AGENTS.md`, `docs/design/modules.md`, `docs/design/scheduler.md`, `docs/design/option-open-gap-jp.md`, `docs/design/job-console.md`, the reconcile, sched, shared and store code the entries name, the manifests of the three jobs, `cmd/ot/listedjob.go`, `cmd/ot/wire_underlying.go` and `cmd/ot/schedstatus.go`, and the advisor's reviews r1, r2 and r3. File:line cites are of that commit; "[unverified]" marks what no read could settle.

Goal: every pull of a snapshot dataset (option_open, option_eod, quote_snapshot; US and Japan) stores its rows whenever it is made, however late and however often; every page that shows a slot's value shows when it was observed; the pages and computations read one row per contract by one rule; the window, its close, its gap and its alert stay exactly as today and are the health mark; after the close a key that is missing, partial, empty or no_value is still collected, one key per pass, until the market's next open.

## What main holds today, against the owner's words

1. A retry is a no-op by key. The three tables key on a capture id (`migrations/0006_option_eod.sql:42`, `0007_quote_snapshot.sql:28`, `0029_option_open.sql:47`) and the capture id is a SHA1 of known text: `day|dataset|subject` (`internal/jobs/optioneod/map.go:57-59`, used at `optioneod/run.go:210`), `day|slot|instrument` (`optionopen/core.go:477-480`), `day|dataset|subject` (`quotesnapshot/run.go:77-79`, used at `run.go:122`). The inserts are `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` (`snapshot/optioneod.go:28`, `snapshot/quotesnapshot.go:24`, `optionopenpg/queries.go:19`). A second pull of a key adds nothing; the first answer stays, even an empty one. Three texts say so: `internal/jobs/api.go:10-12`, `contracts/datasets/obs.option_open.r1.json:14` and `docs/contracts/datasets/obs.option_open.md:10` ("deterministic ... meets the primary key").
2. Guards turn a late run into a refusal: "wrong day" `NoValue` when the key's day is not the local date (`optioneod/run.go:118-120`, `optionopen/pull.go:222-224`, `optionopen/jp.go:261-263`, `quotesnapshot/run.go:99-101`); "missed slot" `NoValue` ten minutes after a slot (`quotesnapshot/run.go:108-109`); `stop_after` as `NoValue` "after session" (`optioneod/run.go:123-124`, `option_eod_jp` only, 1700) or `Permanent` "not started" (`optionopen/pull.go:246-247`, `jp.go:284-286`; `job_pull.toml` 1000 ET, `job_pull_jp.toml` 0930 JST); `freeze_after` returns an empty result when the local time of day is before it (`optioneod/run.go:126-128`, 1615 US, 1550 JP), by the clock of now and not the key's day, so a late pull of an earlier day's key before that time of day does nothing. `quotesnapshot/run.go:150` ends `NoValue` "no quote" at the first empty slot. The slots of `quote_snapshot` are 0945, 1230 and 1530 but its one scheduled instant is the close minus 30 minutes (`quotesnapshot/job.toml:5` and its `[schedule]`), so today only the 1530 key produces; the other two end `NoValue` "missed slot".
3. The runner marks and skips. A `NoValue` result is marked `no_value` (`runkey.go:207-208`) and a marked key is skipped by `Reconcile` (`reconcile.go:266`). A counted run ends a key after two (`reconcile.go:344-378`). A key missing at its window close gets one more run after the close (reason `late`, `reconcile.go:347`), and then `closeRow`/`expireIfClosed` (`reconcile.go:383-437`) rewrite the latest run to `expired` or `partial` and mark `no_value`; `gaps.go:139` (`closed`) opens one `ops.job_gap` row per job, dataset and day (`migrations/0012_scheduler.sql:15`), which the notifier reports once. The window closes at the session close plus `lateness_hours` (`window.go:27-38`). `Backfill` and the console run a closed key that is missing or `no_value` (`backfill.go:60-100`, `backfillKey` at 282-300).
4. Readers take all rows. `OptionEod` and `QuoteSnapshots` return every row of a range ordered by day, instrument and capture id (`snapshot/optioneod.go:31-39`, `snapshot/quotesnapshot.go:27-31`); `optionopenpg` takes the newest row per instrument (`optionopenpg/queries.go:32-37`; contract text `openquote/api.go:35-37`). Consumers pick again by their own "latest" (`optioncandidates/calls.go:252`, `input.go:159`, `option_boards/load.go:85`, `option_data_page/builder.go:219-297`, `cmd/ot/live_quotes.go:230,486`, `companies_inputs.go`).
5. What a page shows of time: `option_boards/quote.go:98-106` shows the open row's `ReceivedAt` (or `EventTs`) as `AsOf` with the source `open_pull` whatever the row's time; `option_data_page` prints per point `Slot`, bid, ask, last and no time (`builder.go:110-116,236-239`), and its `data_time` is the maximum over all points (`builder.go:243-244`).

## Decisions

The rule over all of them, from the owner: **a late run never creates a gap or an alert, and never changes the kind the close wrote; it can only resolve one.**

1. **A pull's capture id is its own, and the key does not change.** The capture id becomes `uuid.NewSHA1(OID, day|dataset|subject|start)` (option_open: `day|slot|instrument|start`) on one line each, where `start` is the instant the run began, `time.Time.UTC().Format(time.RFC3339Nano)`. Same-key pulls then have different capture ids, so a re-pull never meets the primary key and always appends; the primary keys, tables and base views stay, and `x-primary-key` stays. The three texts of item 1 above say "one per pull" instead (`Contracts: changed`; they are in contracts files). `capture_id` stays a column; no query decides by it except as the last tie key of a rule. Rows are only appended. What bounds the copies: a late run is one key per pass, at most one an hour per key and one in six hours after an empty answer (Decision 5), and it stops at the collect-until time; inside the window a run is started as today. A late pull that returns only `no_data` rows appends them (about a chain of rows per run, `optioneod/run.go:377-380`): for a list of 30 names at most about 21 thousand unusable rows a weekend, which the reader hides and the table keeps (Doubt 4).
2. **One rule, in one place, with a final tie key, per day.** One SQL fragment in `internal/store/shared` (allowed by `docs/design/modules.md:37`; `scripts/arch/main.go:102-104` has `sharedSub`; the package is new) is used by the three readers; each reader keeps its column list and signature. The rule picks one row per subject **and session day** (contract and day; for `quote_snapshot` contract, day and slot), because `OptionEod` and `QuoteSnapshots` are range reads (`risk_page/load.go:151`, `structures_page/load.go:145`, `companies_inputs.go:124,149`, `option_data_page/builder.go:185,233`) and a partition without the day would collapse history. Every ordering ends `received_at DESC, capture_id DESC`.
   - `option_eod` and `quote_snapshot`: priced rows before unpriced (`bid`, `ask` or `last` not NULL), among priced rows live data (`market_data_type = 1`) before frozen or delayed, for the option tables complete (`delta` not NULL) before incomplete, then `generation DESC`, then the newest. A frozen or last-only row of a later pull never displaces a live, complete one, and a live empty row never displaces a frozen priced one. A page that shows a close shows the time of the row the reader chose (Decision 4).
   - `option_open`: the open is the earliest live priced row at or after the slot within its session (`market_data_type = 1`, price present, `received_at >=` the slot instant, which the job guarantees by not quoting before its slot, and `NOT late`). The greeks of the open row are its own; a page may show greeks of another row only when it says so. "Newest" applies to the day's best row only: when a contract has no such row the reader returns its newest row, and that row (stored after the session, or without a live price) is never presented as the open: it carries `late` and its time (Decision 4). `late` is a new column of `rd.option_open_r1` and a new property `late` of the dataset contract, a derived flag: true when `received_at` lies in no option_open window of its `session_day` (the view adds `NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM ops.dataset_window w WHERE w.dataset_id LIKE 'obs.option_open%' AND w.session_day = session_day AND received_at BETWEEN w.open_at AND w.close_at)`; the US and Japan sessions do not overlap in time, so no currency mapping is needed). One migration, numbered the next free number on main when the entry is built. A row of the old table has `late` false when its time is in a window: no data migration.
   A later row for one contract appends and is read with no change of the consumers that pick "latest" (they tie on the capture id and the rule decides first). Two consumers change meaning and are named in Decision 4.
3. **The guards stop refusing; the window and its marks stay.** For the snapshot jobs the "wrong day" guard goes (the reconciler passes the key's day), "missed slot" goes, `stop_after` goes (a manifest with it fails to load; "normal or late" is carried by the observed time on the page and by the window-close gap), `freeze_after` applies only to a key whose day is the local date of now (so a late pull of an earlier day is never a no-op), and the before-slot guard builds its slot instant from the key's day, not from now (`quotesnapshot/run.go:105`); `option_open` and `option_open_jp` get that lower bound too, which they lack. This amends Decision 8 of `option-open-gap-jp.md` ("wrong day ... stays") for the two option_open pulls only. The `option_open` share shortfall (`optionopen/pull.go:259-262`) becomes `Retryable` (today `Permanent` ends the key at once), so inside the session the missing share is pulled again by the normal walk, as on main for any retryable result. `quote_snapshot`: with "missed slot" gone, the 15:30 run pulls all three keys and stores 15:30 quotes under the slots 0945 and 1230; that is correct data with a true time, and it must never reach a page without the time, so that entry is built after the entries that put the time on the page (Issues, order).
4. **Every page that shows a slot's value shows its observed time; a late open says so.** `option_data_page` becomes `schema_version` 2 (a new version, because a new required property after the first consumer breaks the page-schema rule of `docs/contracts/00-protocol.md:3`): each quote point carries `observed_at`. The v2 schema, its fixtures, the manifest of their hashes, the bad-version fixture and its test (`internal/contracts/optiondatapage_test.go:72-84`), the generated type, the web page and the builder move together in the order of Issues so that CI is never red between entries: the schema and fixtures first, the web page accepts v1 and v2 next, the builder emits v2 last (`TestBuildValidates`, `builder_test.go:178`, validates against the schema the builder says). `option_boards` already shows the open row's time (`quote.go:98-106`); its source for a row with `late` becomes `late_pull`, so a late open reads "late quote at T" and not "open pull". Two other consumers change meaning and are not edited: `cmd/ot/live_quotes.go:476-488` (`newestStored`, the notice "stored quotes of HH:MM") now names the time of the row the reader returns, which is the open row, and `optionopen/listed.go:211-213` reads the same rows through the reader. Pages that show only closes keep their `eodTime` and `data_time` (`builder.go:216-227,243-244`); after Decision 2 a Saturday frozen row can be the shown close, and its time is the one shown. No "late" flag is computed by a page.
5. **Late collection after the close, by two plain methods and a phase.** `runner.Late` (new in `internal/runner/api.go` next to `Windowed`, `Bounded` and `Postcondition`, lines 158-185): `LateCollect() string`, standard-library type only, so a job package does not import `runner`; it returns `"resolve"` (a usable late answer resolves the key) or `"store_once"` (one late pull is stored and the key's state is left as the close wrote it). A job without the method is unchanged to the line. The window, its close, `closeRow`, the mark, the gap and the alert are not changed; they are the health mark, at today's time, once.
   - **Inside the window.** One change: a `NoValue` result of a job with `Late` before its close is not marked: `verdict` (`runkey.go:203-228`) gives it the reason `empty_before_close` and no mark, as an empty answer gets today, so the key keeps being tried each pass until the close; the run after the close ends it as today (`reconcile.go:347`). `deadlineFor` (`runkey.go:288-296`) and the per-run cut are unchanged.
   - **collect-until**: `shared.CollectUntil(ctx, cal clock.Calendar, calendarID string, day contracts.Day) (time.Time, error)` in `internal/runner/shared` (the open of `cal.NextSession(ctx, calendarID, day)`, `clock/api.go:31`), the one function the reconciler uses.
   - **the late phase**, after the normal walk of a `Reconcile` pass (`reconcile.go:106-121`): `late.go` first closes every lapsed late run of the last 14 session days, also of a day whose collect-until has passed (below). Then, for each calendar of the jobs that have `Late`, the session days of the last 14 whose window of the dataset has closed and whose collect-until is after now (on a Monday before the open that is Friday; after the open Friday is out), oldest first; the keys of that day's stored plan whose dataset belongs to a job with `Late` and whose mark is `no_value`, that is a key the close has already written (the row of `closeRow` or the verdict after the close). A key that is unmarked is never picked: before the close has written its row (`expireIfClosed`, `reconcile.go:412-437`) a late run could race the normal walk and decide a state the close should decide; a present key is never picked. It runs at most one key per pass and returns. A key is skipped: while a run of it is running with a live lease; for `resolve`, when its latest late run started less than one hour ago, or less than six hours ago when that run ended empty (done, no error, no usable row: a permanent empty condition such as no listed options does not take the slot every hour); when its latest late run ended `Permanent` (a fault of the configuration is not time); for `store_once`, when a late run of the key returned rows (`ReturnedN > 0`, done or partial: the key is not retried once a late row exists); a `store_once` run that ended empty or with a job error is tried again like `resolve`, after one hour, six after an empty answer, never after `Permanent`. A key is started only when now plus its run bound is before the earliest `NextStart` of `store.SchedulerLedger.Status(ctx).Jobs` (`ticker.go:409-410`), else the pass logs `late_yield` and ends (a nil ledger has no yield). A calendar error ends the late phase of that calendar for the pass with a logged reason `late_calendar` and does not fail `Reconcile`. The pass cadence is 15 minutes (`ticker.go:31-32`), so N late keys take N passes a round. The console and `ot backfill` run any closed key at any time and are not limited by this phase.
   - **the late run**: the same `r.run` that `backfillKey` uses, with the reason `late_collect` on its row (id `day|dataset|subject|n`, `n` the run count plus one). **No reader counts a `late_collect` row**, as none counts `console_stop`: `keyKind` (`gaps.go:157-196`, through `dropConsoleStop`), `nextAttempt` and `closeRow` (`reconcile.go:344-437`) skip it, whatever its status and error code. So a late run never changes the kind the close wrote (a `partial` gap stays `partial`, a `crashed_repeatedly` gap stays so, a key with no gap gets none) and a crash of a late run never reaches the brake. Why not the close's own reason: a done run with reason `expired` would turn a `partial` gap into `expired` and a gap-less key into an `expired` gap (`gaps.go:176-182`), and a crashed late run would count toward `crashBrake`.
   - **a graceful cut keeps the reason**: on main `RunKey` writes a run cut without rows as `abandoned` with reason `abandoned_stop` and one cut with rows as `partial` (`runkey.go`, the switch after `job.Run`), and a deploy drain or the run's own bound is such a cut; `abandoned_stop` is counted as a crash by `keyKind` and `nextAttempt`. A context value of the late phase (as `consoleKey` is for the console) makes `RunKey` keep `late_collect` on both: status `abandoned` or `partial`, reason `late_collect` (a console cut of a late run keeps `console_stop`, which readers also ignore). `runkey.go` is in the Files of entry 5.
   - **the verdict of a late run**: `resolve` is the usual verdict (`runkey.go:203-228`: usable or a true Postcondition marks `present`; no usable row marks `no_value`, which the key already has); `store_once` marks nothing: a context value of the late phase makes `verdict` return no mark.
   - **a late run cut by a restart**: on a kill -9 its row stays `running` with a lapsed lease (a graceful drain is the cut above and leaves no running row); the restart blocker ignores late rows (`cmd/ot/schedstatus.go:104-107` counts every row of `st.Runs`, read by `store/ops/scheduler.go:47`, so on main a late run would hold a deploy for its whole bound, 16 to 25 minutes, nearly all weekend; entry 8 filters rows with the reason `late_collect`), and the next late phase closes it: status `abandoned`, reason `late_collect` kept. `markCrashed` (`reconcile.go:440`) is the one writer of `crashed` for both the normal walk and `closeExpired` (`backfill.go:263`, `backfill_force.go:183`, run by Backfill and the console force): it keeps `late_collect` and writes `abandoned` for a late row, so a press before the next phase cannot turn a lapsed late row into a crash; `backfill.go` itself is unchanged. A lapsed late row of a day older than 14 session days is no longer visited: it stays `running`, ignored by every reader and by the blocker, and shows as running on the status and console pages (Doubt 7). The window end, `windowEnd` (`ticker.go:454`) and the rest of `restartBlocker` (`schedstatus.go:108-115`) are unchanged.
   - **the gap is re-synced**: the ticker syncs the days it walked (`ticker.go:474-498`, `562-589`: the sessions after the cursor), and the cursor is already past a late key's day, so a late success would leave the gap open. `reconcileAndSync` also syncs the days of the open key gaps (`store.Gap` with no `JobID`) of the last 14 that the walk did not cover, with the preflight gaps of that day passed as they are (so `Sync` neither adds nor clears one: `gaps.go` `apply`). A late success marks the key `present`, `keyKind` gives it none, and the next sync clears the dataset's gap if no other key of it wants one. The owner learns of the recovery by the existing morning digest ("cleared after push", `notify/core/morning_text.go:31,68`). The notify pass runs once per `Sync` call, so a pass with open old gaps runs it more than once; notices are idempotent by `reported_at`.
   Load: at most one late run per pass, bounded by `budget_minutes` 8 and `chunk` 50 of its manifest, so the gateway sees at most 50 lines at once; IBKR's own limits are from memory [unverified].
6. **Only the option datasets are late.** `LateCollect` is offered by a conditional wrapper like `listedPost` (`cmd/ot/wire_underlying.go:136-165`): `listedLate` is built over `listedWin` only when the newest inner job has `LateCollect`, and `listedOf` (172-181) handles it. None of the five late jobs has a `Postcondition` (on main only `option_list`, `optionopen/list_run.go:97`, and `computefigures` have one), so their key is marked by the `Usable` rule of `verdict`; the wrapper therefore does not forward a postcondition, and a job with both methods is refused at construction by the factory (a test), since none exists. Bars, dividends, earnings, fundamentals, `ivhistory`, `computefigures` and candidates, built by the same listed wrapper, are not `runner.Late`. A test asserts it.
7. **Partial keys.** A present key is final: `option_eod`, `option_eod_jp` and `quote_snapshot` have no completeness check, and `option_open` is present from `min_ok_share` 0.5 of its items. Their missing share is not pulled again by the scheduler; the owner's press pulls it, and nothing shows a partial key except the counts of the run and the observed times (Left out, Doubt 3).
8. **Order against the others.** Entry 5 (runner) edits `internal/runner/api.go`, `reconcile/reconcile.go`, `reconcile/runkey.go`, `sched/gaps.go` and `sched/ticker.go` with entries 0, 3 and 4 of `docs/design/job-console.md` (PR #1732): it is built after all three. Entry 6 (jobs) edits `optionopen/pull.go`, `jp.go` and their tests with entry 3 of `option-open-gap-jp.md`, and entry 4 (store) edits `openquote/api.go` with entry 1 of it: both after those. The console's force rows for `option_eod`, `option_eod_jp` and `quote_snapshot` can flip to yes once Decisions 1 and 2 are on main, a follow-up of that design; `BackfillWith` already runs a closed key (`backfill.go:60-100`), so the owner's press of a late key works.
9. **option_open: the open stays missed, the late quote is stored and shown as late.** The window of `option_open` is the session (`pull.Window`, `optionopen/pull.go:140-142`, no offsets), so every late pull is after the session and can never be an open. Inside the session main's retry stands. After the session the late phase makes ONE late pull (`store_once`): rows are stored with their true time and `late` true; the key's mark and its gap stay as the close wrote them (the open is missed, and the gap says so); the page shows the late quote with its time (Decision 4: "late quote at T" by the source `late_pull`); the key is not pulled again once a late row exists; a late pull that stored no row (empty, or a job error) is tried again, one hour later, six after an empty answer. "Recovered" is defined per dataset: `option_eod` and `quote_snapshot` are resolved by a usable row after the close; `option_open` only by a row inside the session, which no late run can make: the gap of a missed open stays.
10. **Extension test, as acceptance.** A new pull time (another entry of `slots` in `quotesnapshot/job.toml`, another `slot` in a `job_pull*.toml`) changes no key, table or job id: entry 6 has the test. A second source that can observe the same instant is not covered: it would need a source column in the key, and it is not built until a second source exists.

## What a late run does to a key the close already wrote

Notation: R the run row of the late run (reason `late_collect`, ignored by every reader, Decision 5); G the gap record of the dataset and day; A the alert; B the restart blocker; T whether the phase tries the key again. Mode `resolve` (`option_eod`, `option_eod_jp`, `quote_snapshot`). Main cites are of 484dbc41 (unchanged on a67a8a7f); each cell names the test that proves it (entry 5 unless marked). The first table is by the state the key closed in and what the late run brings; the second is by how the run ends, which acts the same on every state because no reader counts the row.

| State the key closed in | (a) late run gets usable rows | (b) late run gets nothing |
|---|---|---|
| missing with an `expired` gap (`closeRow` rows 3 and 4, `reconcile.go:383-410`; kind `expired`, `gaps.go:176-182`) | R done, mark `present` (`runkey.go:203-228`); G cleared at the next sync of its day if no other key of the dataset wants one (`TestLateExpiredKeyUsableClearsAndEmptyKeepsGap`, `TestTickerSyncsOpenGapDayAfterLateSuccess`); A none new; B none; T no | R done, mark stays `no_value`; G stays `expired`, not rewritten (`apply` writes only a changed kind); A none (sent at the close); B none; T after 6 h (same first test) |
| `partial` (`closeRow` row 2, latest run partial; kind `partial`, `runnerKind`, `gaps.go`) | present, G cleared (`TestLatePartialKeepsKindOnEmptyAndClearsOnUsable`); A, B none; T no | G stays `partial`, not turned into `expired` (the late row is ignored, `keyKind` `gaps.go:157-196`); A, B none; T after 6 h (same test) |
| empty or `no_value` (verdict `NoValue`, reason `late`, mark `no_value`; no gap on main: `expired` false, `gaps.go:176-177`) | present; there was no gap and there is none; A, B none; T no (`TestLateNoValueKeyHasNoGapBeforeOrAfter`) | stays `no_value`; no gap is made (a late row is ignored, `expired` stays false); A, B none; T after 6 h, one slot in six hours for a permanent empty condition (`TestLateEmptyKeyWaitsSixHours`) |
| `crashed_repeatedly` (`closeRow` row 1, three crashes, `reconcile.go:383-410`; kind by `proof`, `gaps.go:157-196`) | present (the phase calls `r.run`, so the brake of `nextAttempt`, `reconcile.go:344-378`, does not stop it); G cleared (a present key has none, top of `keyKind`; `TestLateCrashedRepeatedKeepsKindOnEmptyAndClearsOnUsable`); A, B none; T no | G stays `crashed_repeatedly` (the crash rows are the proof); A, B none; T after 6 h |
| `abandoned_stop` below the brake at the close (`closeRow` finds no row; the walk runs the key once more, `reconcile.go:344-378`) | the run after the close has ended the key as the empty row or as `crashed_repeatedly`; the late run is then that row's cell (`TestLateAbandonedStopKeyFollowsNoValueOrCrashedRow`) | same |
| present at the close, or marked `present` by an earlier late run | not picked (the phase takes only `no_value` keys); no run, no gap change (`TestLatePhaseSkipsUnmarkedKey` covers the picks) | not applicable |
| unmarked, the close not yet written (a key the walk has not closed: its row is written by `expireIfClosed`, `reconcile.go:412-437`) | not picked; the normal walk closes it first, and it is picked on a later pass (`TestLatePhaseSkipsUnmarkedKey`) | not applicable |
