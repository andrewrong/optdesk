## What you see

A page "P&L split" under Options, Held options. Per account and per currency (currencies are never added) one book card, then one card per lot: every short put that is open or ended in the last 30 days. A lot the page cannot split is still a card, with the reason in words, and the book card says it left that lot out. Numbers are invented here and add up the way the code adds them (`Difference` = ledger minus total minus assignment intrinsic).

## Today

- The attribution rule exists as a pure function with a pricer passed in: `optionattr.New(p optionattrapi.Pricer) optionattrapi.Attributor`, input `Input{Lot, Quotes, Rates, LedgerPnL, LedgerMarkDay, MaxRateAgeDays}`, output `Result` (`internal/compute/optionattrapi/api.go`). Nothing displays it. It sums per lot only; there is no sum across lots.
- `Attribute` returns `(Result{}, err)` with `ErrBadInput` when `LedgerPnL` is not a number or, for a lot that ended expired, assigned or exercised, when `EndClose` is not a number (`internal/compute/optionattr/attr.go:113-117`, `Attribute` at `attr.go:243-246`). It has no `Reason` for these: they are errors. A lot with no quote day at all is not an error: `Result.NoQuotes` with `Reason` (`attr.go:249-252`).
- `Result.Difference` is `LedgerPnL - (Entry + Exit + the sum of Steps + Commission + AssignedIntrinsic)` rounded half-even to 4 places (`attr.go:304-305`); it is empty for an open lot whose `LedgerMarkDay` differs from the last quote day (`attr.go:296-302`, with `Reason`). `AssignedIntrinsic` is positive, the intrinsic value of the strike against `EndClose` times the open quantity times the multiplier, zero unless the lot ended assigned or exercised (`attr.go:158-163`).
- Stored quotes: `optionday.DayReader.Days(ctx, ids, from, to)` returns `Day` rows with `Bid`, `Ask`, `UnderlyingPrice` (the underlying of the same row), `Iv`, `Delta`, `Gamma`, `ThetaPerCalendarDay`, `VegaPerVolPoint` as text pointers (`internal/store/optionday/api.go`).

An open lot has `UnrealizedPnL` only when the replay got a mark: `LedgerInput.Marks []LedgerMark{Contract, Day, Bid, Ask, UnderlyingClose}` is read by `newMarkSet` and the position is `MarkStatus` `no_mark` with `UnrealizedPnL` empty otherwise; the replay takes the newest mark of the contract, uses its mid, and drops a mark older than `MarkMaxAgeSessions` session days (`internal/compute/optionledger/premium.go:58-125, 165-176`). Every production `compute.LedgerInput{...}` literal omits `Marks` (`option_tracking_page/context.go:71` and the others), so today no open lot gets a mark from the replay.

## Decisions

1. **One new page, name `option_pnl_page`, route `options/pnl`, order 35** (between `option_tracking_page` 30 and `option_page` 40 in `cmd/ot/pagereg_test.go`). Schema `page.option_pnl_page.v1`, Go package `internal/pages/option_pnl_page`, web dir `web/src/pages/optionpnl`.
2. **Lots shown.** Short puts (`Right` P) of the alias that are open, or ended with `ClosedOn` within 30 days before the page day (trial, `RecentDays`, as the tracking page). Calls are not shown; the page says how many were skipped. Older lots are not shown (Left out).
3. **Pages sums nothing.** The pages builder maps ledger and store rows to `optionattrapi.Input`, calls the `Attributor` it is given, calls a `Booker` (Decision 4) for every sum and the "rent minus move cost" line, and formats text. It has no sum, difference, product or quotient of figures (rounding for display only) and no `optionattr` or `bsm` import: `Config.Attributor` and `Config.Booker` are the interfaces, `cmd/ot` passes `optionattr.New(bsm.New())` and `optionattr.NewBooker()`. Two things are selections, not arithmetic, and are named: the mark rule of Decision 6 (a sign test of two texts) and counting lots.
4. **Sums across lots are a compute rule, so they get an interface first.** `optionattrapi` gains `Book` and `Booker`: `Combine(rs []Result) []Book`, one `Book` per currency in order of first appearance. A result with `NoQuotes`, or with an empty `Difference` (an open lot whose ledger mark day differs from its last quote day), is counted in `NoQuoteLots` or `UnreconciledLots` and adds nothing to any sum field of the book (`Entry`, `Exit`, `Commission`, `Direction`, `MoveCost`, `IVRemark`, `Time`, `Unsplit`, `AssignedIntrinsic`, `LedgerPnL`, `Difference`, `RentMinusCost`, `Total`; their `LedgerPnL` goes to `NoQuoteLedgerPnL` or `UnreconciledLedgerPnL`), so the identity `LedgerPnL` = `Total` + `AssignedIntrinsic` + `Difference` holds for the lots that were added. `RentMinusCost` = `Time` + `MoveCost` (seller amounts: `MoveCost` is negative). `Total` = `Entry` + `Exit` + `Direction` + `MoveCost` + `IVRemark` + `Time` + `Unsplit` + `Commission` (the option side; `AssignedIntrinsic` is its own field and is not in `Total`, as `Result.Difference` adds it separately). An empty `Exit` counts as zero. Sums are exact decimal addition, no rounding; the page rounds for display. A single lot's card uses `Combine` of one result, so one rule makes both. A lot whose attribution failed (Decision 7) is never given to `Combine`.
5. **Quote day is the ledger rule, applied by `optionattr`, not by the page.** The page passes every stored row of the contract between the open day and the page day as a `Quote` (no filter); the attributor treats a row as a quote day only with ask above 0, bid at or above 0 and an underlying price (`internal/compute/optionledger/premium.go:94-113`; `option-attribution.md` Decision 8). The page's "quote days" and "gap days" come from `Result.Steps` and `Result.GapDays`. The coverage column of the tracking page counts a two-sided quote as bid above 0, so its counts can differ by the bid-0 rows; the page says "quote day as the ledger counts it". The one rule has two choosers because the mark is needed before `Attribute` runs (its `LedgerPnL` needs the mark): `marks.go` picks the mark row, `Result.MarkDay` is the attributor's last quote day, and a rule of three conditions (ask above 0, bid at or above 0, underlying price above 0, rows after the lot's expiry or end ignored, `quoteDays` at `attr.go:166-190`) is in both. Cases of the table: d1 ok row; d2 later row with underlying price 0; d3 later row with ask 0; d4 row after the expiry. Both choose d1: `TestMarkDayFollowsPageRule` (compute, real attributor, entry 2) and `TestMarkIsLatestRulePassingRow` (pages, entry 3) use these four rows.
6. **The mark of an open lot, and the ledger figure.** An open lot needs `LedgerPnL`, so the replay needs a mark. The source is the latest stored `option_day` row of the contract that is a quote day by the ledger rule of Decision 5 (ask above 0, bid at or above 0, underlying price above 0, rows after the lot's expiry or end ignored), read for the same instrument ids and range as the quotes (no second read). The builder replays twice per alias: first without marks, to learn the positions and their contracts (`Contract` a conid; a composite name has no instrument id and so no mark); then, with `LedgerInput.Marks`, one `LedgerMark{Contract: position.Contract, Day: row.SessionDay, Bid: row.Bid, Ask: row.Ask, UnderlyingClose: row.UnderlyingPrice}` per open lot from its latest rule-passing row, the replay giving the mid and its age (`Config.MarkMaxAgeSessions` = 3, the tracking page's value, with `Config.Sessions` and `Config.CalendarID` for the session days). `LedgerPnL` = `UnrealizedPnL` and `LedgerMarkDay` = `MarkDay` while open. Because the mark is our own stored mid of the last quote day, `LedgerMarkDay` equals the attributor's last quote day, and `Difference` is set; for an open lot the check is of the fills, the commissions and the charge against the mid, not of the market value (the card says so). The tracking page's broker mark is a different figure and stays on that page. An open lot with no rule-passing row, or one older than 3 sessions, has `MarkStatus` `no_mark`: it is a lot card with the reason of Decision 7 (never a `NoQuotes` lot, Decision 8).
7. **A lot that cannot be attributed is a card, never a failed page.** `lots.go` decides the reason before calling `Attribute`: an open lot with `MarkStatus` `no_mark` ("no stored quote of AAA within 3 sessions: no split"); an ended lot (expired, assigned, exercised) with no stored `trades` close on its `ClosedOn` ("no stored close of AAA on 2026-11-13: no split"; an expired lot needs the close too, because `Attribute` parses it for every ended lot); an empty `RealizedPnL` of an ended lot. If `Attribute` still returns an error that is `ErrBadInput`, the card says "input refused: <text>" and the page goes on; any other error is returned by `Build`. The lot is a `Lot` with `gap` set and no lines. Other lots render as usual. The book card is built from the lots that were attributed and gets one line "n of m lots left out of the sums: <count per reason>"; the page counts the lots, the `Booker` sums nothing it was not given. A lot with `NoQuotes` (an ended lot only, Decision 8) is not an error: its card shows the ledger figures and "no stored quotes: no split" and the `Booker` counts it in `NoQuoteLots`. `left_out` names every class left out, each with its lot count and its ledger P&L: gap lots (the page lists each lot's ledger figure as stored text, adds nothing; an empty `RealizedPnL` reads "ledger P&L not stored"), `NoQuoteLots` and `UnreconciledLots` (the ledger P&L of each class is a `Book` field, summed in compute, Interface). Card fields: a `NoQuotes` card shows ledger P&L, commission, the reason and the days line, and no split line at all (never `0.00`); an unreconciled open lot's card shows its split lines, ledger P&L, commission, the reason of `Result.Reason` and the days line, and in place of the difference line the reason text, never `0.00`.
8. **Quote table.** `obs.option_day` only. `NoQuotes` applies to ended lots only: an ended lot opened before the table's rows exist, or with no row, is `NoQuotes` (the card shows the ledger figures and "no stored quotes: no split", nothing estimated). An open lot with no rule-passing row is the `no_mark` gap lot of Decision 7, never `NoQuotes`; the goldens follow this one rule (`no_quotes.golden.json` an ended lot, `lot_gap.golden.json` the open `no_mark` lot). No `option_eod` fallback (Left out).
9. **Rates and age.** One `DGS3MO` series for every currency (trial: no yen or other rate is stored), read through the `SeriesReader` argument of `Build` for 14 days before the oldest lot's open day up to the page day, passed as `Rate{Day, Percent}`. `Input.MaxRateAgeDays` = 7: `cmd/ot` passes a named constant `pnlMaxRateAgeDays = 7`, the default of the settings key `max_rate_age_days` (`internal/compute/ruleparam/registry.go:32`); the page does not read the setting (dev mode, one source named in the code comment; a changed setting is ignored, named in Left out). With no `Rate` in range the attributor puts the step in `Unsplit` and lists `NoRate`; the card names the day.
10. **365 days.** The year basis is the attributor's own constant `daysInYear = 365` (`optionattr/attr.go:17`); the page passes no year length and states "model: European Black-Scholes, 365-day year, no dividends; an ex-dividend drop lands in the solved IV; the early-exercise premium is not modelled" once in `notes`.
11. **IBKR figures are comparison only.** The card shows the IBKR implied volatility of the last stored row (`Result.Steps[last].RefIVTo`) beside ours. A `Ref` value that is empty (Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega, IV) is a gap cell "not stored", never `0.00`; this step displays only IV, the other four stay in `Quote.Ref` for later. No IBKR figure enters any sum.
12. **End-day close.** For expired, assigned and exercised lots `Lot.EndClose` is the stored `trades` close of the underlying on `ClosedOn` (the ids from `Config.StockIDs`, then `PriceBars` with `from` = `to` = that day). With no bar the page does not call `Attribute` for that lot: Decision 7 gives the card its reason (`Attribute` would return `ErrBadInput` for an empty `EndClose`, `attr.go:113-117`).
13. **Three reconciliation lines per lot, none hidden** (plan, 与券商盈亏对不上时). Ours versus ledger: `Result.Difference`, with `Result.Reason` when it is empty. Ledger realised versus broker realised: the two stored texts side by side, "Ledger $120.00, broker $118.40, not explained", and the word "differs" only when the texts differ; the page subtracts nothing. Layer 3 (IBKR unrealised against our mid) is not done (Left out). An assigned lot's "Ledger says" line reads total + assignment intrinsic = ledger.
14. **Badges** (text on a lot, from `Result` fields only): `2-day step` (`Step.MultiDay`), `wide quote` (`Step.Wide`), `late entry` (`Result.LateEntry`), `not split: n step(s)` (`UnsplitSteps`), `no quotes` (`NoQuotes`), and `looks like a roll` (same alias, underlying and day, one lot closed and one opened; an equality test on ledger text, nothing else).
15. **Sample sentence.** The number of ended lots (status not open) is counted by the pages builder; under 20 (trial, plan appendix A) the book card says "fewer than 20 ended lots: no conclusion about what pays".
16. **Money and percent text** (pages formats, web shows): `$` for USD with thousands separators, two decimals rounded half away from zero, minus as U+2212 (`−$186.00`); another currency is written `JPY 1,234.50`; percent one decimal (`28.6%`); implied volatility is a percent. Every jargon label has a `hint` written next to it (the gloss in the sample above).
17. **No migration, no write, no clock in compute.** Gaps use the page-schema `Gap` of `common.v2` as `option_tracking_page` does: `no_reader` for a nil reader, `no_flex_rows`, `no_sessions`, `no_quote_reader` (a nil `Days`).
18. **ot wiring.** `pnlReadersOf(rd)` wraps `rd.optionLedger` in the existing `aliasedLedger` (same package, not copied) when it is not nil, as `trackingReadersOf` does. The page has a `named` function, `pnlPageNamed`, built like `trackingPageNamed` (nil zone is an error before any store opens; the demo profile and a missing pages line give a page with empty readers, shown as gaps; otherwise `openSiteReaders`). `registerPage` takes `named: pnlPageNamed` because `TestPageRegistryRoutes` requires `(f.named == nil) == (w.name == "")` (`cmd/ot/pagereg_test.go:41-44`). `Config.CalendarID` is "XNYS", as `newOptionTrackingPage`. `pnlReadersOf`/`buildPnl` and `pnlPageNamed` pass `rd.series` as the `SeriesReader` of `Build` (the tracking page passes nil; this page reads the rates through it, Decision 9), and put the `StockIDs` reader of `siteReaderOf[market_state_page.StockIDReader](rd, siteMarketStateIDsKey)` into `Config.StockIDs`.
