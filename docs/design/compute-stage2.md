## Goal

By the owner's word of 2026-11-10 (Decision 2) the pages use IBKR's values in the early phase and the design's job is to make sure they are all captured and stored, with a gap reason where IBKR returns none: (a) the delta, implied volatility and the other model values of every contract of the watched chains, including the strikes near the spot that the implied-volatility figure needs (entry 5), as `obs.option_eod` rows that optdesk already stores; (b) the IV history that IVP needs: a one-time IBKR bootstrap of one year of daily implied-volatility values per underlying, stored as readings `market.iv_ibkr` (entries 1 to 4 and 6 to 8), so that the IVP formula of `ivp_v1` runs on it today (entry 9) and a page shows `market.ivp_own` when its own window has 252 valid sessions and the IBKR-based `market.ivp_ibkr` before that, labeled by source. HV30 and the own IVP keep their rules and code as on main. Nothing here prints on a page: the builders read the stored rows and readings (`docs/design/builders-wave.md`).

## What main holds today

- HV30 is built. `Figures.HistoricalVol` (`internal/compute/figures/series.go:13`) is the sample stdev of the last 30 log returns times the square root of 252; the rule `market.hv30_own` v1 is in `internal/compute/rules/rules.json:2`; the step `runHv30` (`internal/jobs/computefigures/hv.go:21`) reads the winner `trades` closes and stores one reading per underlying. It runs only when the job has a bar reader (`hvOn`, `hv.go:17`); `cmd/ot/wire.go:560` sets it when the bars store opens and logs "HV step left out" otherwise (`wire.go:562`).
- IVP is built. `IvPercentile` (`internal/compute/figures/ivp.go:48`) is the share of the past 252 valid IV30 sessions at or below today's (`AtOrBelow`, today excluded), `short_history` under 252 (rule `market.ivp_own`, `rules.json:4`: `window` 252, `min_valid` 252). The step `runIvStats` (`steps.go:149`) reads the stored `market.iv30_own` readings of the underlying. So IVP exists only after 252 sessions of ok IV30 readings. The owner's rule of 2026-11-08 (GLOSSARY.md:78) forbade any bootstrap from IBKR history; the owner's answer of 2026-11-10 (Decision 3) lifts it for the displayed value, as a separate series that is never mixed into the own window.
- IV30 needs the at-the-money strike. `Iv30` takes, per expiry, the strike with both legs and the smallest |call mid - put mid| (`iv30.go:67`, `strikeStar`). The watch capture takes strikes from `0.80 x BuyPrice` to `max(1.05 x BuyPrice, spot)` and, when more than `max_strikes` (24) fall in the range, the 24 nearest the buy price (`internal/jobs/optioneod/watch.go:139`, `select.go:35`, `job.toml:11`). A stock whose spot is far above its buy price and whose strikes are dense (a one-dollar step: strikes 88 to 111 for a buy price of 100 and a spot of 130, `watch_test.go:125`) is captured without a single strike near the spot, so its IV30 is missing and its IVP never starts.
- Delta has no producer of its own, and in the early phase needs none (Decision 4). `obs.option_eod.delta` is IBKR's model delta, null when IBKR gave none (`internal/contracts/gen/records.go:2099`, `docs/contracts/datasets/obs.option_eod.md:37`); `option_data_page` prints a null as the gap "no data" (`internal/pages/option_data_page/format.go`, `cell`).
- IBKR data optdesk already has or can get, read at origin/main. (a) The underlying's option implied volatility, generic tick 106, is declared (`quote.proto` field `option_implied_vol`; kind `QUOTE_KIND_UNDERLYING_IV` with ticks 104 and 106, `quote_types.proto`) and `broker.KindUnderlyingIV` exists, but no job uses it (`docs/design/option-jobs.md:166`): it is one point per request, no history. (b) The option's own model `implied_vol`, `delta` and the other Greeks are captured per contract into `obs.option_eod` already. (c) The `Bars` rpc returns daily bars for the kinds `BAR_KIND_TRADES`, `BAR_KIND_ADJUSTED_LAST` and `BAR_KIND_MIDPOINT` only (`pyside/src/optdesk/ibkr/history.py:27`, `transport.py:521`), 1 to 730 days, under IBKR's pacer of 55 requests per 10 minutes (`history.py:18`); a one-year IV series is therefore not available with the rpc as it is, and a new enum value is the smallest change that gets it. (d) No tick or field of main carries an IV rank or IV percentile of IBKR's. (e) `obs.price_bar` cannot hold an IV series: its `basis` check allows `trades` and `adjusted_last` only (`migrations/0021_bars.sql:24`).
- Readings are generic. `readings.reading` has `subject_kind text NOT NULL` with no CHECK (`migrations/0013_readings.sql:16`), so a kind other than `underlying` and `market` needs no migration.

## Decisions

1. HV30 and IVP keep their rules and their code (`hv30_v1`, `ivp_v1`; the owner chose option a, Decision 3). Their gap definitions, written here once so the builders use the same words: HV30 is missing with `no_input` when there is no close, `short_history` under 31 closes (`observations` = closes minus 1, at most 30), `stale_input` when the newest close is more than 4 calendar days old, `bad_input` when a close is not above zero (`HistoricalVol`, `series.go:13`). IVP is missing with `no_input` when the day has no ok IV30 reading, `short_history` under 252 valid prior sessions (`observations` = the count, 0 to 252), `stale_input` and `bad_input` as the core gives them (`ivWindow`, `ivp.go:12`). A missing figure is stored with `state = missing_input`, its `reason_code` (1 no_input, 2 short_history, 3 stale_input, 4 zero_range, 5 bad_input; `reasonCode`, `steps.go:199`) and never a zero.
2. The early phase uses IBKR's values. The owner, 2026-11-10 10:08Z, verbatim: "如果ibkr来源很准的话，我觉得前期就用ibkr，你只要保证都能采集回来" (if IBKR's source is accurate, use IBKR early on; the designer only has to make sure everything is collected). So the pages show, labeled source IBKR: the delta, implied volatility and other model values of each contract (the stored `obs.option_eod` columns), and, for IVP, the IBKR-based value of Decision 12 until the own window is full. The work of this design is the completeness of the capture and the history, not new figures of our own.
3. The owner's other answers of 2026-11-10, said in the dispatcher window. On IVP depth, verbatim: "a, 但是ibkr不是有吗，在他还没真正有数据之前，用现成的" (option a: `ivp_v1` keeps 252 valid sessions; until our own IV history is enough, show IBKR's ready-made value). Confirmation, verbatim: "对，表明来源即可" (the delta rule and the IVP fallback are the owner's word; only the source label is required). The delta rule, the owner's: our own delta where it is computed, IBKR's delta otherwise, source labeled; as no own delta exists yet (Decision 4) a page shows IBKR's delta, labeled IBKR, and the rule applies unchanged when an own delta is designed later. The IVP rule, the owner's: `market.ivp_own` when its reading is ok (its window has 252 valid sessions); otherwise `market.ivp_ibkr` (Decision 12) when that is ok; otherwise the gap with the reason of `market.ivp_own`. The label is the name of the reading the page used: `market.ivp_own` is labeled 自算, `market.ivp_ibkr` IBKR 序列, the delta of `obs.option_eod` IBKR. The two IVP series are never mixed in one window (the rule of GLOSSARY.md:78 that two IV series are not mixed stays; its sentence "no bootstrap" is superseded by this answer and entry 6 rewrites it).
4. Our own delta (Black-Scholes, one reading per contract) leaves this design for a later step, because the owner chose IBKR for the early phase; own figures come later, for reconciliation (GLOSSARY.md:61-64 is the pattern of HV30). It is not needed to fill IBKR's nulls: a row's implied volatility, delta, gamma, vega, theta and underlying price are one group `_MODEL` that the capture releases and waits for together (`pyside/src/optdesk/ibkr/spec.py`, `_MODEL` and `release_fields`), so a row without delta most often lacks the volatility too, and a Black-Scholes delta needs that volatility; no count of how often IBKR returns a null delta exists in code or tests (a partial quote is stored as status 1 with its null columns, `docs/design/option-jobs.md:87`), so the window measures it (Decision 6). The earlier text of this design for it (`option.delta_own`, `optiondelta`, entries on the interface `DeltaCalc`, the step and the wire) is in the history of pull request 1248, not in this file.
5. Complete capture. (a) The strikes the volatility needs: the capture keeps, besides the buy-price range, the two strikes below and the two at or above the spot of each expiry (`nearestStrikes(chain.Strikes, price, band, 2)`, the function that already serves the no-buy-price case, `select.go:67`), both rights, deduplicated: at most four more strikes per expiry. It gives IV30 its strike pair whenever the chain has strikes within the 10 percent band, which the own IVP needs; it does not shorten the wait of the own IVP (252 sessions after its first ok IV30). (b) The model values: the release of an option quote waits for them for at most 20 s and a quote that did not get them is stored with null columns (`spec.py` table, `OPTION_EOD`). This design changes no timing: a longer wait is a measured change of pyside, to be designed when the window's count (Decision 6) shows a null share worth it. (c) The history: Decisions 9 to 12.
6. The window's coverage check, read-only, run after each capture and not in CI: the share of the day's stored rows without a delta or without an implied volatility, by session day, with `select session_day, count(*) filter (where status = 1) as ok_rows, count(*) filter (where status = 1 and delta is null) as null_delta, count(*) filter (where status = 1 and iv_decimal is null) as null_iv from rd.option_eod_r1 group by session_day order by session_day desc limit 10;` (columns of `migrations/0006_option_eod.sql`; the view is `rd.option_eod_r1`). Its result decides whether (b) of Decision 5 and the own delta of Decision 4 are worth designing.
7. Gap texts of the early phase, which the builders print next to the figure name (this design owns the words, the builders the rendering). HV30 `缺 · 无日线`, `缺 · 日线不足 N/30`, `缺 · 日线过旧`, `缺 · 输入无效`; IVP `缺 · 无今日 IV30`, `缺 · 历史 N/252`, `缺 · IV30 过旧`, `缺 · 输入无效`, and for the IBKR-based one `缺 · 无 IBKR IV 序列`, `缺 · IBKR 历史 N/252`; IBKR delta and IV of a contract `缺 · IBKR 未返回 delta`, `缺 · IBKR 未返回 IV`, and `缺 · IBKR 无数据` for a row whose status is not 1; `N` is the stored `observations` parameter. A null column of a status-1 row is the reason itself: the row says IBKR returned none.
8. How accurate IBKR's model values are. The `delta`, `gamma`, `vega`, `theta` and implied-volatility columns are IBKR's model values: IBKR computes them from its own option model and inputs and optdesk stores them as received (`quote.proto`: "implied_vol to theta are the option model values"); this design verifies none and makes no accuracy claim about them. They are model numbers, not trades; for a contract with a wide or stale bid and ask they can be far from what another model gives, and for American-style contracts the model includes the early-exercise value. The owner's condition "if IBKR's source is accurate" is therefore tested by reconciliation with an own figure later (Decision 4), not here.
9. The IV history source: IBKR daily bars of `OPTION_IMPLIED_VOLATILITY` through the existing `Bars` rpc, one request per underlying for 400 days at the first run (the service turns 400 days into the TWS duration "2 Y", `duration_text`, `history.py:44`, so about 500 daily bars arrive and all are stored), then 5 days a night. The own IVP formula then runs on it today. A bar's close is the IV as a decimal (0.28 is 28 percent) and is stored; open, high and low are not. This needs a new enum value `BAR_KIND_OPTION_IMPLIED_VOL = 4` in `marketdata.proto` and the words for it in `history.py` and `transport.py`: no new rpc and no new message, but a change of two owner-only paths and a second-opinion case (a new outside data request), so entries 2 and 3 carry that note and everything that uses the series (entries 4 and 7 to 9) is queued after them. This design does not know whether IBKR's series is a constant 30-day measure (IBKR's documentation is not in the repository); the readings are named `market.iv_ibkr`, not IV30, and a page prints "IBKR IV", never "IV30", for them. The first run on live data checks the series of a name against its stored tick 106 value before the owner relies on the percentile; that check is the window's, not a test.
10. Fallback that needs no new rpc: the existing kind `UNDERLYING_IV` (ticks 104 and 106, `option_implied_vol`) captured once a night per underlying gives IBKR's IV of the day with no history. It would show an IBKR IV value from tomorrow but no IVP for 252 sessions, which is not what the owner asked; it is not an entry and is listed in Left out. Only if the owner or the second opinion refuses entries 2 and 3 does this fallback replace entries 4 and 7 to 9; a new design then.
11. Storage and job: the reading `market.iv_ibkr` v1 (convention `ibkr_iv_bar_v1`, unit `decimal`, scale 6, half-even), `subject_kind = underlying`, `subject` the underlying name, `day` the bar's day, `origin = ibkr`, `value` the bar's close as decimal text; one input row, the table name `ibkr.bars` keyed `<name>|<day>`. A new job `iv_history` (package `internal/jobs/ivhistory`) writes it with `store.ReadingWriter` (`InsertReadings`, idempotent on the key, `internal/store/api.go:154`): the first run of a name asks 400 days (no `market.iv_ibkr` reading of the name in the last 365 days), later runs 5 days, the shape of `bars_refresh` (`backfill_days` 400, `daily_days` 5, `barsrefresh/job.toml`). It takes the names and contracts the way `bars_refresh` does and plans one key per name, `market.iv_ibkr|underlying|<name>`; the Postcondition (a row for the as-of day, ok or missing) is met by storing a missing reading `no_input` when the bar of the as-of day did not arrive. A close that is not above 0 or above 10 is stored missing `bad_input`. The close is turned into the stored text by a function the job receives from `cmd/ot` (`ivbar.FromClose` of entry 6), so the job imports no compute implementation package. Proposed slot 10 minutes after the session close, before `compute_figures` (20 minutes); the owner confirms, as for the other slots. IBKR's pacer: one request per name per night and 55 per 10 minutes mean a long watch list needs more than one pacer window the first night; a paced answer is retried by the job runner, and the figure stays a gap until the series is complete (no partial percentile: `short_history` under 252).
12. IVP on the IBKR series: the rule `market.ivp_ibkr` v1 (convention `ivp_v1`, unit `decimal`, scale 6, `compare` at_or_below, `window` 252, `min_valid` 252, `max_age_days` 4), the same formula and gaps as Decision 1 (`IvPercentile`, `ivp.go:48`) on the stored `market.iv_ibkr` readings. `runIvStats` reads the series named by the rule (own IV30 for `market.ivp_own`, `market.iv_ibkr` for `market.ivp_ibkr`); the planned key is `market.ivp_ibkr|underlying|<name>` and the IVR is not computed on IBKR's series. Both IVP readings are stored every night side by side; neither reads the other; the precedence of Decision 3 is applied where the page reads them. A name's IBKR-based IVP is ok from the first night after its bootstrap (about 500 bars are above 252 sessions) and stays computed after its own IVP starts, so the window can compare the two; the page shows the own one from then on.
13. No new dependency and no migration. `Contracts: unchanged` in every pull request except entry 2, whose proto change is stated in its own `Contracts:` line. No edit of `internal/jobs/api.go` or `internal/compute/api.go` (`docs/design/jobs-api-slim.md` Decision 1; the compute entry file has a ceiling in `docs/design/ratchet.json` that an agent never raises): the job's function field and the readings use ordinary files of the packages, wired by `cmd/ot` as `cmd/ot` already wires `internal/compute/figures`.
