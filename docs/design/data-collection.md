# data collection: SEC facts carry their fiscal period, single quarters are derived from year-to-date values, a company page shows the quarterly table, and a data page shows per company what is stored and what is missing

Author: the optdesk designer
Owner approved: (pending)
Date: 2026-11-17

Source: the owner-approved plan of 2026-11-17 ("optdesk 估值系统：底层数据结构与分步计划", section "第二件：底层数据采集", steps 2.2, 2.3, 2.4; step 2.1 merged as PR 2586, step 2.5 is `docs/design/data-health.md`). Left out by the owner's word: JP statements, quarters of IFRS filers, HKD and CNY rates, the 5-year bar deepen, consensus, sector and country. Principles: the front end shows data and computes nothing; a derived number is not stored (free cash flow is computed at display); a quarter that cannot be derived shows as missing, never a wrong number; a technical word is explained next to it. Every fact below was read on origin/main `84a13216` (after `git fetch`, 2026-11-17); nothing was run and no database was read.

Goal: (1) `obs.fundamental_fact` rows carry the SEC `fy`, `fp` and `frame`; (2) a pure compute package turns the stored facts into single quarters (the latest restatement wins, the older row stays), with a filing date; (3) a read-only page `company-quarters` shows the quarterly table of one company; (4) a page `company-data` has one row per company: what is stored, up to which day, and what is missing and why. Nothing is stored twice and nothing is computed in `web/` or in `internal/pages`.

## Decisions

1. **fy, fp and frame are stored as three columns of the fact; the key does not change.** Migration `0053_fundamental_fact_period.sql` (0052 is `0052_valuation.sql`, merged; owner-only DDL, Up and Down, no row touched): `fy integer` (NULL when the source has none), `fp text NOT NULL DEFAULT ''`, `frame text NOT NULL DEFAULT ''`, `GRANT SELECT (fy, fp, frame)` and `GRANT UPDATE (fy, fp, frame)` to `ot_w_fundamental` (that role has column-level SELECT on the 14 old columns only, `migrations/0046_fundamentals_keys.sql:204-205`, and the upsert's WHERE reads the columns it compares, `internal/store/fundamental/upsert.go:26-27`; without the SELECT grant every `fundamentals` run fails with "permission denied for column"; INSERT is table-level, `0017:38`, so it needs none), and a NEW view `rd.fundamental_fact_r2` = the 14 columns of `rd.fundamental_fact_r1` plus `fy`, `fp`, `frame`, owned by `ot_owner`, `GRANT SELECT` to `ot_ro_pages` and `ot_w_fundamental` (the two roles the r1 view is granted to, `0017_fundamental_fact.sql:39-40`), its Down dropping the r2 view. `rd.fundamental_fact_r1` is not renamed, dropped or widened: a dropped view loses its grants and owner, a widened one carries a name that lies, and a rename spans jobs, store and compute. `pepb`, `internal/jobs/computefigures/valuation.go` and `FactsFiledBy` stay on r1 untouched; only the new reader `PeriodFactReader.FactsWithPeriodFiledBy` (declared in `companydataapi`, implemented by `internal/store/fundamental`, used by the two pages) reads r2. `upsertFactSQL` adds the three columns to the insert and to the `IS DISTINCT FROM` list, so the next daily run fills the existing rows. The fact contract bumps `obs.fundamental_fact.r1` to `r2` (the version rule, `docs/design/week1.md:106`: an additive change after the first consumer bumps); r2 has the three optional properties (not in `required`); `fy` is `integer` or null, `fp` and `frame` are strings, empty when absent. The root Go type stays `FundamentalFact` with `Fy *int`, `Fp string`, `Frame string`, so Go consumers compile unchanged; the Go dataset id `factDatasetID = "obs.fundamental_fact"` (`internal/jobs/fundamentals/plan.go:26`) is unversioned and does not change (Doubts 1).
2. **What SEC `fy`/`fp` mean, and the rule built on it.** In `companyfacts` the `fy`/`fp` of a fact are those of the filing that reported it, not of the period of the fact: a comparative figure in a later filing carries that filing's `fy`/`fp`. So they are used only on the facts of the original filing of a period (the earliest `filed` of the facts with that `period_end`): its `fy` is the fiscal year of the label and its `fp` (`Q1`, `Q2`, `Q3`, `FY`) must agree with the quarter number the period lengths give (Decision 3); if they disagree, that fiscal year's quarters are all missing with the reason `period_mismatch`; an empty `fp` means "not yet re-fetched" (Decision 13) and skips the check: the quarter number comes from the period lengths and the label stays "" when `fy` is nil (a 52 or 53 week year or an odd filer is never guessed). `frame` is stored and shown in the contract notes only; no rule reads it (Doubts 3).
3. **Single quarters are computed from the stored facts at read time, by one pure function, with the bands and the restatement pick of `internal/compute/pepb/ttm.go`.** `ttm.go` already picks the row filed last per (start, end) (`latestByPeriod`, `:77-91`) and accepts a year of 364 to 371 days (`:102-105`). It is not imported: a sub-package of compute may import the root and `shared` only, not a sibling (`docs/design/modules.md`, depends paragraph), and its helpers are unexported; moving them to a `compute/shared` package would edit `pepb`, which is not this design's module work. So `companydata` repeats the two rules with the same numbers (the year band 364 to 371 and latest-filed-per-period, ties by the larger `doc_id`), and `TestYearBandIs364To371` (entry 5) pins the constants and names `ttm.go:102-105` in its failure message; a later move of both rules to `compute/shared` is Doubts 11. The two code paths are pinned to each other by one test in entry 8, which can import both (`TestPepbAndQuartersAgree`). Facts of one (taxonomy, concept, unit) are grouped by `period_start`. A duration is `3M` (84 to 98 days), `6M` (175 to 189), `9M` (266 to 280) or `12M` (364 to 371) days; the bands cover 52 and 53 week years. Single quarter n of a fiscal year (started on the common `period_start`): Q1 is the 3M fact; Q2 is the 3M fact that starts the day after the Q1 end, else 6M minus 3M; Q3 likewise from 9M minus 6M; Q4 is a 3M fact ending on the year end, else 12M minus 9M. Instant concepts (cash, long-term and short-term debt) take the instant fact whose date is the quarter end. The operands of one derived cell (the two year-to-date values, or the three quarters and the year of EPS) all come from ONE concept: the concept list of Decision 6 is tried in order until one concept has every operand, else `cannot_derive`; `Cell.Concept` names that concept. Operands are also subject to the restatement condition of Decision 4. Anything not found, or not in a band, is `missing` with a reason: `no_fact`, `cannot_derive`, `period_mismatch`, `share_basis_changed`. Flows (revenue, operating income, net income, operating cash flow, capex, buybacks, dividends) subtract exactly. Diluted EPS: Q1 to Q3 are the 3M facts; Q4 is the 3M fact when the 10-K tags one, else FY EPS minus (Q1 + Q2 + Q3) and is marked `Derived`, with the gloss "annual minus the first three quarters; may differ from the reported Q4 EPS by rounding or a change in the share count"; if any of the three quarters is missing, Q4 EPS is missing. Q4 EPS is derived only when the diluted share counts of Q1, Q2, Q3 (3M facts) and FY lie within one band of each other (largest over smallest at most 1.10, a trial bound, Doubts 4), else it is missing with `share_basis_changed` (a split after Q1 puts FY on the new basis and Q1 on the old one until the comparatives are restated); a missing share fact among the four also gives `cannot_derive`. Diluted shares are a weighted average and are not subtracted: Q4 shares are shown only when a 3M fact is tagged, else missing (Doubts 4).
4. **The latest restatement wins, the old row stays.** Among facts with the same (concept, unit, period_start, period_end) the pick is the one with the largest `filed`, ties by the larger `doc_id`; the other rows are untouched in the table. A cell whose group holds two different values has `restated` true, so the page says "restated" beside it. A derived value takes `restated` true when either operand is. One condition keeps a subtraction on one basis: when an operand's chosen value differs from its first-filed value, every other operand of that cell must be chosen from a filing filed on or after that restating filing, else the cell is `cannot_derive` (12M original minus a restated 9M, 12M restated minus an original 9M, and 6M minus a restated 3M all give the gap).
5. **Past report date = SEC `filed`, labelled "Filing date".** The date of a quarter row is the earliest `filed` of the facts whose `period_end` is the quarter end (the original filing; for a derived Q4 the filing of the year's 10-K). The column header is "Filing date" with the gloss "the day the report was filed with the SEC; usually a few days after the earnings release; your own report-day record is not changed". The owner's report date (`report_date_override`) is not touched.
6. **Which concept fills a column** (fixed order; for a derived cell the first concept that has all operands, Decision 3; for a plain cell the first that has a value for that quarter; the page shows the concept name on hover/in the row detail): revenue `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax`, `Revenues`, `SalesRevenueNet`, `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerIncludingAssessedTax`, `SalesRevenueGoodsNet`; operating income `OperatingIncomeLoss`; net income `NetIncomeLoss`; diluted EPS `EarningsPerShareDiluted`; diluted shares `WeightedAverageNumberOfDilutedSharesOutstanding`, then `WeightedAverageNumberOfShareOutstandingBasicAndDiluted`; operating cash flow `NetCashProvidedByUsedInOperatingActivities`, then `...ContinuingOperations`; capex `PaymentsToAcquirePropertyPlantAndEquipment`, `PaymentsToAcquireProductiveAssets`, `PaymentsToAcquireOtherPropertyPlantAndEquipment`, `PaymentsForCapitalImprovements` (every concept named here is in the keep-list of `internal/sources/sec/mapping.go:18-56` on main, checked; entry 3 adds none); buybacks `PaymentsForRepurchaseOfCommonStock`; dividends `PaymentsOfDividendsCommonStock`, then `PaymentsOfDividends`; cash `CashAndCashEquivalentsAtCarryingValue`; long-term debt `LongTermDebtNoncurrent`, then `LongTermDebt`; short-term debt `DebtCurrent`, then `LongTermDebtCurrent`. Debt is two columns, not one: a company with no short-term debt tags nothing, and a sum would be either a wrong number or always missing (Doubts 5).
7. **Free cash flow is computed in Go at display, in compute.** `Calculator.Quarters` (module compute, package `internal/compute/companydata`) returns `FreeCash` = operating cash flow minus capex as exact decimal text (`shopspring/decimal`, no rounding), `missing` when either is missing; its `Concept` is the two concept names joined by " - ". The page builder calls it per request; the value is stored nowhere and the web prints it.
8. **Two pages, both read-only, no form, no repair button.** `company-quarters` (`page.company_quarters_page.v1`): query `?c=<name_key>` (for example `US:AAPL`, the `Key` of `settings.underlying`); without or with an unknown key it shows the list of companies as links and one gap line. `company-data` (`page.company_data_page.v1`): one row per row of `underlying.Reader.Current` with `Kind` stock and `Status` not retired. Money and numbers are exact text with thousands separators and the typographic minus, built by the page builder from the decimal text (string grouping only; no rounding, no arithmetic), as the household premium note does. A company page in the plan's words ("公司页") is this page: the companies page `companies_page.v2` is not changed (Doubts 6).
9. **The data page's columns** (one row per company): fiscal year end (the month, from the 12-month fact of the plan's step 2.2: "财年截止月由 2.2 带出"; also printed in the header of the quarterly table), price up to which day (the newest bar with status `ok` of basis `trades`, from 400 days back), number of daily bars with status `ok` in that range, statements yes or no, latest quarter (label and quarter end), number of quarters that have a diluted EPS, next report date (the newest `listed` row of `EarningsDates` on or after today), and "what is missing and why". The count of bars, statements, latest quarter, EPS quarters and the reasons come from one compute call, `Calculator.Summarize`, so the pages package counts nothing.
10. **Reasons are a closed list with a sentence each** (the builder prints the sentence given by compute): `jp` (market JP: no statements source; left out on purpose), `no_cik` (US, no `cik:` issuer: type the SEC number on the underlyings page), `fundamentals_off` (the flag is off), `no_statements` (US, CIK, flag on, no `us-gaap` fact stored: "either an IFRS filer, whose quarters are left out on purpose, or the daily run has not stored it yet"), `annual_only` (stored facts carry the forms 20-F or 40-F and no 10-Q: the filer reports once a year, no quarters), `no_instrument` (no `con_id` yet: no bars can be collected), `no_bars` (instrument but no bar), `no_next_report` (no listed report date; informational). The reasons reuse the vocabulary of the job `fundamentals` (`no_cik`; `edinet_not_fetched` is shown as `jp`).
11. **IFRS is not detected by a marker fact.** An `ifrs-full` document yields no stored row at all (`mapping.go:17-18`: no `ifrs-full` key in the keep-list), so today no stored data tells an IFRS filer from a name the daily run has not reached; a marker fact would add a permanent row per filing for a boolean. The page says what is known (`no_statements`, Decision 10). Where a US-GAAP foreign filer's 20-F or 40-F facts are stored (`internal/compute/valuation/params.go:93` already lists the annual forms 20-F and 40-F) the reason is `annual_only`. A company that is IFRS and has stored nothing therefore reads `no_statements`; the owner checks the three names of the acceptance by hand (Doubts 10).
12. **What reuses data health.** The page `company-data` shows one link to `/data-health` for "did the job run, repair a day"; it shows no ledger cell, because a ledger cell is counted per dataset and day and holds at most 20 keys (`rd.coverage_day_r1`). The name "coverage" is not used by any file of this design (checked by item 6 of entry 7). `coverage.WindowSessions` is not used: the bars window is the 400 days the job keeps (`barsrefresh/job.toml`).
13. **Dev mode and rebuild.** No time guard. After the migration and the code merge, the window runs `fundamentals` for today with `-force` once (console, any-day row with force) to fill `fy`, `fp` and `frame` at once, or waits for the next daily run; either fills the whole history because the job fetches the whole document.
14. **Versions are named.** The pages are `v1`; the fact contract bumps to r2 with three optional properties and the view `rd.fundamental_fact_r2` is new; any later change of a field of a page is `v2`.
15. **Migration.** Number `0053` (the last on origin/main is `0052_valuation.sql`, the migration of `docs/design/valuation-structure.md` entry 5, merged; if another migration takes `0053` first, the entry is cut with the next free number and this design is edited with it). The window deploys it with `setup pg`, `migrate`, `setup pg`; the code that writes the columns merges after it (Order lines). Entry 4 is the only migration and holds the auto-deploy.

## Interface

Entry 2 (module compute, new package `internal/compute/companydataapi`, `api.go` declarations only, `api_test.go` the contract from both sides: a fake `Calculator` and a caller). Numbers are decimal text, never a float; no clock, no I/O.

    // Why a cell has no value (five codes).
    type Why string
    const (
        WhyNone           Why = ""              // the cell has a value
        WhyNoFact         Why = "no_fact"       // no filing reported it
        WhyCannotDerive   Why = "cannot_derive" // a year-to-date operand or a quarter-length period is missing
        WhyPeriodMismatch Why = "period_mismatch" // fp of the filing disagrees with the periods; the fiscal year is not guessed
        WhyShareBasisChanged Why = "share_basis_changed" // diluted share counts of Q1 to Q3 and FY differ by more than one band (split)
    )

    // Cell is one figure of one quarter. Value is exact decimal text, empty when Missing.
    type Cell struct {
        Value    string
        Missing  bool
        Derived  bool    // year-to-date minus year-to-date, FY EPS minus three quarters, or operating cash flow minus capex
        Restated bool    // two filings gave different values for the period (Decision 4)
        Concept  string  // the concept the value was read from; empty when Missing
        Why      Why
    }

    // Quarter is one fiscal quarter of one issuer, newest first in the answer.
    type Quarter struct {
        Label          string         // "FY2025 Q3" from fy and fp of the original filing; "" when fy is unknown
        FiscalYear     int            // 0 when unknown
        FiscalYearEndMonth int        // 1 to 12, from the 12-month fact; 0 when unknown
        Number         int            // 1 to 4
        PeriodEnd      contracts.Day  // the quarter end
        FilingDate     contracts.Day  // Decision 5
        Revenue, OperatingIncome, NetIncome, EPSDiluted, SharesDiluted Cell
        OperatingCashFlow, Capex, FreeCash, Buybacks, Dividends        Cell
        Cash, LongTermDebt, ShortTermDebt                              Cell
    }

    // Reason is a code of "what is missing and why" (Decision 10).
    type Reason string
    const (
        ReasonJP, ReasonNoCIK, ReasonFundamentalsOff, ReasonNoStatements, ReasonAnnualOnly,
        ReasonNoInstrument, ReasonNoBars, ReasonNoNextReport Reason = "jp", "no_cik", "fundamentals_off", "no_statements", "annual_only", "no_instrument", "no_bars", "no_next_report"
    )

    type SummaryInput struct {
        Market       string                     // "US" or "JP"
        Issuer       string                     // "cik:0000..." or "edinet:..." or ""
        Fundamentals bool
        HasInstrument bool                      // con_id resolved
        BarDays      []contracts.Day            // days of bars with status ok
        Facts        []contracts.FundamentalFact
        NextReport   *contracts.Day
    }

    type Summary struct {
        LastBarDay   *contracts.Day
        BarCount     int
        FiscalYearEndMonth int         // 0 when unknown
        Statements   bool            // at least one quarter with a revenue, net income or EPS value
        LatestLabel  string
        LatestEnd    *contracts.Day
        EPSQuarters  int             // quarters with an EPS value
        Missing      []Missing
    }
    type Missing struct { Reason Reason; Text string }  // Text is the one-sentence explanation the page prints

    // PeriodFactReader reads the facts with fy, fp and frame (the view rd.fundamental_fact_r2); FactsFiledBy stays on r1.
    type PeriodFactReader interface {
        FactsWithPeriodFiledBy(ctx context.Context, source, subject string, cutoff contracts.Day) ([]contracts.FundamentalFact, error)
    }

    // Calculator is the only place that derives these numbers.
    type Calculator interface {
        Quarters(facts []contracts.FundamentalFact) ([]Quarter, error)
        Summarize(in SummaryInput) (Summary, error)
    }

## Doubts

1. The fact contract bumps r1 to r2 and `rd.fundamental_fact_r2` is a new view (advisor, 2026-11-17), not "additive within r1": the version rule (`docs/design/week1.md:106`) bumps an additive change after the first consumer, and a record with new fields under an old name misleads the next reader. The r1 view is not touched: dropping it loses its grants and owner, widening it gives a name that lies, renaming it spans jobs, store and compute. Cost: a new contract file, one new view and one new reader method; `FactsFiledBy`, `pepb` and `computefigures` stay on r1 and the Go type `FundamentalFact` keeps its name, so no existing reader changes.
2. Mixed restatement state is closed by the condition of Decision 4 (an operand that differs from its first-filed value needs every other operand from a filing filed on or after it, else `cannot_derive`); the cost is more gaps in the quarter after a restatement, until the comparatives are filed.
3. `frame` is stored and not used. It could pick the one fact SEC assigned to a calendar period; the design does not rely on a field the owner's filings check cannot confirm.
4. Q4 EPS is `FY EPS minus (Q1+Q2+Q3)` when the 10-K tags no 3M fact, marked derived (window decision 2026-11-17, advisor to confirm); it can differ from the reported Q4 EPS by rounding or a share-count change, so the owner check allows 0.02 dollars for it. Q4 diluted shares are shown only from a 3M fact, else missing. The band for a share-basis change (largest over smallest diluted share count of Q1, Q2, Q3 and FY at most 1.10) is a trial bound; a 2-for-1 split is far outside it, a buyback year inside.
5. The plan has one "debt" column; the design has two (long-term, short-term) so that no sum is a guess. Merging them is a page-only change.
6. The plan says "公司页加一张表". `companies_page.v2` is a wide page of many companies, so the design adds the table as its own page per company and does not edit that schema. A link from each company line of the companies page is a later small entry.
