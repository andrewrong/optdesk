# option pages r8: one distance, one underlying close, a fresh-day rule for notices and an ex-dividend date on the structures page

Revision note: revision 2 after `adversarial.md` (against c16ebc95): the shared types and `Pick` are no longer a new compute package (the root `compute` may not import a sub-package, `scripts/arch/main.go:286-289`, `docs/design/modules.md:37`); the rule is now one pages helper, `internal/pages/shared`, that sets the existing `UnderlyingClose` field of the quotes the pages already hand to compute; the scope covers every page that shows an underlying close or a distance; the pick is by day first.

## Goal

After this design a short put or call has one distance to its strike on every option page that shows one, computed from one underlying close that has a date on the page; a position without a usable option mid still shows its stock close and distance; the notices page does not call a snapshot of the last closed session stale; and the structures page shows the next ex-dividend date of a short call from the stored dividend expectation, or says in Chinese why there is none. The pages that show an underlying close or a distance are named in Decision 5 with what each does; two are left out with the reason (Left out).

## Decisions

1. Distance, one definition [B1]. Cause: `internal/compute/optiontrack/numbers.go:113-123` writes `(spot - strike) / strike x 100` for a put (base the strike); `internal/compute/optionstruct/marks.go:262-267` writes `(strike - spot) / spot` for every leg, put or call, so a put shows a negative number when it is out of the money; `docs/GLOSSARY.md` (entry "Distance out of the money", line 406) defines `(close - strike) / close` for a put and `(strike - close) / close` for a call, a fraction of the close, positive out of the money, negative in the money, and line 100 defines a second term, "Distance to the strike" (`abs(underlying - strike) / underlying`, none in the money). The line-406 entry is the definition and absorbs line 100 (entry 7). Tracking `otm_percent` becomes the glossary figure times 100 (two places, half even); the structures `expiry_distance` becomes the glossary figure as a ratio of six places. The moneyness of both pages is the sign of this number: above zero out of the money, below zero in the money, zero at the money. The distance of a key level of the detail page (`structure.level_distance`, `(price - spot) / spot`) keeps its formula and its meaning (a level has no in/out); its numbers move only because its spot is now the one close of Decision 2. The older text of `docs/design/option-tracking.md` Decision 5 (base the strike) is superseded; that file is not edited. The structures cell `moneyness` (`structures_page/cells.go:137-154`) reads the sign today and, for a put, the old sign; it flips with the sign, so entry 1 carries the compute change and the cell with its fixture together (one merge, no deployed page with the opposite moneyness); the cell reads the sign only and no longer the leg's right.
2. Underlying close, one rule [B1, B3]. Cause: tracking takes the close from the newest option_eod row of the held contract (`context.go:282`, `rows.go:60-62`); structures from any row of the underlying with the greatest day, the first met in the order of the terms reader (`load.go:143-163`, `marks.go:125-132`). `obs.option_eod.underlying_price` is "price of the underlying at the capture" (`docs/contracts/datasets/obs.option_eod.md:30`), so one day holds several prices (the TSM chain page shows 373.80 for 2026-11-17, the tracking row of TSM 340 shows 367.71). The rule already used by the positions page is the base: the newest `trades` bar up to the day with a look-back of 14 calendar days (`docs/design/spot-from-bars.md` Decision 2, `positions_page/bars.go:73-77`). It is extended to this single rule, in one pages helper (Decision 3): the candidates are the newest bar with a positive close and the newest option_eod print with a positive `underlying_price`, both with a day not after the as-of day and within 14 calendar days. The one with the newer day wins, so the close of the newest closed session wins whether it comes from a bar or a print; on the same day the bar wins; among the prints of one day the greatest `UnderlyingEventTs` wins, a print with no event time ranks last of its day, and equal times go to the later row of the input. When no candidate is left there is no close. The result is the price, its day and its source (`bar` or `option_eod`), and the page shows the day and the source: `close <day>` for a bar and `last print <day>` for a print, because an option_eod print of the as-of day is a snapshot of the capture and may precede the close of that day. Bars come first on a tie of days because a bar is one price per day for every contract; the print is the way in for an underlying the bars job does not pull (it covers the watch list and its indexes, `internal/jobs/barsrefresh/plan.go`; CPSH, TMUS, INTC, NOK, ORCL and WDC are held outside it).
3. Where the rule lives [B1]. A page already hands compute the price it selected (the positions page sets `Mark` from a bar, spot-from-bars Decision 2); selecting is not computing a figure. The rule is the function `Pick` in a new sub-package `internal/pages/shared` (`docs/design/modules.md:37`: a sub-package named `shared` may be imported by the other sub-packages of its row, and it imports no sibling), imported by `positions_page`, `structures_page`, `option_tracking_page`, `risk_page` and `option_candidates_page`. No new compute interface, no change of `internal/compute/api.go` (it is at its ceiling of 840 lines, `docs/design/ratchet.json`), no `FigureValue.Day`. A page sets the existing `UnderlyingClose` text of every `StructureQuote` or `TrackQuote` of the underlying to the picked price; a close that is missing leaves it empty. The day is kept by the page that picked it and shown by it (Decision 4). `docs/design/modules.md` is not edited.
4. The close does not depend on the option mid, and has a date on the pages [B4]. Cause: `markFor` (`rows.go:41-58`) returns no mark when the quote is older than the limit or not two-sided, and `fillMark` (`rows.go:124-132`) writes `Spot`, `Moneyness` and `OtmPercent` only inside it, so CPSH call 20 and the wife's TMUS put 140 lose their close with the mid. After the change `Spot`, `Moneyness` and `OtmPercent` come from the `UnderlyingClose` of the newest quote of the contract whether or not the quote gives a mid; `Mid`, `Kept`, `Extrinsic`, delta and IV still need the mark. `spotOf` of the structures rule reads the `UnderlyingClose` of the newest quote of the underlying that has one, with no age check of its own (the page already left an old close out); a missing one is the gap `missing_quote`. The spot cell of the tracking page shows the source and day of the picked close as its badge, `close 2026-11-17` or `last print 2026-11-18` (today it shows `MarkDay`, the day of the option quote, so a close of 2026-11-17 under the header of 2026-11-18 reads as wrong); the header day is the page day and stays. The structures page writes one Chinese line in its `note` naming the source and day of the close of each underlying whose distance it shows (a string, no schema change); that line is added after the run status is decided, so it never makes a page `partial` (the status is `partial` only for a gap or another note, `list.go:140-141`). The payoff block of the structures page (`payoff.go:68-76`, `spot()`, the quote of the underlying with the greatest day) needs no change: after this decision every quote of an underlying carries the same picked price, so the payoff spot is that price.
5. Scope: every page that shows an underlying close or a distance [B2]. `option_tracking_page` and `structures_page`: Decisions 1 to 4. `risk_page` (`load.go:185-195`, close copied from the option_eod row): sets `UnderlyingClose` from `Pick`. `positions_page`: keeps the stored mark first and takes its bar fallback from `Pick` with no prints (the same 14 days), so a stock priced from a bar has the same rule. `option_candidates_page`: its spot is the price the candidates job stored at task time (`zh_texts.go:106` says it is not the close); it is not aligned (it is the price of the moment of the screen, not a close, and its hint already says so) and the page is not changed. `option_pnl_page` (`marks.go:21-30`) uses the quote-day price of each lot for the attribution identity (`optionattr` golden); it is a historical per-day value, not a close shown, and stays. `bookrisk` (`internal/jobs/bookrisk/load.go:292-305`, a job of the `jobs` row) cannot import `internal/pages/shared` (`docs/design/modules.md:37`: modules import only the root package of another module) and has no rule from compute; it stays on its own rule and is named in Left out as a question of the module map. The stock pages that read bars only, with no option rows, keep their own bar rule (Left out). `option_notices_page` shows no close and no distance. `home_options` has none (grep).
6. Notices freshness [B2 of the first review]. Cause: `internal/pages/option_notices_page/blocks.go:99-101` marks a block `stale` whenever the capture day is before the build day. The capture of the last closed US session is dated the day before the Hong Kong day until the next session closes, so the page says "Stale 2026-11-17" all day on 2026-11-18, while the tracking page's "marked 2026-11-18" is the day of an option quote, a different thing. Rule: as r7 Decision 5 for a chain: a capture is fresh when its day is the XNYS session of the build day (the last session not after it) or the session just before it; two or more sessions behind, after the build day or off the list it is stale. The page reads the session days with a `store.SessionDayReader` added to its `Config` (nil keeps the old comparison). The label text of the stale case is unchanged. The page holds its own copy of the rule because a page cannot import the unexported `chainFresh` of `internal/compute/optioncand/events.go:21`; the two tests use the same cases (build day session, session before, two sessions behind, after the build day, off the list).
7. Ex-dividend of a structure [B5]. Cause: `ExDividend` of `internal/pages/structures_page/list.go:53` is never set, so the cell is null in every row. The stored source is `obs.dividend_expectation` read through `dividend.Reader` (`internal/store/dividend/api.go:20`): `Dividends(ctx, instrumentID, from, to)`, ascending by day, the highest attempt of each day. The page reads the rows of the instrument of the underlying (the id it already finds for quotes, converted to `int`) for the 10 days up to the page day and takes the newest row. A structure with a sold call leg shows: for a `present` row whose `next_ex_date` is not before the page day, `<next_ex_date> · <next_amount>` (as the tracking page writes its Ex-dividend cell, `cells.go:318-331`); for a `present` row whose date has passed, the Chinese gap `除息日已过，等待下次采集`; for no row, `no_value`, `unsupported` or `error`, the Chinese gap `没有除息预期记录（该股可能不派息，或尚未采集）`. A structure with no sold call leg shows the Chinese gap `不适用：没有卖出认购腿`. A nil reader gives `没有除息数据来源`. The gap `reason` of these cells is from the schema enum: `no_reader` for the nil reader, `no_data` for no row and for a passed date, `no_rule` for a structure with no sold call; the text is the Chinese text, set on the gap directly (not through `gapTexts`, which holds English). The web hides the column only when every row is null (issue r8-2), so a gap row keeps it.
8. Register and glossary [B1, B2]. `docs/conventions/register.toml` and `docs/GLOSSARY.md` are in the `docs` module (`docs/**`, the last row of `docs/design/modules.md`; no other row names them), so their change is its own entry (7): `structure.expiry_distance` version 3 (formula and examples of Decision 1; the close and its day of Decision 2; gap `missing_quote` for no close, `stale_input` no longer used for it); the glossary line 100 "Distance to the strike" is merged into the line-406 "Distance out of the money" (one definition, positive out of the money, the 5% near-strike level of the close pages stated as a threshold of that one number: out of the money by at most `near_strike_pct`).
