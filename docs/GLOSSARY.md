# Glossary and units

Every module, test, report and issue uses these definitions. If code needs a different convention (for example, a library's native unit), convert at the boundary and say so in a docstring.

## Scope

- Listed US equity options: American exercise, physically settled.
- Standard contracts only: 100 shares deliverable, multiplier 100. Adjusted contracts (after splits, mergers or special dividends, with a non-standard deliverable) are flagged and excluded from calculations.
- Short options can be assigned early. The main risk for short calls is the day before an ex-dividend date. Reports flag it.

## Conventions

| Quantity | Unit / convention |
|---|---|
| Volatility (IV, HV, forecasts) | Annualised, decimal: `0.25` means 25%. Reports may show percent. |
| Vol point | `0.01` of volatility (25% → 26% is one vol point). Differences reported in vol points are `(a − b) / 0.01`. |
| Expiry moment | 16:00 US/Eastern on the expiration date, or the exchange's early close that day. |
| Time to expiry `T` | Years = seconds from the quote's source timestamp to the expiry moment / (365 × 86 400). Libraries that work in whole dates must not silently drop intraday time; document how it is handled. |
| DTE | Calendar-date difference in US/Eastern between the quote date and the expiration date. No annualised figure is computed when DTE ≤ 0. |
| Interest rate `r` | Annual, continuously compounded, decimal. |
| Dividends | Discrete cash dividends (ex-date, amount per share) for pricing. A continuous yield `q` is used only for European cross-checks. |
| Prices and premiums | Per share, for one long option. Position values multiply by the signed quantity (short = negative) and the multiplier. |
| Money | All money is a `Money` value with an amount and `Currency`. Arithmetic does not mix currencies. |
| Currency | USD or JPY. The final decision currency is USD (ADR 0004); local amounts are kept next to USD values. |
| Timestamps | Timezone-aware UTC in storage; US/Eastern for display and expiry. |
| FX observation | One received USD.JPY bid/ask record, retained even when policy rejects it. |
| Decision-grade FX | A live, current-generation USD.JPY observation that passes price, spread, market-hours and freshness policy. |
| FX unknown | No decision-grade observation is available; FX-dependent decisions are held with no fallback rate. |
| Conversion purpose | Valuation, proceeds, obligation or funding; it determines which USD.JPY quote side is used. |
| Funding buffer (`h`) | The decimal reserve applied to the USD funding requirement; it starts at `0.10`. |
| Figure convention | The original document of the rule of a page figure (inputs, period, currency, exclusions, gap text) is an entry of `src/option_trader/conventions/register.toml`; a change to its code needs the entry updated (ADR 0039). |

## Greeks

Greeks are sensitivities of one **long** option's price, per share. Positions scale them by signed quantity × multiplier.

| Greek | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Delta | Price change per $1 move in the underlying. |
| Gamma | Delta change per $1 move in the underlying. |
| Vega | Price change per +1 vol point. |
| Theta | Price change when one calendar day passes, all else equal (usually negative for a long option). |
| Rho | Price change per +1 percentage point (`0.01`) in `r`. |
| Dollar delta | The delta of a name in shares of underlying times the latest close, in the position's currency. It is not the profit or loss for a move of any size. |

Every library or broker source has its own scaling. Convert to these units at the boundary and test the conversion.

Delta is a hedge ratio, not the probability of assignment. Never label it as one.

## Volatility terms

- **IV**: implied volatility. Always say which source it comes from:
  - **bid IV / ask IV**: solved from the bid or ask price;
  - **mid IV**: solved from the mid price, never the average of bid IV and ask IV;
  - **IBKR model IV**: from IBKR's own model, comparable to ours only when the assumptions are aligned.
- **IV30**: the underlying's 30-day at-the-money composite IV.
  - Historical series: IBKR `OPTION_IMPLIED_VOLATILITY` daily bars.
  - Live value: IBKR generic tick 106 (tick type 24).
  - It describes the underlying's volatility level. It is not the IV history of any single contract.
- **N225 implied volatility (IBKR)**: IBKR generic tick 106 on the N225 index contract (`Index("N225", "OSE.JPN", "JPY")`), one raw value per JP session, captured at 15:55 JST after the OSE day-session close; the first valid value of a session date is kept. Stored as received (an annualised decimal, 0.32 is 32%) with its receipt time, source time and market-data type. It is never the same thing as Nikkei VI and never mixed with the IV30 of a stock. The label "IBKR ~30d" stays until a manual comparison with Nikkei VI is written down. There is no backfill; the history depth of any IBKR IV series for this index is unknown.
- **HV30**: IBKR's 30-day historical volatility of the underlying, an annualised decimal, stored as received.
  - Historical series: IBKR `HISTORICAL_VOLATILITY` daily bars.
  - It is IBKR's figure, not our **HV(n)**; never mix the two.
  - It is reconciliation only: the tool's own figure is **HV(n)** (`market.hv30_own`), and a difference between the two is recorded, never shown.
- **HV(n)**: sample standard deviation (ddof = 1) of the last `n` daily log returns of split-adjusted closing prices (so `n + 1` closes), × √252. Closes are not dividend-adjusted.
- **Beta**: the slope of a name's daily log returns on its benchmark's over the last 250 common sessions, sample covariance over sample variance (ddof=1), using the same returns as HV(n). Fewer than 120 common pairs gives no figure. Benchmarks: SPX for US names (SPY until SPX has the history), 1306 (NEXT FUNDS TOPIX ETF) for JP names (TOPX until then). A ratio shown to 2 decimals, with the benchmark and the sessions used stated beside it.
- **EWMA vol**: exponentially weighted volatility forecast. The decay factor is stated where used.
- **Forecasts from `arch`**: `arch` returns variance in the units of its input. If returns are scaled (for example ×100), undo the scaling. Sum the daily variances over the trading days to expiry before taking the square root, then annualise.
- **IV percentile**: share of days in the lookback window whose IV30 was strictly below today's.
  - The window is the 252 trading days before today, excluding today, sampled from daily closing bars.
  - Missing days are skipped. Fewer than 200 valid days → "not available".
  - On the card "today" is the newest stored daily value, no older than 4 calendar days.
- **IV rank**: `(today − min) / (max − min)` of IV30 over the same window. If `max == min` it is "not available".
  - Not the same as IV percentile; never mix them.
- **Cboe volatility indices (VIX, VXN, RVX)**: the Cboe indices of the S&P 500, the Nasdaq-100 and the Russell 2000, stored from FRED as `VIXCLS`, `VXNCLS` and `RVXCLS` in index points as Cboe publishes them (18.20 is 18.20 points, about 18.2 vol points annualised; not a fraction, not a percent). Each is a methodology of its own, not an at-the-money IV30: shown in their own block of the home 市场 card, never in the IV cell of the index table.
- **Own IV30** (`market.iv30_own`, dataset `derived.us_equity_iv30_own`, convention `iv30_v1`): the tool's 30-day implied volatility from `option_daily`, as a fraction per year. Per expiration the ATM IV is the mean of the call and put IBKR model IV (`iv_decimal`) at the strike nearest the forward `F = K* + exp(rT)(C-P)`, K* the strike with the smallest |C-P| (the lower strike wins ties); the rate is `DGS3MO` (percent converted to a decimal at the adapter; the prior value with the flag `stale_rate` when the session has none). The two expirations around 30 days are joined by the Cboe VIX variance interpolation, no extrapolation; an expiration of exactly 30 days is used alone; expirations of 0 or 1 day are ignored. It is IBKR's own IV30 only in reconciliation: the difference is recorded (tolerance in `volatility/params/iv30_v1.toml`, first guess 2 points absolute or 10% relative, whichever is larger), shown nowhere, and flips no verdict.
- **IV source labels**: `自有 IV30` marks the tool's own IV30 and `IBKR ~30d IV` marks IBKR's stored reading, each shown with its data day (`mm-dd`) beside an IV value of the home market table. The IBKR reading on an index row is a stop-gap where own IV30 is not stored (ADR 0033 exception); the difference between the two stays recorded, never shown.
- **Own IV percentile / IV rank** (`market.ivp_own`, `market.ivr_own`, convention `ivp_v1`): on the own IV30 series, IVP is the share of the past 252 valid sessions whose IV30 is at or below today's, today excluded; None with fewer than 252 valid sessions. IVR is `(IV − min) / (max − min)` over the same window, None when the range is 0. There is no bootstrap from IBKR's history; the legacy rule above (strictly below, at least 200 values) stays a separate dataset of IBKR's IV30 and the two series are never mixed.
- **`ivp_v1` is the rule of `market.ivp_own`**: the figure `market.ivp_own` is computed by the convention `ivp_v1` of the entry above (at or below, 252 valid sessions, today excluded); the `Compare` of its rule is `AtOrBelow`.
- **VIX term ratio** (`market.vix_term_ratio`): `VIXCLS` over `VXVCLS` (VIX over VIX3M, both FRED, index points) of the same day, a ratio (1.0 is flat; below 1 is contango). A zero `VXVCLS` or a missing leg is "not available", never a zero; the two days must be the same. Stored as decimal text rounded half-even to 6 places.
- **3-year percentiles of the market series** (`market.vix_pctile_3y` on `VIXCLS`, `market.vxn_pctile_3y` on `VXNCLS`, `market.rvx_pctile_3y` on `RVXCLS`, `market.sp500_pctile_3y` on `SP500`): each follows the `N225_IV` 3-year percentile rule above: the share of stored daily values in the 3 calendar years before the day, the day excluded, that are strictly below the value of the day (`Compare` is `Below`, fixed per figure), 0 to 1, with its count of observations; missing sessions are skipped, never filled; none before the stored history spans 3 years. They are not the own IV percentile (`ivp_v1`, at or below, 252 sessions); the names are never mixed.
- **收盘价来源** (close source): the source a market-weather vote's figure rests on, `IBKR` or `FRED`, shown with the date of that figure. A row uses IBKR when its newest IBKR value exists, otherwise FRED and the FRED date; where the window of a series is part FRED and part IBKR, the first IBKR date is said once in the footnote.
- **来源不一致** (source mismatch): a day on which IBKR and FRED both have a value for a market-weather series and the two differ by more than 0.05 (index points, or percent for credit) or lie on different sides of a band. It is shown as a line and changes no verdict and no state.
- **盘中试算** (intraday trial): the 盘中 gauge shown beside the close gauge of the market weather card while the US session is open. Volatility is the only vote it recomputes: each band (VIX, term ratio VIX÷VIX3M) starts from its close state and takes one live figure; trend and credit are the close's and are marked 收盘 with the close date. It keeps no state between reads, never writes `bar_daily` and never changes the close gauge, an advice, a quantity, a cap or a term.
- **盘中** (intraday): the part of the US session in `[open, close)`; a reading is 盘中 when both live legs (VIX, VIX3M) are counted: age under 15 minutes for live data (stale from exactly 15), under 30 minutes for delayed data (market-data type 3, always labelled 盘中·延迟), source times within 120 seconds of each other. Frozen and unknown types are rejected. The section expires at the oldest counted quote plus its limit, or at the session close if sooner.
- **Volatility premium**: IV minus a realised or forecast volatility for the same horizon, in vol points. Descriptive only; not an expected profit.
- **N225 IV beside RV20 (display)**: the block 日本：隐含波动对实现波动 of the market page, three tiles: `N225 IV` (`market.iv_n225`, IBKR ~30d, percent, one decimal), `RV20` (`market.rv20_n225`) and `IV − RV20` (`market.iv_rv20_n225`, vol points, signed).
  - The sentence under the tiles states the sign only (higher, lower, same); it never says the premium is rich, cheap or enough. One footnote says the basis is IBKR's ~30-day estimate, not checked against Nikkei VI, and that the horizons and sources differ. The label "IBKR ~30d" stays until a manual comparison with Nikkei VI is written down in this repository.
  - A missing input is said once with `†` and one line. IV and RV20 of different days leave the difference pending.
  - 3-year percentile: the share of stored daily `N225_IV` values in the 3 calendar years before the day, the day excluded, that are strictly below the IV of the day, with its count of observations. Missing sessions are skipped, never filled. No percentile is shown before the stored history spans 3 years ("历史不满 3 年，不给分位"). It is not the IV percentile above (252 trading days of a stock's IV30, at least 200 values); the two names are never mixed.
- **Reference ETF closes**: the daily IBKR closes of GLD, IWM and RSP, stored in `benchmark_closes` and shown as ETF closes, never as the Russell 2000, spot gold or an index. An index or spot series that arrives later is a new series and is never spliced onto these (see Index closes). Spot gold (XAUUSD) is likewise a separate series: GLD is a fund share price in USD, not an ounce of gold, and neither replaces the other (see Spot gold).
- **Index closes (RUT, TOPX)**: the dataset `index_closes`: IBKR `TRADES` daily bars of the cash index (not a future), in index points, not money; price return excluding dividends. The history of a series starts on its first stored date; the depth is whatever IBKR gives (unknown before the first run). Never spliced onto IWM or 1306 and never labelled as an ETF.
- **Spot gold (XAUUSD)**: the series `XAUUSD` of the dataset `index_closes`: the IBKR `MIDPOINT` daily bar of the `CMDTY` contract `XAUUSD` (SMART, USD), in USD per ounce. Where IBKR cuts the day is IBKR's convention and unverified; the history depth is unknown before the first run. Never GLD and never spliced onto it. Display only: one row at the end of the market page's index table, after the GLD row (both rows are shown), and its detail page `/indicator/xauusd`; no FRED companions, no currency translation, no use in heat or ease.
- **Benchmark list**: the `[[benchmark]]` rows of `refdata/datasets.toml`: SPX (IND, CBOE), SPY, QQQ and IWM (STK, SMART), each with its `sec_type`, `exchange` and option classes (SPX: `SPX` and `SPXW`, both under the SPX underlying). System data, apart from the owner's watchlist. `master-benchmark-load` asks IBKR for their contract details and writes them straight into the security master: SPX as an `index` instrument, the ETFs as `etf`, their options with provenance `ibkr:contract_details`. A name the master lacks is `not_in_master` in a capture report; a capture run in which every requested name is `not_in_master` ends FAILED.
- **Term structure**: IV across expiries at a comparable moneyness or delta.
- **Skew**: IV across strikes within one expiry.
- **Volatility state**: the descriptive report section combining the items above, plus earnings inside the horizon and data quality. It never says "buy" or "sell".

- **Distance to the strike** (the 接近行权价 point of the 风险点 block of the close pages): of a held short option leg out of the money, `abs(underlying − strike) / underlying`, a fraction of the underlying price (0.05 is 5%), Decimal. The level 接近 is a distance at most `near_strike_pct` of `reports/params/close_risks_v1.toml` (0.05, the owner's word of 2026-11-01; exactly 5% is inside); a leg in the money has no distance level, only the existing 可能被指派 point. A missing, non-finite, zero or negative underlying price, a crossed quote or a stale price is a named gap for that leg, never 0%. It is not the `near_strike` of the grade (`abs(close − strike) <= 0.0025 × strike`, `strategies/grading.py`), which is a different thing and is untouched. Display only: it says what is near and never what to do.

## Candidate status

- **PASS**: every evaluated candidate gate passes; funding and concentration are not yet evaluated.
- **UNKNOWN**: no evaluated gate rejects, but at least one lacks usable information.
- **REJECT**: at least one evaluated gate fails; all reasons are retained.

## Strategy contract

- **Strategy**: one option structure and its rule (for example `short_put`), with an id and a rule version; it reads one market-only input and returns an Evaluation.
- **Evaluation**: a strategy's result for one structure at one moment: strategy id, rule and parameter versions, parameter hash, as-of time (UTC), symbol, candidate status, legs, checks and facts. It is market-only: no account, position or owner-mark value enters it.
- **Leg**: one option contract of the structure: symbol, right (`P` or `C`), expiry, strike, multiplier, `con_id` when known, and the signed quantity per one unit of the structure (−1 = one contract sold).
- **Check**: one gate that did not pass, as a reason code with REJECT or UNKNOWN. `checks` lists only what did not pass; the status is REJECT if any check rejects, else UNKNOWN if any is unknown, else PASS.
- **Facts**: the strategy's own numbers for the structure (for a short put: DTE, quotes, yields, breakeven), in the units of this glossary; they differ by strategy, the rest of an Evaluation does not.

## Opportunity record (ADR 0018)

- **Opportunity record**: the permanent, append-only store of every evaluation of every scan, rejected ones included, each with a copy of the input it saw (run, stock and contract part) and its whole output as canonical JSON with a SHA-256. Market-only; never pruned.
- **Scan**: one strategy's evaluations of one job, in evaluation order, with the coverage per stock (contracts planned, evaluated, deferred, skipped). Stored once per job and strategy as revision 0; a correction is a new revision.
- **Structure key**: the identity of a proposal by its economic terms: per leg `symbol|right|expiry|strike|multiplier|quantity` (strike without trailing zeros or exponent), legs sorted and joined by `;`. A `con_id` is an alias, not part of it.
- **Replay guarantee**: a scan is stored only if joining each evaluation's three stored input parts and evaluating again gives exactly its stored output text; reading a scan verifies its hashes and projections first.
- **Grade**: where the underlying closed on the expiry date of one stored evaluation, read from the stored daily closes alone once the expiry has passed. Not an assignment, not a win: early assignment and adjustments that leave the close series unchanged (a special cash dividend, a merger) are not modelled, and a grade never changes a later scan's result. Stored append-only in `grade`; a corrected close appends a row that supersedes the old one, and `grade_current` gives the rows nothing supersedes.
  - Status `GRADED`: the expiry close is stored and the series was not rebased. Status `UNGRADEABLE`: no net credit at entry (`no_entry_credit`), no close stored for the expiry date 7 or more calendar days after it (`no_expiry_close`), no close stored at the evaluation's `as_of` for any of the 14 calendar days before the entry date (`no_entry_basis`), or a close series as stored now whose usable-from date is after the first needed date, the entry date less 14 calendar days (`close_series_step`). Status `VOID_CORPORATE_ACTION` (`close_series_rebased`): the newest close before the entry date, as stored now, differs from the same date's close as stored at `as_of` by more than 0.005 of the latter (a split between entry and expiry).
  - Grade `OTM`: `close >= strike`. Grade `ITM_ABOVE_NET_BREAKEVEN`: `strike − credit / multiplier <= close < strike`. Grade `ITM_BELOW_NET_BREAKEVEN`: below that. `credit` is the net premium of one contract in the contract's currency (the short put's `premium_net`).
  - `net_breakeven` = `strike − credit / multiplier`, per share. `payoff` = `(credit − max(strike − close, 0) × multiplier) / (strike × multiplier)`, the return on the cash secured, a decimal fraction over the holding period (not annualised), 8 decimal places, half-even. `bill_return` = `cash_rate × days / 365`, the Treasury-bill return over the same days, rounded the same way; absent when the cash rate is. `min_close`, `min_close_date`, `closes`: the lowest stored close from the entry date to the expiry date, both included, its date (the earliest on a tie) and how many stored values the span had. `near_strike`: `abs(close − strike) <= 0.0025 × strike`.
  - Spread grade (put credit spread, `grade_spread_at_expiry`): every figure is "if held to expiry"; exits, fills, fees for closing or for assignment and early assignment are not modelled, and a close between the strikes gives a mark at that close, not a realised result. Same statuses and reasons as above, plus `UNGRADEABLE` `credit_not_below_width` when the credit is not below `(short_strike − long_strike) × multiplier`, decided before the wait. `credit` is the net credit of one structure in the contract's currency; zero or negative is allowed. The grade is one of the same three values on the short strike, with `net_breakeven` = `short_strike − credit / multiplier`; it is a position of the close, not a profit. `close_between_strikes`: `long_strike <= close < short_strike`; `close_at_or_below_long`: `close <= long_strike`; both are positions of the close and say nothing of delivered shares or a realised loss. `amount` = `credit − max(0, short_strike − close) × multiplier + max(0, long_strike − close) × multiplier` and `max_loss` = `(short_strike − long_strike) × multiplier − credit`, per structure. `return_on_max_loss` = `amount / max_loss` and `return_on_short_strike` = `amount / (short_strike × multiplier)`, decimal fractions over the holding period (not annualised), 8 decimal places, half-even. `near_short_strike` and `near_long_strike` use the tolerance of `near_strike` on each strike. `first_close_below_short` (with its date): the first stored close below the short strike from the entry date to the close-by date, or to the expiry without one. `close_by_close` (with its date): the newest stored close from the entry date to the close-by date. Nothing about an exit, a fill, an option price or a realised result is inferred from these two.
  - Covered-call grade (`grade_call_at_expiry`): every figure is "if held to expiry", per contract in the contract's currency, the computed figures with 8 decimal places, half-even; nothing is a rate, nothing is annualised and there is no word for good or bad. Same statuses and reasons as the short put's. `credit` is the offer's net premium of one contract at the bid after fees, not a fill; zero or negative is allowed. `close_above_strike`: `close > strike`, a position of the close and not a statement that the shares were called (early exercise, exercise at or near the strike and the holder's own choice are not modelled); `near_strike` as above. `upside_given_up` = `max(0, close − strike) × multiplier`. `against_holding` = `credit − upside_given_up`, the call's result against holding the same shares without it; positive does not mean the shares made money. `holding_crossover` = `strike + credit / multiplier`, per share: above this close, holding without the call was better. `stock_move` = `(close − spot) × multiplier`, what the shares did meanwhile, with `spot` the price at the offer. `max_close`, `max_close_date`, `closes`: the highest stored close from the entry date to the expiry date, both included, its date (the earliest on a tie) and how many stored values the span had. `first_close_at_sale_price` (with its date) and `first_close_at_strike_date`: the first stored close of the span at or above the owner's sale price, and the first date at or above the strike; none when there is none. `limit_state` compares the call with a limit sell order at the sale price (not at the strike), by daily closes only: `NEITHER` (no close at or above the sale price), `BOTH` (one, and `close > strike`) or `LIMIT_ONLY` (one, and `close <= strike`: the limit order is taken as filled, the call ended without a sale). `against_limit` is the call path less the limit path under this model: `credit` for `NEITHER`, `credit + (strike − sale_price) × multiplier` for `BOTH`, `credit − (sale_price − close) × multiplier` for `LIMIT_ONLY`. `near_sale_price_unseen`: no close at or above the sale price and `max_close >= sale_price × (1 − 0.0025)`, where an intraday touch the tool cannot see is plausible. Left out: the intraday high, a fill above the limit on a gap and the fact that a touch is not a guaranteed fill (which can favour either side); dividends, in both comparisons, so `against_limit` is "excluding dividends"; any return on the cash of an earlier sale; the stock after a sale, which is a separate later record.
- **Covered-call grade**: the figures of `grade_call_at_expiry` (under Grade, above) for one stored covered-call offer, stored append-only in `call_grade`, where a corrected result appends a row that supersedes the old one and `call_grade_current` gives the rows nothing supersedes. Only the offer of each expiry of a stock judged with a sale price is graded, chosen by the selector of the scan's rule version; a contract whose multiplier is not 100 is stored `UNGRADEABLE` with reason `adjusted_deliverable`. `call_grade_scan` holds, per scan, how many evaluations were selected and whether the grade job is done with it (`closed`).
- **Advice entry**: one advice sentence a report showed, stored append-only in `advice_entry` (store `strategies/advice_entry_store.py`) once per run: `advice_id`, `run_id`, `kl_date` (the report's market day), `market`, `kind` (`held_action`; an `offer` row only links a scan and position as its `structure_key` and is not re-graded), account alias, `structure_key` (`SYMBOL|con_id:contracts,...`, legs by contract id, contracts signed, short negative), `sentence`, `urgent`, `rule_version`, `param_hash`, `ref_price` (the underlying's price) and `ref_value` (the structure's net buy-back mid: shorts bought back, longs sold), both per share in the contract's currency at advice time. Headlines stay in `report_headline`.
- **Advice outcome**: what the next close showed of an advice entry, in `advice_outcome`, written by the grade job: `outcome_date` (the first day after `kl_date`, within 4 calendar days, with a stored close of the underlying), `acted` (`followed` when a Flex trade row of the account is on a contract of the structure from `kl_date` to `outcome_date`; `not_followed`; `unknown` when there is no Flex data or no close in the window), `after_price`, `after_value` (the buy-back mid from the quotes stored that day) and `delta` (`after_value` minus `ref_value`, per share). It draws no verdict and has no result column.
- **Shown offer**: one contract a report page showed on a card: the contract of one expiry block of one card, so one per card and expiry (the "other strikes" listed beside it are not shown offers). It names the stored evaluation (`scan_id`, `position`) with copies of `symbol`, `expiry` and `strike` (per share, in the contract's currency), and is frozen with the group of its card (`card_group`: `FORMAL`, `UNCONFIRMED` or `SPANNING`), the card's `layout` (`FORMAL` or `UNCONFIRMED`; a spanning card has either) and the name of the display rule that picked the contract (`highest-strike/1` on a formal layout, `nearest-delta-0.20/1` on an unconfirmed one), because the display rules change and the choice cannot be worked out again later. Shown is not taken: the tool does not know what the owner sold. Stored append-only in `shown_offer`, once per scan when its report is written; `shown_scan` records that a scan's shown offers were written, so a page without a card (no row) can be told from a scan that was never recorded. Since format version 2 the offers are chosen behind the pool gate and the decision is stored with them: the offer's `pool`; on `shown_scan` the import's id (`pool_import_id`), the manifest's hash (`pool_sha256`), the `gate_version` and the `freshness`; and in `shown_excluded` one row per stock of the scan that the gate did not allow, with its `outcome` (`other_pool`, `untagged`, `conflict` or `expired`), `pool` and `reason`. Rows of format version 1 have none of these. A covered call's shown offer (format version 3) is one offer row without a reason of a drawn card, so one per stock and expiry with an offer: layout `CALL`, display rule `offer-rows/1`, group `CALL_OFFER`, or `CALL_CHANGED` when the card says the sale-price record changed after the scan, and `sale_price`, the owner's sale price per share in the contract's currency, which only a `CALL` offer has.
- **First offer**: the first time a card showed a stock for an expiry under one rule version: among the shown offers with the same (strategy, rule version, symbol, expiry), the one of the scan with the earliest `as_of`, on a tie the lower `scan_id`. It is a **case** and keeps that offer's contract (`scan_id`, `position`, strike) and card group for good; later shown offers with the same key are never cases. It tracks what the tool showed, not what the owner traded. Derived on read, never stored. A covered call's first offer has the sale price in its key, (rule version, symbol, expiry, sale price), with the lower position as the last tie-break, so a changed sale price makes a new case; its summaries are per rule version and card group and are never pooled with the short put's.
  - State of a case, from the current grade of its evaluation: `ENDED` (`GRADED`), `VOID` (`VOID_CORPORATE_ACTION`), `WAITING` (`UNGRADEABLE` with `no_expiry_close`, or no grade and an expiry before today), `UNGRADEABLE` (any other reason), `OPEN` (no grade and an expiry on or after today).
  - Summary: one per (strategy, rule version, card group), never pooled; its figures are of the `ENDED` cases only, the other states are counted. `cases`; `expiries`, the distinct expiry dates; `days`, the latest expiry minus the earliest entry date (the market's local date of the first offer's scan) in calendar days; `above_strike`, the cases with grade `OTM`; `worst`, the case with the lowest `payoff` (on a tie the earlier expiry, then the symbol).
  - Excess of a case = `payoff − bill_return` of its grade, a decimal return on the cash secured over the holding period, not annualised. `average_excess` is the mean over the expiry dates of the mean over the cases of that date; it is unknown while a case's grade has no `bill_return` (`excess_unknown` counts them).
  - `stage`: `ENOUGH` when `cases >= min_cases` (200), `expiries >= min_expiries` (12) and `days >= min_days` (183), else `TOO_EARLY`; parameters in `strategies/params/first_offer_v1.toml`. Nothing is concluded before the thresholds, and the summary has no win rate, no share above the strike, no annualised figure and no word for good or bad.
- **Taken offer**: a first offer (a case) that the stored option record says the owner sold: a USD structure of the same account whose leading position is sold, of the case's right (`P` short put, `C` covered call), with the case's symbol and expiry, and `entry_date <= opened_on <= expiry`; it is `sold` at the offered strike, else `other strike`.
  A structure belongs to the one case with the latest `entry_date`; counted per account and card group, never across accounts (`reports/tracking_taken.py`).

## IBKR classification strings

- **IBKR industry, category, subcategory, stockType**: the four strings of IBKR's `ContractDetails` (`industry`, `category`, `subcategory`, `stockType`), taken from the contract-details answer already requested at qualification and stored with the instrument (`instrument.industry`, `category`, `subcategory`, `stock_type`) exactly as received: no normalisation, no mapping, empty when IBKR gave none. They are a check only (ADR 0033): no rule, cap or page reads them. `option-trader sector-coverage` counts them per market (present and empty) and lists the `stockType` values seen; it is read-only and makes no network call.

## Trade terms

- **Put candidate**: PASS/UNKNOWN/REJECT with reason codes; yield at the bid net of fee, annualised simply by 365/DTE, compared with the cash rate supplied by the caller (the 3-month Treasury bill rate).

- **Spread-eligible**: with `0 < bid ≤ ask`, known positive `min_tick` and multiplier,
  and a known non-negative fee per contract, both `ask − bid ≤ max(tick_floor × min_tick,
  max_rel × mid)` and `((ask − bid) / 2 × multiplier + fee_per_contract) / (mid × multiplier)
  ≤ max_cost` hold, where `mid = (bid + ask) / 2`. US defaults: tick floor 2, relative
  limit 0.15; provisional JP defaults: tick floor 3, relative limit 0.30. Both use
  cost limit 0.10 and fee 1.00 in contract currency. Unknown inputs are never eligible.

- **Cash rate**: annual decimal 13-week US Treasury bill coupon-equivalent rate (ADR 0009). The reader `current_cash_rate` gives the newest stored value with a value, at most 7 calendar days old, else unknown; a newer row without a value is passed over (an exception to ADR 0015 for this reader only).
- **JP put rates**: the two dated rates a JP short put is measured against (owner decisions of 2026-10-16), annual decimals as IBKR Pro quotes them, set in `verdict_jp_v2.toml` with the date each was read: the USD cash rate (interest paid on USD cash) and the JPY borrowing rate (JPY margin loan rate). A JP put candidate's cash rate is the higher of the two; its excess over each rate is the bid yield minus that rate, stored beside it. JP DTE counts calendar days in Asia/Tokyo. Since then the rates are usable (`jp_rates_usable`) when both `as_of` dates are at most `rate_max_age_days` = 30 days before the scan day; otherwise every verdict that would be marginal or worth a look is 待定 with the reason `RATE_MISSING` (缺可用利率). Stale, reject and unknown verdicts keep their precedence over it.
- **JP covered-call excess**: a JP covered call (`covered_call_jp`) is measured against the same two dated rates of `verdict_jp_v2.toml`: its excess over each is its annual premium yield on share value minus that rate, a decimal, stored beside it; the rule's cash rate (read only by its ITM probability) is the higher of the two. Strikes, quotes and premiums are in JPY, multiplier 100. The 30-day age limit of the rates applies to it as to the put.
- **Cash-secured put (CSP)**: short put fully backed by cash: reserved cash = strike × multiplier.
- **Put spread (margin)**: a short put paired once with a filled long put of the same alias, underlying and settlement, with lower long strike and long expiry ≥ short expiry; v1 uses USD, multiplier 100 (ADR 0013).
- **Gross put notional**: short-strike × multiplier × contracts over short put positions and every working SELL put; used for concentration, cash funding and assignment display (ADR 0013).
- **Put spread risk**: gross put notional less paired long-strike × multiplier × contracts; paired contracts contribute spread width × multiplier, unpaired shorts retain full strike (ADR 0013).
- **MaintMarginReq**: broker-reported maintenance margin requirement, retained as a read-only summary value for ADR 0013 calibration and stress gates.
- **Policy margin estimate**: conservative candidate reserve in its currency under ADR 0013's Reg-T naked-put, put-spread or flat-40 policy, labelled `POLICY_ESTIMATE`; not a broker forecast.
- **Local maintenance estimate**: ADR 0013 estimate of filled positions' maintenance in the alias base currency, with 25% long-stock maintenance and eligible option cover; unknown inputs never become zero.
- **Shadow inputs**: read-only held IBKR marks and underlying prices for one account alias, with per-underlying source provenance and data-quality reasons (ADR 0014).
- **Fallback mark**: IBKR's per-share mark price from market data (generic tick 221), in the contract's currency, used for a held position without a portfolio mark when received at most 15 minutes ago; stored once with its account snapshot (the first stored row is never replaced) and reported as `PRICE_FALLBACK` (ADR 0014).
- **Expiry intrinsic price**: the per-share price given to a held short option that the broker still lists without a mark after its last session: `max(0, strike − close)` for a put and `max(0, close − strike)` for a call, with the underlying's stored daily close of the expiry date; simple arithmetic, no pricing model. It applies from the close of the option session of the expiry date until 3 calendar days after that date, after the portfolio mark and the fallback mark, and is reported as `PRICE_EXPIRY_INTRINSIC`; a long option stays `PRICE_MISSING`. A read that uses it is an estimate: it is not calibrated (`CALIBRATION_SKIPPED_EXPIRY`) and a comparison of the margin rules must leave it out (ADR 0014).
- **Calibration**: comparison of local maintenance with broker `MaintMarginReq`; underestimation by more than 10% is UNKNOWN `MODEL_MISCALIBRATED`, while higher local estimates are acceptable (ADR 0013).
- **SMA**: broker-reported Special Memorandum Account, retained as a read-only summary value for ADR 0013's Reg-T assignment gate.
- **Broker room**: post-candidate ExcessLiquidity compared with buffered NLV, also requiring non-negative post-candidate AvailableFunds; `BrokerRoomResult.af_after` exposes the latter in USD (ADR 0013).
- **接股后借款（总额）** (borrowing after assignment, total): per account and currency, `max(0, ΣK × q × mult − CashBalance)` over every held short put of that currency assigned at once, in that currency; never added across accounts or currencies (ADR 0013, 2026-10-21).
- **新增借款** (added borrowing): the part of 接股后借款（总额） that is new: the total less today's debit `max(0, −CashBalance)`; same currency (ADR 0013, 2026-10-21).
- **政策估算余量** (policy room after assignment, `Y`): `Room₁ / NLV₀` with `Room₁ = EL₀ + ΔELV − 0.30 × f × q × mult`, `f = min(P, K)`, every short put of a Reg T margin account assigned in a 0 % scenario; a policy estimate, not the broker's figure and distinct from **Broker room**; red below 10 %, amber below 15 % (ADR 0013, 2026-10-21).
- **Stressed equity**: NLV plus stock, option, cash and candidate value changes under simultaneous underlying decline and JPY appreciation, converted to USD at the scenario rates (ADR 0013).
- **Stressed requirement**: maximum of broker MaintMarginReq and scenario stock maintenance plus option policy reserves, in USD (ADR 0013).
- **Equity with loan value**: the broker's own ELV, taken as ExcessLiquidity + MaintMarginReq in USD: cash, stock and non-US option value; US option value is not in it (ADR 0013).
- **Sizing stress**: ADR 0013's fifth gate, `SIZING_STRESS`: after every underlying falls 30% and JPY appreciates 10%, with each short put in the money after the fall taking the worse of "assigned" and "left open", stressed equity with loan value less stressed maintenance must be at least 20% of the stressed base; USD, Reg-T only, parameters in `accounts/params/sizing_stress_v1.toml`. A put credit spread candidate is counted as an open equal-expiry pair of the book is: its width × multiplier × contracts joins the stressed maintenance, and neither the equity nor the base changes.
- **Forgotten-exit stress**: the gate `FORGOTTEN_EXIT_STRESS` of a put credit spread candidate, asked beside the sizing stress; both must hold. It is the sizing stress with every underlying falling 15% (`accounts/params/spread_stress_v1.toml`; every other figure is the sizing-stress parameter set's) and with every short put contract the book pairs with a long put of the same expiry, and the candidate's short leg, assigned whatever its moneyness: with `f = min(underlying after the fall, strike)`, equity with loan value and the stressed base change by `−(strike − f) × multiplier × contracts` and the stressed maintenance grows by 30% of `f × multiplier × contracts`. No width is counted and the long legs give no help; unpaired short puts are treated as in the sizing stress. It reserves money as if the exit was forgotten and the shares arrived; it fails with `FORGOTTEN_EXIT_STRESS_FAILED`, and equal passes.
- **Stressed base**: stressed equity with loan value with every short put in the money after the fall assigned and every other put left open, whatever treatment the sizing stress chose for its test; USD (ADR 0013).
- **Expiry scenario**: a display figure of one account (`accounts/scenario.py`, ADR 0013 note of 2026-10-10), not a gate: net liquidation in USD after every price falls by `drop` at once, JPY appreciates by `fx_shock`, and everything stays there until every option held has expired. With `S′ = S × (1 − drop)`, `i′` the intrinsic value at `S′`, `r` today's USD rate of the currency and `r′` the shocked one: stock changes by `q × p × ((1 − drop) × r′ − r)`; a long option and a short call by `q × m × (i′ × r′ − p × r)`; a short put outside a pair by `q × m × (max(i′, p) × r′ − p × r)`, never a gain in its own currency; the contracts of a pair of "Put spread (margin)" whose legs are both held and expire on the same day by `contracts × m × (a × r′ − n × r)` with `n = p_long − p_short` and `a = min(i′_long − i′_short, n)`; cash by `cash × (r′ − r)`. An option expired before the day and any other security type do not move, and are counted or listed. It is an expiry payoff, not a liquidation value: marks right after a fall are worse, and margin is the sizing stress's subject. No pricing model. Shown on a page, a position or cash balance with an unknown input is left out and named, and the figure says it is partial; the net liquidation or a snapshot the gates would not read still gives no number.
- **Margin-gone band**: a display figure, not a gate: the two falls between which the sizing-stress gate's remaining excess liquidity turns negative, asked at 5 %, 10 %, … 60 %: `(lower, upper)` with `upper` the first fall whose value is below zero and `lower` the step before it; `(60 %, none)` when none is; unknown when a value is unknown before that. A band, never one number.
- **Assignment by expiry**: a display figure, not a gate: per currency and expiry day, ascending, the cumulative gross put notional of every put expiring on or before that day, beside that currency's cash as given, with `gap = max(0, cumulative − max(cash, 0))` (unknown when the cash is). The last step of a currency is its part of the total assignment value before conversion. Nothing is converted or added across currencies. On a page, a put row whose amount cannot be known is left out and named, and the amounts are then marked as bounds (put amount at least, cash after assignment at most); a currency stands alone.
- **Largest name**: a display figure, not a gate: among the underlyings of which the account holds stock or short puts, the one with the largest exposure as the concentration rule counts it without a candidate (stock value, puts at strike, working stock buys, in USD), on a tie the first in the alphabet; with its `share` = exposure ÷ net liquidation and its `loss`, the change of the expiry scenario when that name alone falls 50 % and no exchange rate moves. On a page, a name whose exposure cannot be counted is left out and named; the name shown is then the largest of those counted.
- **SMA assignment debit**: stressed assignment debit for every in-the-money short put and candidate, with no paired-long credit, deducted from Reg-T SMA in USD (ADR 0013).
- **margin_regime**: optional per-alias declaration `reg_t` or `portfolio`, allowed only with `margin_type = margin`; missing loads successfully and later margin gates report UNKNOWN `MARGIN_REGIME_UNDECLARED` (ADR 0013).
- **Headroom**: cash available minus put reservations, working BUY commitments including the option-order fee allowance, and long-call exercise reserve, per account and currency; unknown if any term is unknown.
- **Capital status**: HOLD if any rule definitely fails, otherwise UNKNOWN if any rule cannot be evaluated, otherwise OK; all reasons are retained (ADR 0011).
- **Room**: the largest whole number of contracts of one put, from 0 to the search ceiling, for which one limit passes with every smaller number passing too. It is found by asking the existing check, never by a second formula. An unknown room carries no number. The one-contract floor can make the issuer cap's room 1 where the search gives 0.
- **Fit**: the smallest room over the limits that apply to one account: sizing stress, total assignment value, issuer cap, spanning cap, sector cap, settled cash. It is a ceiling for one account and one contract, not a recommended size. Fits of several offers are alternatives, not additive. The weekly pace is not applied yet, and every fit says so.
- **Put count (N)**: how many contracts of one put an account can take, with its basis (`accounts/put_count.py`). Basis `margin` for an account in ADR 0013's scope (declared `margin`, type check `OK` or `STRUCTURE`): the largest number from 0 to the fit ceiling for which every ADR 0013 gate passes (broker room, stress, sizing stress, SMA, calibration and concentration), found by the fit search; the option page calls it 融资额度. Basis `cash` for every other account: available cash // (strike × multiplier) (ADR 0011). Unknown when any required input is unknown, never a number. Unlike a fit it applies no assignment-total, spanning or floor rule.
- **Total assignment value**: the account's gross put notional in base currency, plus the candidate. At most 100 % of net liquidation in a margin account and in a cash account (owner decision 2026-10-08, fit version 3; the margin limit was 50 % under the decision of 2026-10-07). A margin account also has the assignment warning line. A put spread counts with its full short strike.
- **Assignment warning line**: for a margin account, 75 % of net liquidation (`assignment_warn_margin`, owner decision 2026-10-08). The warning room is the room of the total assignment value asked with the line instead of the limit; equal passes. It is not a limit: it never changes a fit's number, status or binding limits, and the page only says when the fit would cross it. A cash account has none.
- **One-contract floor**: for a margin account and a put that does not span results (owner decision 2026-10-08). When one contract alone takes an issuer that was within its issuer cap over it, the issuer cap's room is 1 (note `ONE_CONTRACT_FLOOR`) if the issuer's exposure with that contract is at most `issuer_floor_cap` (15 %) of net liquidation, equal passes, and fewer than `issuer_floor_slots` (2) other issuers of the account use the floor. An issuer uses the floor when it has a short put position or a working SELL put in the account and its exposure without the candidate is above the issuer cap; stock alone does not count. Otherwise the room stays 0 with `ISSUER_FLOOR_EXCEEDED` or `ISSUER_FLOOR_SLOTS_USED`; an unknown amount leaves the room unknown. Never more than one contract, never for an issuer already over its cap, never for the spanning cap; every other limit still applies.
- **Spanning cap**: the limit `SPANNING_CAP` of a put that spans results, in one account and for the put's issuer (owner decision 2026-10-08): the put principal of the issuer's puts that span the results, plus the candidate's short strike × multiplier × contracts (a spread is counted at its short strike here, not at its width), must not exceed `spanning_cap_share` (0.50) × the account's issuer cap × net liquidation; equal passes, else HOLD `SPANNING_CAP_EXCEEDED`.
  - Counted: short put positions and working SELL put orders of that account, symbol and currency, at strike × multiplier × contracts, when their expiry is on or after the first day the results may come (the confirmed date, the `used` vendor results date, or else the day gate 4 compared with the expiry: the first day of the estimated earnings window, or an earlier vendor results date outside it), converted to the base currency with the ledger exchange rate.
  - Not counted: stock, working stock buys and puts that expire before that day; they stay in the issuer cap, which is unchanged.
  - Unknown, never "not spanning": a put position or working SELL put order of the issuer without an expiry, or a candidate without one, is UNKNOWN `EXPIRY_MISSING`; a missing exchange rate is UNKNOWN.
  - It applies to every account type, and is N/A for a put that does not span results.
- **Covered call (CC)**: short call against shares already held, one contract per 100 shares.
  - Strategy `covered_call` (ADR 0020): the rule judges one call, sold against shares the owner already holds, and only advises. Shares held, cost, pools and the number of contracts are not in it. Parameters in `strategies/params/covered_call_v1.toml`.
  - **Run-up reminder (display)**: the `涨幅过大` chip on a US sell-put card. `MovingFigures.return_20` = the newest close ÷ the value of the point 20 positions before the newest in the points, minus 1, unrounded; None with fewer than 21 points or when that point is None or 0. A card is *run up* when `close_vs_ma50` ≥ 0.12 (definition A) or `return_20` ≥ 0.15 (definition B), the provisional levels of `backtest/extended.py`; a level reached exactly counts. It is a reminder only: no verdict, grade, size, tenor or gate reads it, until a backtest says otherwise. Not shown on JP cards, spreads or the option page.
- **Sale price**: the owner's price per share, in the contract's currency, at which the owner is willing to sell the shares. With one, the rule looks for a call with a strike at or above it, at 30 to 45 DTE first and 21 to 29 DTE after; without one there is no offer, only a question with at most three candidate prices. The tool never picks the price. It is a market-only input of the rule; where it is recorded is not part of the rule.
  - **If assigned**: strike + net premium / multiplier, per share and before tax: what one share brings when the call is assigned. `if_assigned_to_spot` = (if assigned − spot) / spot, a decimal fraction. Display only, per contract and per account: the gain over the owner's cost is (strike − average cost per share) × multiplier × contracts, shown beside the premium `premium_net` × contracts as `被叫走可得`; the average cost is the account snapshot's `avg_cost` of the stock position (IBKR reports it per share). No pricing model.
  - **Premium floor**: with a sale price, a net premium below `premium_floor` (0.005) × spot × multiplier is "too far this month" (`PREMIUM_BELOW_FLOOR`); equal passes. `premium_to_stock` = net premium / (spot × multiplier), a decimal fraction; annualised by 365 / DTE it is the premium yield on share value.
  - **Excess line (US)**: display only, an offer row's page line prints `annual_yield` − the scan's `cash_rate` (the 13-week Treasury rate the US rule read for N(d₂)) in points, two decimals half-up and signed; the rule stores no US excess.
  - **Near-spot mark**: `near_spot`, true when strike < spot × (1 + 0.03). A mark, not a gate: the call is shown and the owner decides.
  - Results before the expiry (`spans_results`: true, false, or unknown without a trustworthy date) and an ex-dividend date after today and on or before the expiry are marks too; neither refuses a call.
- **Sale price**: the price per share, in the stock's currency, at which the owner accepts to sell a stated number of shares of one stock (`shares`, a positive multiple of 100), with a reason sentence; entered with `sale-approve` and kept append-only in `sale_price` (ADR 0019). It is the owner's decision and is never derived by the tool. Only the newest row of a stock counts (by entry time, then id); a withdrawal is a new row and an older row never comes back. Its state is `IN_FORCE`, `WITHDRAWN`, `NONE` (no row) or `RECONFIRM`, the last with its reasons: `AGED` (entered more than 45 days ago; exactly 45 days still stands) and `RESULTS_PASSED` (the US calendar date is after the next results date known at entry; on that date it still stands). The row keeps the latest stored daily close at entry and its date; a price at or below that close is refused. US stocks only in the first version. Distinct from the approved put price (`maximum_assignment_price` of the acquisition policy, the highest strike at which the owner accepts assignment) and from the watchlist's `call_exit_price`. Nothing reads it yet.
- **Spread-covered call**: a short call paired with a long call of the same account,
  underlying and currency (USD, multiplier 100, margin account), with long strike ≤
  short strike and long expiry ≥ short expiry.
- **Cash available**: per-account, per-currency ledger cash less negative accrued cash and buffered debt in other currencies (ADR 0011). Declared margin accounts whose `AccountType` cross-check is `OK` or `STRUCTURE` use trade-date cash without a settlement bound, labelled `SETTLEMENT_NOT_BOUND`; cash and retirement accounts keep the settled bound, as do undeclared accounts and failed type cross-checks. Margin capacity and unposted positive accruals add nothing; missing required inputs are UNKNOWN, never zero. This is before reservations.
- **Settled bound**: the whole account-level unsettled amount charged against every tested currency using ledger exchange rates; deliberately conservative. Actual per-currency SettledCash replaces this bound when available.
- **Reservation**: what an account's positions and working orders already commit, per account alias and currency (shares: per alias, underlying and currency), never summed across either (ADR 0006).
  - Cash reserved for puts: strike × multiplier × contracts over short puts and working SELL put orders.
  - **Long-call exercise reserve**: E = Σ strike × multiplier × contracts over long call positions of the alias and currency in every account type, expiring on or before the put candidate's expiry (all long calls when expiry is unknown, and in the report). Working closing SELLs give no credit; pending BUYs are already in working buys. Headroom = available − P − W − E.
  - Cash committed by working BUY orders: limit × quantity (× multiplier for options),
    bounded only for LMT and STP LMT. Other BUY types are unknown (`order_unbounded`);
    a bounded BUY without a limit is unknown (`missing_limit`).
  - Shares reserved for calls: multiplier × contracts over short calls and working SELL call orders, against long shares less working SELL stock orders.
  - Working orders count `remaining`, or the total quantity when it is unknown. Pending closing orders get no credit. An unknown input makes the total unknown, never zero.
  - Working orders are de-duplicated by account alias and permId; identical rows count
    once. Conflicting rows make every total of that account unknown
    (`order_duplicate_conflict`). The reservation report exposes conflicted aliases
    even when no rows exist; absent rows for these accounts are unknown, never zero.
    Unassigned permId 0 orders each count.
- **Estimated fill**: the bid for a sale. It is an estimate, not a guaranteed fill.
- **Net premium**: estimated fill × multiplier − opening fees. Closing and assignment fees are not included and are shown separately when known.
- **Effective cost basis** (CSP assigned): strike − net premium per share.
- **Premium yield on reserved cash** (CSP): net premium / reserved cash × 365 / DTE.
- **Premium yield on share value** (CC): net premium / (underlying price × multiplier) × 365 / DTE.
- Neither yield is an expected return, and neither denominator is the maximum loss.
- **Theoretical value**: model price from stated inputs (model, volatility source, rate, dividends). Pricing with market IV only reproduces the market price, so a "cheap/expensive" claim must name a different volatility.
- **Snapshot plan**: the ordered option contracts one snapshot slot asks for (plan v4, ADR 0003), with 21 to 45 DTE, in three groups: `around_spot` (both rights at the listed strikes just below and just above spot), `puts` and `calls` (the other strikes of the strike range). Names take turns nearest to the money first, the first name rotating by day and slot; the items of a group past its `max_items` ceiling are deferred.
- **Within tolerance**: a slot outcome (ADR 0005, note of 2026-10-07). A snapshot slot with missing option items is still `succeeded` when no name was skipped, every stock quote is `ok`, every option item that is not `ok` is `no_data` (a contract without a usable quote), those items are at most `max_missing_share` (5 %) of the slot's option items, and no name misses more than `max_missing_share_per_side` (50 %) of its puts or of its calls. Both limits are inclusive, versioned parameters (`outcome_v1.toml`) and compared exactly on the counts; `auth_required` and `capacity` are decided first. The page has no banner; the header's run status and the snapshot section's note state the number missing, its share, the limit and the worst name and side.
- **Listed contract**: an option contract IBKR confirmed to exist for one (underlying, expiry), stored with its `con_id` and fetch time; chain definitions say what may exist, listed contracts say what does (ADR 0003).
- **Strike range**: listed strikes from `spot · exp(−k·σ·√T)` up to `spot · exp(+k·σ·√T)`, both ends inclusive, with σ = IV30 (annualised decimal), `T = DTE / 365` and `k = 1.5`; `spot · (1 ∓ 0.30)` when σ is unknown. Puts are taken at or below spot, calls above it. For a US name on the spread list the lower end of the puts is lowered by `spread_depth` (0.15) × spot (deep puts, plan v4).
- **Spread list**: the owner's list of names on which put credit spreads are offered, each with a kind: *own it, one contract too large* (`own_too_large`) or *premium only* (`premium_only`). A local, versioned owner file (`spreads.toml`), like the limits file; a missing file means no spreads. The snapshot plan collects deep puts for its US names. The file is now one of two sources of the spread manifest; the other is the pool tag `spread_only_pool`.
- **Spread manifest**: the one list of put credit spread names merged from the pool manifest and the spread list file (ADR 0021 note of 2026-10-09), US stocks only: a stock whose pool is `spread_only_pool` is `premium_only` by its tag, and a file entry counts with its kind when the pool table allows it for the stock; an entry the table refuses is left out and named with a reason (`UNTAGGED`, `CONFLICT:…`, `NOT_ALLOWED:<pool>`). `known` holds every name whatever the import's age (the kind of an open spread), with a SHA-256 over the manifest version and the sorted `symbol=kind` pairs; `offered` is `known`, or no name when the pool manifest is `EXPIRED`. The US snapshot slot and the commands `spread-kind-show` and `spread-kind-confirm` use it: `offered` gives the deep-put names and the names of the spread scan, `known` the list the kind record is judged against. When the owner's pool file exists its manifest is the pool source in place of the import's: the list's version then starts `pools:file`, a stock the file gives no options is left out with the reason `NO_OPTIONS`, and under a refused file nothing is offered while `known` keeps the spread list file's names.
- **Put credit spread**: one put sold and a lower put of the same underlying, expiry and multiplier bought, as one structure (strategy `put_credit_spread`): legs −1 at the short strike and +1 at the long strike. Offered on the names of the spread list in its two kinds; the rule only advises. Width = short strike − long strike, per share. `premium_only`: the short put has an absolute delta of 0.15 to 0.25 and 30 to 45 DTE, the width is 3 % to 10 % of spot, and the spread is closed by its close-by date. `own_too_large`: the short put passes every short-put gate, the owner's approved price included, the width is 5 % to 15 % of spot, and nothing is closed early. Parameters in `strategies/params/put_credit_spread_v2.toml` (owner decision of 2026-10-19: a minimum credit ÷ width of 0.12 on both kinds, a pair under it rejected with `CREDIT_BELOW_WIDTH_SHARE`; version 1 held 0.10 for `premium_only` and no floor for `own_too_large`; scans stored under version 1 still restore). It is the offer of a new structure; "Put spread (margin)" is the pairing of positions already held.
- **Spread verdict** (`strategies/spread_verdict.py`; display only, stored nowhere, computed at page build; no rate): one word per put credit spread pair, for both kinds, with the reasons. The first matching rule decides: a stale quote is 价格过期 (`STALE_PRICE`); a rejected pair is 不做 (`SKIP`); an unknown evaluation is 待定; a pass whose mid credit or (for `own_too_large`) preferred range is missing is 待定; any marginal reason makes it 勉强; otherwise 值得看, which only means no strained condition was found. Marginal reasons: credit ÷ width from the floor 0.12 to below 0.14 (`CREDIT_WIDTH_MARGINAL`, `verdict_spread_v1.toml`); for `own_too_large`, a short strike above the preferred range (`STRIKE_ABOVE_PREFERRED`); worst ÷ mid below the owner's line (`QUOTE_RATIO_MARGINAL`). A value equal to a threshold passes. Worst ÷ mid (worst-case credit ÷ mid credit, unrounded) is the owner's parameter `worst_to_mid` in `verdict_spread_owner_v1.toml`, absent while undecided; the card then says so. The long leg's cost and IV are printed facts, not a rule.
- **Protection share (spread)**: long put ask ÷ short put bid, a decimal (0.50 = 50 %), the cost of the protection as a share of the credit collected on the short leg (`reports/spread_section.py`, `protection_share`). No number when either quote is missing or the short bid is not positive. Display only; the card shows it rounded down to one decimal of a percent.
- **IV gap (spread)**: long put IV − short put IV, in volatility points (decimal fraction, 0.02 = 2 points), half-even (`iv_gap`). Positive when the bought leg is the richer. No number when either IV is missing. Display only.
- **Worst-case credit**: short put bid − long put ask, per share: what the spread collects when both legs fill at the far side of their quotes. Known only with both quotes usable, a positive short bid and a positive long ask. The **mid credit** is short mid − long mid. **Net credit** = worst-case credit × multiplier − 2 × fee per contract (the opening fees of both legs), per one spread in the contract's currency. An estimate, not a guaranteed fill.
- **Max loss (spread)**: width × multiplier − net credit, per one spread in the contract's currency: the loss at expiry with the underlying at or below the long strike. Closing fees, assignment fees and an early assignment of the short leg are not in it. The spread's breakeven is short strike − net credit / multiplier, per share.
- **Return on max loss (spread)**: net credit ÷ max loss (spread) × 365 ÷ DTE, a ratio (0.10 = 10 %), six places half-even (`reports/spread_section.py`, `spread_return_on_max_loss`): the yield on the money at risk, annualised on 365 days. It is over max loss rather than over the short strike, and it is not the `return_on_max_loss` of a grade. No number when either amount is unknown, either is zero or negative, or DTE is zero or negative. Display only: plain arithmetic on the stored facts, no pricing model; no offer, size, tenor, close reminder or gate reads it.
- **Period return on max loss (spread)**: net credit ÷ max loss (spread), a ratio, six places half-even (`spread_period_return_on_max_loss`): the same yield over the spread's own period, not annualised. No number when either amount is unknown, zero or negative. Display only: plain arithmetic on the stored facts, no pricing model; nothing else reads it.
- **Close-by date**: the date by which a `premium_only` put credit spread is to be closed, after today and on or before the expiry, given to the rule by its caller. The scan sets it to the fifth trading date before the expiry (`close_by_sessions`; owner decision of 2026-10-08). `own_too_large` has none. A fact of the offer (`exit` = `close_by` or `none`); the tool never closes anything.
- **Spread loss amount**: of an open put spread pair or of a put credit spread candidate, `(short strike − long strike) × multiplier × contracts`, in the account's base currency (converted with the ledger exchange rate, as the concentration check converts). The credit is never subtracted, so it is larger than "Max loss (spread)". Simple arithmetic, no pricing model. An **open spread** of an account is a pair of "Put spread (margin)" whose two expiries are equal; its kind (`premium_only`, `own_too_large`) is given by the caller, and a pair whose kind is not known counts against both kinds.
- **Spread fit**: the fit of one put credit spread candidate in one account: the smallest room over the stress limits passed in and the spread's limits (`accounts/spread_limits.py`, parameters in `accounts/params/spread_limits_v1.toml`, ADR 0018 note of 2026-10-09), searched to the file's `ceiling` for `premium_only` and to `own_too_large.contracts` (1) for `own_too_large`. The limits, in order: `SPREAD_MARGIN_ACCOUNT` (a margin account only), `ASSIGNMENT_TOTAL`, `ISSUER_CAP` (`premium_only`, without the one-contract floor) and `SECTOR_CAP` asked with the candidate as puts at its short strike, `SPREAD_ISSUER_EXPOSURE` (`own_too_large`: the issuer's exposure with those puts at most 20 % of net liquidation, 10 % when the contract spans results, and no short put position or working SELL put on the issuer), `SPREAD_NAME_LOSS` (spread loss amount on the symbol: 0.5 % / 0.25 % spanning for `premium_only`, 2 % / 1 % for `own_too_large`), `SPREAD_NAMES` (5 names for `premium_only`; 2 for `own_too_large`, shared with the issuers that use the one-contract floor), `SPREAD_KIND_LOSS` (`premium_only`: 2.5 %) and `SPREAD_ALL_LOSS` (every open spread: 5 %). Equal passes; an unknown amount leaves no number. A ceiling for one account and one candidate, not a recommended size; the weekly pace is not applied.
- **Spread kind record**: the append-only table `spread_kind` that says which of the two kinds an open put credit spread is, keyed by account alias and the contract id of the short put; the latest row of a key counts. A `list` row is written the first time a filled equal-expiry pair of the key is seen, with the kind the spread list gave the symbol that day (`LISTED`), or without a kind (`NOT_LISTED`, or `OWNER_ROW_ON_OTHER_CONTRACT` when the owner confirmed another short contract of the same alias, symbol and expiry); an `owner` row is the owner's confirmation with a reason sentence. Each row keeps the lowest paired long strike and the paired contracts of that moment, and the SHA-256 of the list file; a missing list file writes no row. Rows are never updated, deleted or pruned. The effective kind of an open spread has one of four states.
  - `RECORDED`: the latest row is a `list` row with a kind and today's list still gives the symbol that kind; the kind is the row's.
  - `CONFIRMED`: the latest row is an `owner` row, the lowest paired long strike is still the row's and the paired contracts (working SELL orders' pairs included) are not above the row's; the kind is the row's, whatever today's list says.
  - `UNCONFIRMED`: the latest row no longer settles the kind (a `list` row without a kind or whose kind today's list no longer gives, also when the list file is missing; an `owner` row whose long strike moved or whose contracts grew); the kind is unknown until the owner confirms.
  - `NO_ROW`: the key has no row yet; the kind is unknown.
  - **Close reminder** (`close_reminder`): true for `premium_only`, false for `own_too_large`; for an unknown kind false only when some row of the key says `own_too_large` and none says `premium_only`, true otherwise. The tool never closes anything.
- **Open structure money line** (`accounts/structure_loss.py`): one line per open structure of `accounts/open_structures.py`, in the structure's own currency, never summed across currencies or accounts. `Q` = contracts × multiplier; `P` = the sum of the legs' per-unit signed premiums (credit +, debit −), each the open leg's broker average cost per contract ÷ multiplier (**按开仓价**), with a short's sign flipped. A missing premium gives the width term (width_loss × Q) only, with 权利金缺失. One footnote per block: 上界按开仓价，不含手续费、提前行权与长腿时间价值变化.
  - **最大亏损**: the exact loss at expiry of a vertical, max(0, width_loss − P) × Q, width_loss = max(K_long − K_short, 0) for calls, max(K_short − K_long, 0) for puts; of a single long, 已付权利金 × Q.
  - **上界**: a bound to a named date, 按开仓价: max(0, width_loss − P) × Q to the short's expiry for a diagonal or calendar whose later long is American (未定义 for a European or unknown style); and a vertical's 0 when the premium covers the width.
  - **净权利金**: signed, per share: **净收** (net received, +) or **净付** (net paid, −).
  - **下限 / 上限**: a collar's put and call strikes, per share; no maximum loss (the stock basis is not in it).
  - **等同股数**: the shares a synthetic stands for: a synthetic long is 等同 Q 股 @ (K − P), a synthetic short 等同空 Q 股 with an unbounded loss.
  - Expiry: shown with the year when it is not the current year.

## Watchlist (manual valuation input)

One entry per underlying, maintained by the owner:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `fair_value_low`, `fair_value_high` | Valuation range per share. |
| `put_entry_price` | Highest **strike** at which the owner is willing to be assigned. A put is a candidate only if `strike ≤ put_entry_price`. The effective cost basis is shown alongside. |
| `call_exit_price` | Lowest **strike** at which the owner is willing to sell shares. A call is a candidate only if `strike ≥ call_exit_price`. |
| `margin_of_safety` | Required discount, as a decimal, of `put_entry_price` below `fair_value_low`. A violation is a validation warning. |
| `budget` | Maximum capital in this underlying: market value of shares held plus cash reserved for short puts, including the candidate. |
| `valued_on`, `valid_until` | Valuation date and expiry. Entries past `valid_until` are flagged as stale. |
| `notes` | Free text. |

- **Pool**: what the owner wants with a stock, read from the strategy tags of the watchlist import: `core_pool` (held for the long term), `tradable_pool` or `spread_only_pool` (the shares are not wanted). The pool says which new option offers the stock may get, by one table (ADR 0021); `tradable_pool` with `spread_only_pool` counts as tradable. A stock without a strategy tag is `untagged` and one whose tags do not give one pool is a `conflict` with a reason; neither gets an offer. Positions are managed whatever the pool.
- **Pool manifest**: the resolved pools of the active members of the newest watchlist import, with the import's id, its `exported_at` and `imported_at`, the gate version and a SHA-256 over the gate version, the import id and the members. Its freshness counts the whole days since `exported_at`: more than 7 is `OLD` (a warning, it still gates), more than 21 is `EXPIRED` (no new offer), and so is nothing imported; parameters in `strategies/params/pools_v1.toml`. Applied to short-put offers: the page, its headline and the shown offers leave out the stocks the gate does not allow. Applied to the covered-call section: a card is drawn only for a stock the gate allows a covered call on. Spreads do not ask it yet.
- **Pool file**: the owner's local file of the decision per stock (`~/.config/option-trader/pools.toml`, ADR 0022): the pool the stock is in, or `none` for no options, watch only. When it exists it is the one source of the pool manifest for every page, the spread manifest and the commands, with no age limit: a stock it does not name is `untagged`, a rejected entry is a `conflict` of its own stock, and a refused file gives no new offer of any strategy. The file's stocks are the ones exported and scanned: the watchlist export selects the assets whose symbol is in the file (`pools-symbols`), and the import says where the file and the member table disagree (`no_facts`, `no_entry`, `not_linked`). **Reconciliation lines**: up to three notes of the short-put page under the file, which block nothing: the entries with a pool whose stock has no company facts (`no_facts`, never scanned), the active stocks without an entry (`undecided`, no offer) and how many decisions are older than `review_months` (12) calendar months (`review`). **List changes**: the canonical body of every distinct manifest of the file is kept once under its hash (`pool_manifest_body`), and the short-put page says what was added, removed or changed in its market against the list of the newest page recorded `change_days` (7) days before or earlier, which blocks nothing.
- **Follow list**: names the owner watches and nothing else, read from a text file exported from the owner's IBKR watchlist or written by hand (`follow-import`). Symbols are separated by newlines, commas or tabs; `NASDAQ:AAPL` keeps its exchange as given; a 4-digit code, with or without `.T`, is market JP with the bare code, anything else US. Stored in `follow_member` of the instruments database per `source` (default `ibkr-watchlist`); an import marks the source's names missing from the new list inactive and never deletes a row. Watched only: no pool, option scan, report or order reads it until the owner moves a name into the pool file.

## Facts (ADR 0008)

- **Reverse DCF**: equity cash-flow proxy with fixed shares and zero net borrowing, discounting year-end forecast flows and a growing perpetuity to value per share; annual discount and growth rates are decimals.
- **Implied growth**: constant annual FCF growth required by a supplied price under the stated reverse DCF assumptions; unavailable when the price is outside the solver range. It is solved once per US snapshot job, after the snapshot and before the report, from the slot's spot rounded to cents, per discount rate, and stored permanently (`implied_growth/1`); the slot report renders the stored rows.
- **Terminal share**: terminal present value divided by total present value in reverse DCF, expressed as a decimal fraction.
- **Fact**: one reported value from SEC companyfacts, keyed by CIK, taxonomy, concept, unit, period start (none for instant facts), period end and accession. It keeps the form, filed date, retrieval time and raw-response hash. Values are exact decimals. `fy`/`fp` never decide its period.
- **As-of rule**: the fact for a period as of a cutoff date is the one with the latest filed date on or before the cutoff. None → N/A `missing`; different values on that latest filed date → N/A `conflict`. N/A is never zero.
- **FCF yield draft** (method `fcf_yield/1`): a Valuation Draft with FCF = operating cash flow − capital expenditure − stock-based compensation, as reported, per fiscal year. Value per share (USD) = median FCF over the latest 5 fiscal years ÷ diluted weighted-average shares of the latest year ÷ required yield (decimal), quantized to `0.0001` half-even. Parameters live in the packaged resource `src/option_trader/valuation/params/fcf_yield_v1.toml`; `--params PATH` overrides it. Incomplete or conflicting inputs reject the draft; it is never a candidate input.
- **Reverse DCF draft** (method `reverse_dcf/1`): a price-free Valuation Draft holding a grid of value per share (USD) by annual discount rate and growth rate (decimals); stored in its own tables with two parameter hashes, it has no primary value and is never read as a price.
- **Floor DCF value** (method `floor_dcf/1`): one conservative value per share (USD) from the latest 5 fiscal years of total revenue and FCF (operating cash flow − capital expenditure − stock-based compensation), priced by the reverse DCF formula at each discount rate; the value at the primary rate is the result. A mechanical estimate, not a forecast.
  - Base (USD) = latest revenue × min(median FCF margin of the 5 years, mean FCF margin of the latest 2 years).
  - Growth (annual decimal) = `growth_share` (0.75) × the slower of the 4-year and 2-year compound annual revenue growth, capped at `growth_cap` (0.08) and never below 0.
  - Outcomes: **rejected** (no number: incomplete years, unknown or non-positive revenue, unknown or financial SIC code, stale latest year, non-positive base); **blocked** (a number is shown but must not be approved: cyclical SIC code, a revenue drop of more than 10% in one year, fewer than 4 years of positive FCF); otherwise a **value**.
  - Prior claims: a preferred dividend paid in cash, or cash paid to noncontrolling holders above `prior_claim_share` (0.05) of the base, is a **rejection** (`prior_claims`).
  - More blocks: a preferred dividend declared or charged to income but not confirmed as paid; income attributable to noncontrolling holders above `minority_income_share` (0.05) of consolidated income, or with consolidated income unknown or not positive.
  - Acquisition cap: when acquisitions (the larger of cash paid over the 5 years and the rise in goodwill plus impairments) are positive and either exceed `acquisition_share` (0.5) of the 5-year FCF sum or that sum is not positive, growth is also capped at `acquired_growth_cap` (0.03) and flagged `growth_bought`.
  - Flags: `growth_bought` marks the capped growth; the others are shown and have no effect on the value: `preferred_adjustments`, `preferred_outstanding`, `growth_paid_with_shares` (diluted shares up more than `share_rise`, 0.12, over the 5 years) and `share_count_anomaly` (a one-year change beyond ± `share_jump`, 0.40).
  - A figure whose tag the company's filings do not carry is "none tagged": not zero and not unknown; it never rejects, blocks or flags, and the result lists it.
  - Check figures: each is the largest as-of value among the concepts listed for it in `floor_dcf_checks_v1.toml`, which describe the same amount and are never added; the only sum is the two parts of the cash paid to noncontrolling holders, compared with their total.
  - Conflicts: a check figure with a conflict rejects the draft (`input_conflict:<concept>:<period_end>`); for the two figures that can only raise a flag (`preferred_adjustments`, `preferred_outstanding`) the figure is none and that text is a flag.
  - Share change: chain-linked. Each one-year ratio of diluted shares takes both years from one filing (the latest that carries both), so a stock split is not a jump; the series is anchored at the selected share count. Any ratio unknown → the change is unknown (`share_change_unknown`).
  - Draft-level blocks: `revenue_restated` (a year's revenue differs from its first report by more than the fundamental history's tolerance) and `shares_unverified` (no cover-page share count, so a split after the year end cannot be ruled out).
  - Draft-level flags: `revenue_concept_mixed`, `share_change_unknown` and the `input_conflict:…` flags; they have no effect on the value.
  - Parameters live in the packaged resource `src/option_trader/valuation/params/floor_dcf_v1.toml`; the concept lists in `floor_dcf_checks_v1.toml` beside it.
  - Stored draft: one row of `floor_dcf_draft` per changed content; its blocks and value are read from the hashed text, never from a column (ADR 0008).
  - Current: what is displayed for a stock: its rejection when one exists (a rejection hides every older draft), else its newest stored draft.
  - Approvable: the newest stored draft of a stock with no rejection and no blocks; the only draft an approval may cite.
  - Check row: one row per stock in `floor_dcf_check` with the time (UTC) the refresh last evaluated it, the company's latest successful SEC fetch at that moment and the outcome (`drafted`, `unchanged` or `rejected`); it tells a draft confirmed last night from one the refresh has not looked at.
- **Own record** (method `own_record`, version 2): the annual growth (decimal) of a company's reported operating cash flow over a ten-fiscal-year window, as of a cutoff: `exp(ln(end / start) / 10) − 1` between two fiscal years ten apart with every year between them consecutive and both values above zero. It gives the best window (largest `end / start`) and the latest window (ending at the newest fiscal year). It is total operating cash flow, not per share and not after capital expenditure or stock-based compensation. Context for the reader at approval; not a valuation, not a forecast, not a gate.
  - Concept order: `NetCashProvidedByUsedInOperatingActivities` first, then `NetCashProvidedByUsedInOperatingActivitiesContinuingOperations`; the two are never added together.
  - Periods: the union of each concept's annual periods, in that order. A period of a later concept is added only when no earlier concept has a period ending within 300 days of its end, so one fiscal year is never counted twice; sorted by period end, the latest `max_periods` (40) are kept.
  - Values: a period's value is the as-of fact of the concept that gave the period. A period given by the first concept never takes its value from a later one: when its fact is unusable (conflict, no value) the period counts and its value is unusable.
  - **Fallback period**: a period that came from a concept other than the first; `fallback_period_ends` lists their period ends, and `best_uses_fallback` says the best window starts or ends at one.
  - `discontinued_period_ends`: the fallback periods for which a usable, non-zero fact of `CashProvidedByUsedInOperatingActivitiesDiscontinuedOperations` exists for exactly that period; their figures are not fully comparable with the other years. The discontinued amount is never added.
  - **Gap**: a break in the series, a period that does not start the day after the previous one ends; `gaps` lists the period end before each break. A gap is never bridged; a rejection for too few consecutive years names each one (`gap_after:<period end>`).
  - Version 1 read one concept only, the input spec's first operating-cash-flow concept; it has no fallback periods and reports the gaps it has.
  - Parameters live in the packaged resource `src/option_trader/valuation/params/own_record_v2.toml`; `own_record_v1.toml` is kept so a stored version-1 record can be explained.
- **Approval notice** (method `approval_notice`, version 1): what the system tells the owner about one approved price at the moment of approval: the growth of owner earnings the price requires under the reverse DCF at the main discount rate, the company's own ten-year record, one base-year fact, the implied market value, the last stored stock price and what changed since the previous approval; a required reason sentence in the cases; it never blocks a price by itself and is never recomputed. It is printed by `policy-approve` before the owner confirms and stored with the policy (table `approval_notice`); the batch field `reason` carries the owner's reason sentence. Parameters live in the packaged resource `src/option_trader/valuation/params/approval_notice_v1.toml`.
- **Implied discount rate**: the annual discount rate (decimal) at which the reverse DCF value equals a supplied price for a stated growth; unavailable when the price is outside the solver range. A model output, not an expected return.
- **Return at price** (method `price_return/1`, ADR 0023): the yearly rate (decimal, yearly compounding) at which ten yearly cash amounts per share and a growing perpetuity after them equal a supplied per-share cost, under a named scenario. It is a rate under stated assumptions, not a value, a price or a forecast, and it gates nothing.
  - The four scenarios, each believing one thing more than the first and the last both: `SPENDING_STAYS` (strict base, low path, every year paid out), `SPENDING_EARNS` (earnings base, low path, each year paying `1 − g / R`), `ANALYST_PATH` (strict base, analyst path, every year paid out, no `R`) and `SPENDING_EARNS_ANALYST` (earnings base, analyst path, each year paying `1 − g / R`).
  - **Analyst growth**: the compound yearly rate (decimal) from the latest reported company-wide revenue to the last of 2 to 9 forecast years of analysts' revenue, in the filings' currency. A first forecast year outside 0.5 to 2 times the reported revenue is of another scale and is not used (`ANALYST_SCALE`).
  - **Plant fact**: revenue ÷ year-end gross plant over the latest five years, the median (`usual`) and the latest year's (`latest`), two decimal places. A latest figure more than `plant_drop` (0.20) below the usual one marks the two scenarios on the earnings base (`PLANT_REVENUE_FELL`); it omits nothing.
  - **Latest level**: beside `SPENDING_STAYS` and `ANALYST_PATH`, the same path on the latest year's own strict figure per share (`latest_base`) when that is above zero and below the base; at or below zero the scenario is flagged `LATEST_LEVEL_NOT_POSITIVE`.
- **Return at a price (card)** (ADR 0023): the page's section of one company's `Return at price` rates at its costs (the spot, then up to three of the strikes of the shown put offers), four lines per cost, each a percent with two decimals: ① 建设按五年通常水平花，慢增长, ② 建设回落，按公司过去的水平见效, ③ 建设照常花，收入按分析师预测 and ④ ②③同时成立（最乐观）; lines ① and ③ also show the rate at last year's spending. A line left out prints `—`. It states no value and no verdict, is never ranked or filtered, and gates nothing; the ten-year Treasury yield stands once beside it and no cell compares a rate with it.
- **Strict base**: latest revenue × the median over the latest 5 fiscal years of (operating cash flow − capital spending − stock compensation) ÷ revenue, divided by the current diluted shares; per share, in the reporting currency. A return at price is refused when it is at or below zero.
- **Earnings base**: the strict base's rule applied to operating cash flow − depreciation and amortisation − stock compensation, so depreciation stands in for the spending that keeps the business as it is. It is unknown when a depreciation figure of the 5 years is missing.
- **Reinvestment return (R)**: the median over the latest 5 fiscal years of the year's earnings figure ÷ the previous year end's common equity, an annual decimal, at most 0.25. A year that grows by `g` pays out `1 − g / R` of its earnings; R is unknown without positive equity, and below 0.08 the scenarios that use it are left out.
- **Analyst snapshot** (ADR 0025): the analysts' forecasts of one vendor (Financial Modeling Prep, annual estimates) for one stock as one fetch gave them: per fiscal year the average, low and high of earnings per share (per share) and of revenue (company-wide), in the vendor's reporting currency, with the number of analysts behind each; exact decimals, as sent. Third-party data for one labelled line of the valuation card: never an input of an offer, a filter or an order of a page, and never a reported figure. The vendor gives no as-of date, so a snapshot is dated by its fetch time (UTC) alone; every attempt is stored append-only with an outcome (`ok`, `empty`, `not_covered`, `error`), and an `ok` fetch whose figures equal the previous one's names the fetch that holds them (`same_as`). A **forecast year** k is the row whose period end is within 45 days of the last reported fiscal year end moved on k years; it qualifies with a value above zero and at least 5 analysts. A **forecast run** is the consecutive qualifying forecast years from year 1, at least 3, with its `rate`: the compound yearly rate from its first year to its last, a decimal with six places, half-even. A snapshot more than 90 days old, or fetched on or before the day the latest annual report was filed, gives no reading. Parameters in `valuation/params/analyst_v1.toml`. Nothing reads it yet.
- **Five questions** (method `five_questions`, version 1): the ratios of a company's reported annual figures over the newest 10 fiscal-year ends that have operating income (at least 6, else `NOT_ENOUGH_YEARS`), display only: no rule, gate, grade, size or offer reads them. A verdict is `pass`, `edge` or `fail`; a question that cannot be read has none. The fifth question is the owner's and has no figure.
  - **NOPAT** (USD): operating income × (1 − tax rate); the rate is income tax ÷ pretax income clamped to 0.15…0.28, and 0.28 when either is missing or pretax income is not positive.
  - **Tangible capital** (USD): property and equipment + receivables + inventory − payables at the year end; a missing receivables or inventory counts as zero, a missing property or payables leaves the year without capital.
  - **Strict free cash flow** (USD): operating cash flow − capital spending − stock compensation (missing stock compensation counts as zero).
  - Q1 return on tangible capital: yearly NOPAT ÷ capital (decimal), capped at 1; `pass` at mean ≥ 0.20 and worst ≥ 0.10, `edge` at mean ≥ 0.15 and worst ≥ 0. Q2 capital that growth costs: new capital ÷ new NOPAT between the first and last year with both; `pass` below 1, `edge` up to 3. Q3 pricing power: gross (else operating) margin, mean of the first three against the last three years, each rounded to a whole percentage point; `pass` when the last is not below the first. Q4 cash behind the profit: Σ strict free cash flow ÷ Σ net income; `pass` at ≥ 0.8, `edge` at ≥ 0.7.
  - Parameters live in the packaged resource `src/option_trader/valuation/params/five_questions_v1.toml`.

## Reference data (ADR 0015)

- **Reference data (参考数据)**: external series, events, classifications, quote rules and owner files governed by ADR 0015; unavailable, missing or stale data is Unknown with a reason.
- **Central bank meeting dates (央行会议日期)**: FOMC and BOJ meetings typed by hand from the banks' published schedules into `refdata/reference/central_bank_meetings.toml`, one row per meeting with its source URL and the day it was read; dates only, in the issuer's local calendar (Washington for FOMC, Tokyo for BOJ). The decision day of a meeting is its last day. A bank reads Unknown with the reason `no_future_meeting` (no meeting with a last day on or after today) or `next_year_missing` (from 1 December, no meeting in next year); otherwise its meetings are Available. The Fed's dates are tentative until confirmed at the meeting before. Display only: nothing votes, offers, sizes or gates on it (ADR 0015).
- **BOJ policy target (日本央行政策利率)**: the BOJ's guideline for the uncollateralized overnight call rate ("around x percent"), in percent, typed by hand from the BOJ's statements into `refdata/reference/boj_policy_target.toml`, one row per decision reviewed (unchanged ones included) with its quoted sentence, source URL and the day it was read. Announced (the statement's day) differs from effective (the day the guideline starts): a target announced but not yet effective is shown beside the one in force. It reads Unknown with the reason `meetings_unknown` (the BOJ meeting dates are Unknown), `unreviewed_meeting` (a scheduled meeting ended more than one weekday ago, Monday to Friday without holidays, and no row has its decision day) or `no_decision_yet` (no row is effective yet). An unscheduled decision cannot be seen until its row is typed. It is not the OECD call-money average (`IRSTCI01JPM156N`) and not a market rate. Display only: nothing votes, offers, sizes or gates on it.
- **Reference data status**: Fresh, Stale, Missing at source or Never fetched per registered series; only Fresh displays a current value, while Unknown may show a dated last valid value separately.
- **Freshness (数据时效)**: whether the stored as-of of a dataset is the one its refresh schedule (the `freshness` rule in `datasets.toml`) says should exist by now, given the market calendar. A row without a rule says `unmodelled = true`. The states:
  - **FRESH (及时)**: the stored as-of is the one the schedule says should exist now, or later.
  - **LATE (迟到)**: one expected update is missing and the next deadline has not passed.
  - **STALE (过期)**: the next deadline has passed too (two or more sessions behind), or, for weekly, monthly and quarterly data, the allowance after the due day (`stale_after_days`, 3, 7 and 21 calendar days by default) is used up or two releases are missing. Nothing stored is STALE and marked never fetched.
  - **UNKNOWN**: no schedule is modelled; the date is shown and nothing is claimed. Never FRESH.
  - **INVALID**: the stored as-of is in the future (later than today in the rule's timezone).

  A closed market is not STALE (休市 is not 过期): a weekend or a holiday has no deadline. The **build target** is the session being prepared (the latest session whose publish instant has passed); the **source target** is the period the source data covers, for example the previous close (the build target moved back by `source_offset_sessions` sessions); the expected as-of is the source target. Lag is counted in sessions (due dates for slower periods) after the stored as-of up to the expected one. It is not the Reference data status above, which still decides Fresh and Stale by the age of the last fetch.
- **Dataset registry (数据集注册表)**: the packaged ADR 0015 definitions of dataset and series ids, sources, source-native units, maximum ages, refresh groups, rule usage and labels. Registry units retain the source convention (for example, `cash_rate` is `percent`); readers or consumers convert at the boundary before calculations, including conversion to the annual, continuously compounded decimal interest rate `r` when needed.
- **SIC code**: four ASCII digit SEC Standard Industrial Classification code; missing or stale codes are Unknown.
- **Entity type**: SEC submissions entity classification, such as `operating` or `other`; an empty value is Unknown.
- **Filing index**: per SEC registrant, the stored filing rows (accession, form, filing date, report date, 8-K items, acceptance time in UTC) of every form; earnings events are derived from them at read time and not stored.
- **Result day**: the day a results announcement is taken to have been made: the filing date of an `8-K` with item 2.02, or its report date when earlier (bounded); for a period without one, the filing date of its `10-Q`/`10-K` (ADR 0016).
- **Earnings window (estimated)**: the first and last calendar date between which the next results announcement is expected under rule `sec_filing_estimate/1`; an estimate, and "unknown" when none can be given (ADR 0016).
- **Earnings-exempt fund**: a fund on the owner-set, versioned list `earnings_exempt_v1.toml` of funds that have no earnings: a broad index fund or a physical commodity trust, no other kind. Its earnings state is "not applicable" only when the stored broker contract id (`con_id`) of the one US stock with its symbol equals the listed one; a mismatch is unknown. Absence of filings never makes a fund exempt (ADR 0016).
- **Earnings acknowledgement**: the owner's stored decision, for one US stock and one results window, to see its puts although results come before expiry, with a reason sentence; entered with `earnings-acknowledge` and kept append-only in `earnings_acknowledgement` (a withdrawal is a new row; the current record is the row with the highest id). The window is the qualified estimated earnings window (source `estimated`) or the single day of a known date (source `confirmed`), first and last calendar date included. It applies only while it is the current record, the stock's present window overlaps the acknowledged one and today is not after the acknowledged window's end; it never carries to the next quarter (ADR 0016).
- **Vendor results date (供应商财报日)**: a results announcement date as a third-party calendar (Alpha Vantage) lists it for a watched US stock, stored once a day per issuer (SEC CIK) with the status `listed` (calendar date `report_date`, with the fiscal period end, the vendor's time-of-day text and the vendor's estimate figure as given, its currency not stored, each possibly missing) or `absent` (the calendar does not hold the symbol). It is not the estimated earnings window and not a confirmed date. Since short put rule version 3 (owner decision 2026-10-08) gate 4 reads it for an issuer with a qualified, not overdue estimated earnings window: over the sources observed at most 2 days before the scan's US calendar day, the earliest listed date from that day on, over every symbol of the issuer. The result is a record with a status: `used` (inside the window and *timed*, that is a row of that date has the time-of-day text `pre-market` or `post-market`, stripped and lower-cased; the earnings state becomes `VENDOR` on that date), `untimed` (inside the window without such a text: treated as not yet announced), `outside_window` (not trusted), `absent` (no fresh source lists a date) or `stale` (every source is older than 2 days). Timed is a necessary condition, never a confirmation by the company: the report marks every vendor date with `?`. No estimate and no valuation reads it (ADR 0015, ADR 0016).
- **Spans results**: a fact of one put contract (`spans_results`), true when the company's results are known or estimated to come on or before the contract's expiry and the date can be trusted: a confirmed date from the evaluation day to the expiry, both included, or, with the "estimate pass" policy on, the date of a qualified estimate on or before the expiry. Since short put rule version 3 that date is the `used` vendor results date when there is one (a fresh, timed third-party date inside the window); otherwise the start of the estimated earnings window, or a vendor results date outside the window when that is earlier. An `untimed`, `absent` or `stale` vendor record changes nothing, and a vendor date never makes a blocked stock span or pass. In rule version 2 the date was always the start of the window. It is not a status and not a reason code: a spanning contract can be PASS, UNKNOWN or REJECT at the other gates. A spanning contract is never counted or shown as an ordinary pass; the report shows it in the group "跨财报" with the results date or window and a fixed warning, and the owner decides. Dates that cannot be trusted (unknown, overdue, unqualified estimate, estimate not enabled) never make a contract span results; they stay UNKNOWN. Stored with each evaluation since short put rule version 2, with the vendor record since rule version 3; a stored evaluation of rule version 1 reads as false (ADR 0009, ADR 0016).
- **Held results mark**: the results day of a held structure as one mark (`EarningsMark`, `reports/results_note.py`), display only: a confidence, a day and, for an estimate, a window; the source and the qualification never show. The confidences are `confirmed` (the company's date inside the term), `third_party` (a vendor date inside the term, not contradicted by the window), `estimate` (a window touching the term; its day is the middle of the window, rounded down) and `unknown`; when they disagree the order of trust is that order. The chip has three forms: `E 10-28` (confirmed), `E 10-28?` (third party) and `E 约 11 月初|中|末` (estimate, by the middle day: 1-10, 11-20, 21 onward); `unknown` has no chip and is named only in the quiet line (`财报日：KO 第三方未确认，XOM 只有粗估，NKE 未知。`). It is not "Spans results", which the put cards keep.
- **Results move (财报日波动)**: of one past results day R of a stock (its release date from the stored SEC filing index; a period without a release date is left out), measured on the stored daily closes. The base is the last usable close dated before R and at most 5 calendar days before it; the *two-day move* is the first usable close after R (at most 5 calendar days after) divided by the base, minus 1, a decimal fraction. Two days because the hour of the release is not stored: a release after the close moves the next day and one before the open moves the same day, and the window from the close before R to the close after R covers both. The *21-day decline* is the lowest of the first 21 usable closes from R on divided by the base, minus 1, never above zero. Closes are not adjusted for dividends. Display only (`earnings.results_moves`).
- **Results risk (财报风险)**: the lines a spanning put card prints from a stock's results moves: how many moves, since which year, the worst and the typical (median of the absolute values) two-day move, the worst 21-day decline, how many past two-day falls went below the strike's distance from the price, and the five worst two-day moves. It is historical: the worst seen, not a limit; a count is not a probability. Fewer than 4 moves print only that the history is missing or too short. Display only: no gate, grade, size, offer, headline or summary line reads it.
- **Earnings backtest**: replay of the earnings window rule on a company's own past days; "qualified" means at least `min_quarters + 1` periods and at least one tested day, no result day before a window start, and every tested day had a window or was overdue (ADR 0016).
- **Step (close series)**: in a stored or received daily `close` series, a pair of consecutive dates that have a value (dates with a missing value are skipped) where `after > bound × before` or `after × bound < before`; equality is no step. It is dated by the later date. `bound` is 2 for `US` and 1.4 for `JP`. It is what an unadjusted split leaves in a series; a split smaller than the bound is not a step. A step that is not accepted is an **open step**: an answer with one is refused (`unadjusted_answer`), and a stored series with one is requested whole until it is repaired (ADR 0015).
- **Accepted step**: a step listed in `volatility/accepted_steps.toml` by contract id and day, with its kind: `real` (a real price move; the series is used across it) or `source` (the source itself sends the step; the series is used from that day on) (ADR 0015).
- **Usable from**: of a close series on one view (now, or as of a past moment): the day of its latest step that is open or accepted as `source`; none when it has no such step. Readers use only the closes dated on or after it (`volatility.closes.usable_closes`) (ADR 0015).
- **Market regime (市场状态)**: display-only context describing volatility, credit stress, yield-curve shape and the policy rate; no rule or candidate gate reads it.
- **Market weather (市场天气)** (ADR 0026): a display-only reading of public FRED series under rule `gauge/1`: three votes (volatility, trend, credit), a state and one liquidity figure. The state is `BAD` with at least `bad_votes` (2) known bad votes, `NORMAL` when so many votes are known and clear that 2 bad votes are impossible (3 − `bad_votes` + 1, which is 2 for the packaged value), else `UNAVAILABLE`. The liquidity figure is the 13-week change, a fraction, of Fed total assets − Treasury general account − overnight reverse repo in billions of dollars (the first two are published in millions and converted at the reading); it is labelled `FALLING`, `RISING` or `LITTLE_CHANGED` and never votes. No offer, size, limit, tenor, rule or stored row reads it. Parameters in `macro/params/gauge_v1.toml`.
- **Vote**: one of the three answers of the market weather, each bad, clear or unknown. Volatility: the VIX (index points) or VIX ÷ 3-month VIX; trend: the S&P 500 close ÷ the mean of its latest 200 closes; credit: the high-yield spread (percent) or its change over 60 observations (percentage points). A vote dated more than 3 weekdays (Monday to Friday, no holidays) before the day of the reading, or without a figure, is unknown and counts neither way.
- **Banded test**: a test of a sequence of figures with two levels. It starts clear; figure by figure in date order, beyond the `enter` level it becomes bad, beyond the `clear` level on the other side it becomes clear, and in between it stays what it was. Its answer is its state after the last figure.
- **Release calendar (event)** (发布日历): the dates of four US data releases as FRED lists them (`fred/release/dates`): Consumer Price Index (release `10`), Employment Situation (`50`), Gross Domestic Product (`53`) and Personal Income and Outlays (`54`), stored as events in `event_observation`, a whole picture per fetch. A row is a calendar date with no time of day, with a status: `scheduled` (on or after the New York date of the fetch), `released` (before it), `moved` or `cancelled` (not given by FRED). A release reads Unknown with the reason `never_fetched`, `stale` (its newest successful fetch is more than 7 days old) or `no_future_date` (no `scheduled` date within `NEXT_DATE_WITHIN`, 45 days from today); otherwise its rows are Available. FOMC and BOJ meeting dates are not in it; they are in the central bank meeting dates. Display only: no rule reads it (ADR 0015).
- **Regional manufacturing survey (diffusion index)** (地区制造业调查): the Philadelphia Fed (`GACDFSA066MSFRBPHI`) and Empire State (`GACDISA066MSFRBNY`, seasonally adjusted) manufacturing surveys, current general activity, in dataset `economy`: the share of firms reporting an increase minus the share reporting a decrease, in index points; 0 = no change against the previous month. Not a PMI (ISM and S&P Global PMI are licensed and not bought) and the page draws no 50 line. Display only: nothing votes, offers, sizes or gates on it.
- **Chicago Fed National Activity Index (3-month average)** (芝加哥联储全国活动指数三个月均值): FRED `CFNAIMA3` in dataset `economy`, in index points; 0 = growth at the historical trend. A composite of 85 data series, not a survey. Display only: nothing votes, offers, sizes or gates on it.
- **BOJ total assets** (日本央行总资产): FRED `JPNASSETS` in dataset `economy`, stored as FRED sends it in 100 million yen (`jpy_100_millions`); the macro page divides by 10,000 for trillion yen (`万亿日元`), two decimals half up. Display only: nothing votes, offers, sizes or gates on it.

## Trade history (ADR 0017)

- **Flex report**: IBKR's trade report for one configured query and date window, fetched from the Flex Web Service and kept as sent in the private raw store.
- **Trade row**: one `<Trade>` execution of a Flex report with all its attributes as sent (text, uninterpreted), stored under the account alias and keyed by level of detail and `transactionID`.
- **Section row**: one row of a registered section of a Flex report (daily net value `nav_day`, change in net value `nav_change`, cash transaction `cash`, open position `open_position`) with all its attributes as sent (text, uninterpreted), stored under the account alias in `flex_section_row` and keyed by section and the values of the section's key attributes joined with `:` (ADR 0017, 2026-10-13). Nothing reads it yet.
- **Trade row revision**: a stored version of a trade row; a row IBKR later changes becomes the next revision, and earlier revisions are never updated or deleted.
- **Lot**: the quantity one opening trade row (`openCloseIndicator` `O`) opened, signed (short = negative), with its opening cash `proceeds + ibCommission + taxes` in the contract's currency; closing rows close lots last-in-first-out (rule `option_books/1`).
- **Option book**: the group a lot belongs to, decided at its opening: `sold`, `protective` (the long leg of a spread, bought with the same `orderTime` as its sold leg), `bought_long_dated` (bought with 180 calendar days or more to expiry) or `bought_other`. **Book A** = `sold` + `protective`.
- **Closed position**: one contract in one account from its opening until nothing of it is open; the unit of wins and losses. A roll is two closed positions, and so is a contract sold again after it was closed.
- **Option result**: the summary object of the option books through one day (`option_result/1`), per account and contract currency: book A's realised option result by month, how the sold parts ended, closed positions won and lost, the premium collected and the share of it kept, and the shares and cash that assignments and exercises moved. What happens to assigned shares afterwards is not part of it.
- **Realised option result**: for a closed part of a lot, its share of the opening cash plus its share of the closing cash, in the contract's currency, recognised on the closing row's trade date. Derived on read, never stored.
- **`base_at_close`**: a realised option result × the closing row's `fxRateToBase`, in the account's base currency.
- **Unmatched close**: the part of a closing row for which no lot is open; it is in no book.
- **Not compared**: a closing row left out of the control against the broker's `fifoPnlRealized`: a trade or expiry row with an unmatched close, or an assignment or exercise row that closed nothing. Counted on the control line (`checked N, equal N, different N, not compared N`).
- **Assignment bridge**: the opening cash of the parts closed by assignment or exercise rows (also when another part of the row is an unmatched close): the amount by which the realised option result differs from the broker's `fifoPnlRealized`, which is 0 on those rows.
- **Delivery close**: the per-share `closePrice`, in the contract's currency, on the broker's own stock rows of an assignment or exercise (same account, underlying and trade date, code `A` or `Ex`). It is known only when it is above zero and the same on every such row.
- **Chain result**: the books' result of every member of a chain, commissions and taxes inside, in the contract's currency. It is premium only: nothing is added or taken away for shares a delivery moved (taking the shares is not the option's loss; ADR 0017, "Comparisons on chains"). The ledger page (成交成绩账本, `reports/ledger_page.py`) shows chains and their comparisons and computes nothing. The win count of the ledger page judges an assigned sold put or put spread by its judged result, not by this figure.
- **Judged result**: of a closed chain, the figure its win or loss is counted by (`ClosedChain.judged`). For a chain of a sold-put group (`put`, `put_spread`) with at least one delivery it is `chain result − Σ shares × (strike − delivery close)` over its deliveries, in the contract's currency, unrounded, with the shares signed as the account sees them (received positive, delivered away negative); it is unknown when the delivery close of any of them is unknown, with no fallback to the strike, another day's close or a stored close series. For every other closed chain (no delivery, or another group: a covered call called away and a bought call exercised are not judged by a stock price) it is the chain result. A chain wins when its judged result is above zero; exactly zero is not a win. It is a count only (`wins` of `judged_count`, with `not_judged`, per group, account and currency): it enters no other figure, judged amounts are never summed, and since it uses only the chain's own rows it never changes with later prices.
- **Closed total**: the sum of the chain results of the closed chains of one account and contract currency, whatever their group; the ledger page's headline (`CurrencyComparison.closed_total`); never across currencies or accounts.
- **Reference capital**: of a member of a chain on one night (a calendar date from the chain's opening day to the day before its closing day, weekends included), the cover evaluated that night: its sold contracts open are covered by its other put contracts open that night (the protective lots of the books and the bought lots of a same-day pair), highest strike first, each used once; a covered contract counts width × 100 and an uncovered one its strike × 100. The chain's reference capital of a night is the sum over its members. Without a strike there is none (`terms_unknown`), and the groups other than sold puts and put spreads have none. It is a comparison base on full cash: no account is said to have set that cash aside.
- **Capital-nights**: for a chain that began as a sold put or put spread, the sum over its nights of the reference capital, an amount of the chain's currency × nights.
- **Peak outlay**: for a chain that began as a bought call, the largest cumulative net cash paid out over its life.
- **Holdings checkpoint**: a stored account snapshot used as evidence of the shares one account held of one underlying at the end of the snapshot's day (UTC calendar date of its `finished_at`): one that names the account and has no stock row of that account and underlying dated inside its window (`flex/holdings.py`, ADR 0027). Only when every checkpoint reconciles through the stock rows (state `agreed`) is the newest one handed on as the holding.
- **Counted back, not checked**: the mark of a covered call that is covered only by counting back from the holding and was opened before the earliest holdings checkpoint's day of its account and underlying (ADR 0027). It changes no kind and no figure; the page prints it as 未核对.
- **Bill hurdle**: what the reference capital of a closed USD chain would have earned in Treasury bills, `Σ capital × rate / 365` over its nights (simple interest), each night at the latest bill rate (annual decimal) observed on or before it and at most 7 calendar days earlier; unknown, never partial, when a night has no such rate. A comparison figure only: it is never subtracted from a result, and JPY has none.
- **Cohort rates**: of a bill cohort (the closed chains of a group that have a bill hurdle), its result and its hurdle each as a simple annual rate on the cohort's capital-nights, actual days over 365 (ACT/365) on reference capital: `realised_rate = result × 365 / capital-nights` and `bill_rate = hurdle × 365 / capital-nights` (`BillCohort`), decimal fractions, none when the capital-nights are zero. Closed chains only. The two are read side by side: the bill is a reference and is never deducted, and no difference is printed.
- **Distance out of the money** (`otm`): for an open chain of a sold group, how far the latest close is from the nearest sold strike still open, as a decimal fraction of the close: the smallest of `(close − strike) / close` for a put and `(strike − close) / close` for a call over the sold parts still open; negative when that strike is in the money, and bought parts never enter it. None without a usable close, a known strike or an open sold part. A distance, not a probability and not a valuation.
- **Stock change** (of a chain): for a closed chain of the put and put spread groups, the close of its closing day ÷ the close of its opening day − 1, each the latest close on or before the day and at most 7 calendar days older. Price only, without dividends, and context, never a verdict.
- **Position** (structures, `structures/1`): the lots of one account and contract that share a span: the sold lots with the protective lots the books paired to them (a sold position), or the lots of the bought books (a bought position). Its `contracts` are unsigned; it is never split between structures.
- **Structure**: one or more positions recognised from the fills as one thing that was done: `put`, `put_spread`, `put_debit_spread`, `call_spread`, `covered_call`, `call_unseen`, `bought_call`, `bought_put`, `call_combination`, `put_call_combination`, or `unclassified`. Its realised option result is the sum of its positions'; a note on it changes no figure and no kind.
- **Call combination**: a bought call (the anchor) with the sold calls allocated to it, in one account and underlying. A sold call in it is never a covered call.
- **Put-and-call combination**: an anchor with a sold put attached to it (same day, same expiry, equal contracts, each the other's only candidate), and whatever sold calls are allocated to it. Its result is the options' only; shares delivered by an assigned put count with the shares.
- **Chain**: the structures of one account, underlying and side connected by links, a link being a part closed by a trade on the day a lot of another structure's leading position is opened (a roll). A structure without a link is a chain of one; the chain is the unit of the ledger page and its comparisons. It says that trades were connected, not what was intended.
- **Covered** (of a sold call, in structures): at the end of its opening day the account's shares of the underlying, known from the stock rows and the holding handed in, less the shares reserved for calls of before the record and for the calls already found covered, were at least `100 ×` its contracts. A sold call that is not covered and not in a combination is a **call unseen** (`no_shares` or `holding_unknown`), not a naked call: the record may simply not show the shares.

## Readings (ADR 0030)

- **Reading (读数)**: one named figure computed from stored data for one subject and one day (a market heat score, a household net value), kept in the reading store with its unit: `score`, `fraction` (a decimal fraction: `0.25` means 25%), `fraction_per_year`, `ratio`, `basis_points` (a change of a yield: one basis point is 0.01 percentage point), `count`, `money` (an amount of the reading's one currency) or `jpy_per_usd` (an exchange rate: yen for one US dollar). Its state is `ok` (a value), `missing_input` or `stale_input` (no value, a reason); a reading that cannot be given is a stored row, never an absent one. The store is not the truth: the data stores are.
- **Household levels**: seven readings of the account snapshot of a day (`readings/household.py`, version 1, `money` in USD, 2 decimal places half-even, display only). Per account alias: `account.net_liquidation` (the summary value `NetLiquidation`), `account.cash` (`TotalCashValue`), `account.stock_value` and `account.option_value` (the `BASE` rows of `$LEDGER:StockMarketValue` and `$LEDGER:OptionMarketValue`; negative when options are short), as the broker gives them and never converted here. An account whose `NetLiquidation` is not in USD has `base_not_usd` for all four; an absent row is `no_value`. For the group `household` (the account groups of `readings/params/groups_v1.toml`: `owner` and `spouse`): `household.net_liquidation`, `household.cash` and `household.stock_value`, each the sum of the account readings of the same name over every allow-listed account of those groups; when one of them has no snapshot or no `ok` reading it has no value and the reason `missing:` with the aliases, sorted and comma-separated. It is never the sum of the accounts that are there, and the daughter's account enters none. The option value is given per account only: there is no `household.option_value`. Recorded by the job `us-after-close-snapshot` only (with `serve --record-readings`), from the complete snapshots that run's own collection stored, never one of an earlier run. When the times the household members' snapshots finished (`finished_at`) differ by more than `max_minutes_apart` of the group file (15 minutes), the three household readings are `stale_input` with the reason `apart:` and the whole minutes between them, and have no value; the account readings are unaffected.
- **Stress readings**: three readings of the account snapshot of a day under the parameters of `accounts/params/sizing_stress_v1.toml` (`readings/stress.py`, display only). `account.stress_loss` (version 2, `money` in USD, 2 decimal places half-even) is the `change` of the partial expiry scenario of the account ("Expiry scenario"; negative for a loss): a position or cash balance with an unknown input is left out, and the row table `reading_stress_left_out_row` holds one row per item left out (`account`, the alias; `symbol`, the currency code for cash; `kind`, `STK`, `OPT` or `CASH`; `cause`, one of `unsupported_currency`, `missing_fx`, `missing_mark`, `missing_underlying`, `missing_contract`, `missing_balance`, `pair_leg`); a scenario that is still `UNKNOWN` (net liquidation, snapshot) has `missing_input` with `scenario:` and its reason codes, sorted and comma-separated. `household.stress_loss` (version 2) is the sum of `account.stress_loss` over the allow-listed accounts of the group `household`, each account computed separately with the same parameters, under the rules of the household levels (`missing:` with the aliases, `apart:` with the minutes, never a sum without a member); its rows (`reading_household_stress_left_out_row`) are those of its members, in member order. The macro board shows `美元兑日元 158.20（10-17 04:00 · 24 小时 +0.32%）`: the newest hour's mid with two decimals and its change, in percent with two decimals, against the mid of the same hour one day earlier, or of the newest hour at least 24 hours before it when that hour has none (`缺` without one); without an hour in the 72 hours before the page is built, `美元兑日元：没有 72 小时内的报价`. - **Net liquidity (level)** (净流动性): the reading `macro.net_liquidity` (`readings/statistics.py`, version 1, `money`, USD, scale 0, subject kind `market`, subject `US`, calendar `week`; display only, nothing votes, offers, sizes or gates on it). The value of a Wednesday is the weekly figure of the market weather (`macro.gauge.weekly_net_liquidity`: `WALCL` ÷ 1000 − `WDTGAL` ÷ 1000 − `RRPONTSYD`, in billions of dollars) × 10⁹, converted at the boundary, so the stored value is in US dollars. A Wednesday without a weekly figure has no reading. The cutoff names the newest observation of each series on or before the Wednesday. Recorded by the job `macro-readings` with `macro.net_liquidity_13w`. The macro board shows it as a chart in billions (`十亿美元`) and as a line in trillions (`万亿美元`).
- **Nikkei 225 realised volatility** (日经 225 实现波动): the reading `market.rv20_n225` (`readings/statistics.py`, version 1, `fraction_per_year`, scale 6, subject kind `market`, subject `JP`, calendar `jp_session`; display only). The same definition as `market.rv20`: the sample standard deviation (n − 1) of the last 20 daily log returns × √252 (ADR 0031), of the FRED `NIKKEI225` closes; none with a shorter history. The market page shows it with the S&P 500 figure one tap down under the index table. It is not implied volatility and not a replacement for Nikkei VI.
- **Nikkei 225 implied volatility** (日经 225 隐含波动): the reading `market.iv_n225` (`readings/statistics.py`, version 1, `fraction_per_year`, scale 6, subject kind `market`, subject `JP`, calendar `jp_session`; display only). The IBKR implied volatility of the Nikkei 225 (~30 calendar days) stored once per JP session (dataset `index_implied_vol`, series `N225_IV`), as an annualised decimal (0.28 is 28%); none for a day without an observation, nothing carried forward.
- **IV−RV20 (N225)**: the reading `market.iv_rv20_n225` (version 1, `fraction_per_year`, scale 6, subject `JP`, calendar `jp_session`; display only): `market.iv_n225` minus the 20-day realised volatility (`market.rv20_n225`) of the same JP day, a fraction of volatility, shown in vol points (the page multiplies by 100). A day lacking the IV, the close of that day or 21 closes up to it has no value. Descriptive only: the two figures have different horizons (IBKR ~30 calendar days against 20 trading days) and different sources (IBKR, FRED), so it is not a variance premium and not an expected profit.
- **Series reading**: a reading computed from a stored series of the reference store for one observation of it (`readings/macro_record.py`). Its day is that observation's period in the reading's calendar: the observation's date for `us_session` and for `week` (the week's last day as the source dates it), the last day of the observation's month for `month`; for a reading of two series the observation is the newest date both have. The day is never the date the job runs on. Recorded by the job `macro-readings` after each `reference-refresh`, with the history: every period that has no row yet or whose figure differs from the stored one, so a revised observation gives a new revision. Without a date every input has there is no period and no row. A newest period without a value is `missing_input` with the function's reason; an input whose newest observation is more than its `max_age_days` before the run's date gives one row `stale_input`, reason `stale:` and the series, for the period the run's date falls in.
- **Rows under a header reading**: the rows a reading version declares (a row table, `reading_<words>_row`, with typed columns). The rows of one reading revision are written with it, carry its key and their place under it, are never changed or deleted and are read only through it; the revision is their header. `rows_sha256` of the header is the hash of the canonical form of its rows. A reading that is not `ok` has no rows.
- **Live observation (实时观测)**: a VIX or VIX3M quote with IBKR market-data type 1, shown with its source time (tick 45) in the owner's local zone, or labelled `取于` the receipt time when the source time is absent (reading `market.vix_live`; display only). Valid only inside the CBOE session in Eastern time and when finite and positive.
- **Delayed (延迟)**: market-data type 3. Shown, labelled `延迟` with its own time; not a fallback. A pair of two delayed figures of one capture has a ratio; a delayed and a live one have none.
- **Frozen (冻结)**: market-data types 2 and 4. Never shown as live: the figure falls back to the dated FRED close with the reason `IBKR 冻结报价`.
- **Reading version**: one registered formula of a reading, `(name, version)`. A formula that changes is a new version with its own golden cases; a series is of one version, and versions are never joined. A retired version can be read and cannot be written.
- **Reading revision** (`rev`): a stored row of one reading version, subject and day. The first is `rev` 1; a later write with another value, state, reason or hash of rows (`rows_sha256`) becomes the next `rev`, and earlier ones are never changed or deleted.
- **Cutoff**: what a stored reading was computed from: the code commit, for every input its name, the date it is of and what identifies the stored data read, the SHA-256 of each file read, and for a money reading the currency basis. Stored as canonical JSON, the same text for the same cutoff.
- **Recheck (reading)**: the weekly recomputation of a sample of the stored macro and market readings (`readings/recheck.py`, job `readings-recheck`): a sampled row is recomputed from the reference store as it stood through the fetch runs its cutoff names, and is `same`, `differs` or `not_recomputable`. Kept in `reading_recheck` without a value.
- **Unread reading**: a registered version, not retired, that no page has read for more than 60 days (`reading_read`); flagged by `status --stores`, never retired or deleted for it.
- **Origin** (of a stored reading): `observed` when the row was recorded (UTC date) at most the version's `observed_within_days` calendar days after its day, else `backfilled`: computed afterwards from data as stored then. Set by the store, never by the caller.

## Reports (ADR 0010)

- **Headline (摘要)**: one answer of one published page, as final plain text, produced when the page is built and stored with the page's record (`report_headline`): a key (`sell`, `risk`, `result` or `data`), a state (`ok`, `warn` or `unknown`), one sentence of at most 160 characters, at most 8 items of at most 120 characters and the id of the page element it speaks of (or none). A page has at most four, one per key. The home page shows the headlines of the newest pages and computes nothing from them.
- **Margin watch (保证金提醒)**: a side task of serve (`notify/margin_watch.py`, parameters `notify/params/margin_push_v1.toml`) that sends Telegram messages on the owner's own margin only, never on market moves; IBKR's own margin warnings come first. It watches the accounts whose `margin_type` is `margin`, from the stored snapshots' `Cushion` (a decimal fraction; a *read* is a newer snapshot with a `Cushion`) and the stored `account.margin_gone_lower`. Levels L1, L2, L3 are Cushion below 0.50, 0.35, 0.20: each is told once (`风险提醒 L1 · <alias>`; L2 and L3 urgent) and re-arms after 2 consecutive reads at or above its line + 0.05 (`回到线上 · <alias>` once all have). `监控中断 · <alias>`: in the NYSE regular session the newest read is older than 2 hours. `风险提醒 估算 · <alias>`: a stored margin-gone fall ≤ 0.20. A message names the alias only. An urgent message is sent in the quiet hours too.
- **Index table rows (RUT, TOPX), relative performance** (display only: nothing votes, offers, sizes or gates on it, heat and ease are untouched): the market board's index table lists `SP500`, `NASDAQCOM`, `RUT` (Russell 2000), `NIKKEI225`, `TOPX` (TOPIX) and `GLD`; the RUT and TOPX rows replace the former IWM and 1306 rows. Their closes are the price-index closes of the dataset `index_closes`, shown in points, price returns excluding dividends. The ETF series IWM, 1306, GLD and RSP are unchanged and still stored; 1306 still feeds the TOPIX-17 benchmark and the July caveat of the breadth section. A *common session* of two series is a date on which both have a close; no filling and no carry-forward. The `相对` cell needs 22 common sessions, else `—`. `RUT`: 21-session return of RUT minus 21-session return of `SP500`, both between the newest common date and the date 21 common sessions earlier, in percentage points (`点`), signed, one decimal half-up. `TOPX`: the NT ratio is the `NIKKEI225` close ÷ the `TOPX` close on the newest common date (two decimals half-up), shown with its change against the ratio 21 common sessions earlier (signed, one decimal half-up). The sub-line follows the sign of the unrounded figure. When the common date is not the row's own newest date the cell says `（截至 <date>）`. The NT ratio and the points figure are relative performance of two price indices and nothing more: they say nothing about concentration, heavyweights, breadth or leadership. No percentile is shown on these rows. A coverage line gives the first stored date of each index series (within the loaded window of about 500 calendar days).
- **Household benchmarks** (display only: no offer, size, tenor or gate reads them): two lines on the household trend and the household's return over the span against each, from stored reference data only (`rates/benchmark.py`, pure). Observations are the `(date, value)` rows of the `SP500` close of the dataset `macro` and of the 13-week bill rate `treasury_13w_coupon_equivalent` (percent) of the dataset `cash_rate`, rows without a value dropped, read from 10 days before the first stored `household.flex_net_value` day through the page's day. `value_on(d)` is the newest observation dated on or before `d` and at most 7 calendar days before it, else unknown. The index return from `F` to `L` is `value_on(L) / value_on(F) − 1` (price only, no dividends; unknown when either is unknown or the first is 0). The bill return is the **Bill hurdle** convention on a unit of capital: `Σ r(n) / 100 / 365` over the nights `F <= n < L` (ACT/365, simple), `r(n) = value_on(n)` of the bill rate; 0 when `F = L`, unknown when a night has no rate. Both are unrounded. With `F` the first drawn day of the trend and `V_F` the household value on it, the line `标普 500` is `V_F × value_on(d) / value_on(F)` and the dashed line `只拿国债` is `V_F × (1 + bill return from F to d)` on each drawn day `d`, null (a break) where unknown. Under the change of the span, `同期：标普 500 {a} · 只拿国债 {b}` gives each return from the first to the last day of the span as a percent with one decimal (half away from zero, `+` or U+2212), `缺` when unknown; when the change shows its percent, `家庭比… {高|低} x 个点` follows for each known one, `x` the household percent less the benchmark's in percentage points, rounded once to one decimal, `持平` when that is 0.0. The household percent is that of the change (not time-weighted), so it is not the same measure as an index return.
- **Live figure (weather table)**: the latest accepted IBKR VIX or VIX3M quote of the ET session date (`market.vix_live`), shown beside the FRED close as `IBKR <value> · MM-DD HH:MM 实时|延迟` in US Eastern time. The ratio is shown live only for a coherent pair. Display only: verdicts stay on FRED closes.
- **FRED close date**: the date of the FRED daily close a table row is read from (the 观测日 column); FRED publishes the previous business day's close on the next business day, so it is one or two days old.

## Data quality

- Adapters never drop data silently. Each quote gets a quality flag with a reason, and calculations use only unflagged quotes.
- A quote is flagged when any of these holds:
  - a side is missing, zero, NaN or an IBKR sentinel value (for example `-1`);
  - it is crossed (bid > ask);
  - the spread exceeds the configured threshold;
  - it is older than the configured staleness limit;
  - the option, underlying and Greeks timestamps differ by more than the configured skew limit.
- Thresholds live in configuration, with defaults documented in code.
- The legacy `QualityConfig.max_spread_ratio` applies only to the M1 report; candidate
  spread eligibility uses the Decimal policy above.
- Every stored quote keeps:
  - the receive time, and the source time when available;
  - the market-data type (live, frozen, delayed, delayed-frozen);
  - bid/ask sizes;
  - the contract multiplier.

## Owner plan files (ADR 0032)

- **Target allocation (owner file)**: `target_allocation.toml`, the owner's intended split of the household net value: `us_stocks`, `jp_stocks` and `cash`, each a decimal fraction from 0 to 1, sum at most 1. Display only.
- **Price line (owner file)**: `price_lines.toml`, the owner's price per held company (symbol and market), a price above 0 in the market's currency (USD for US, JPY for JP), with the date decided. Display only.
- **Watch list (owner file)**: `watch_list.toml`, companies the owner wants to buy: a target price above 0 in the market's currency (USD for US, JPY for JP) and an optional `max_share`, a decimal fraction above 0 and at most 1 of the household net value after the price is reached. Display only.
- **Plan edit page**: `/plan/price-lines` on the report listener, the one page that writes a plan file (`price_lines.toml`), through `owner_plans/writer.py` (ADR 0032, amendment of 2026-10-19). It needs an exact `Origin` and a form token bound to the file's revision (SHA-256 hex, or `missing` for no file); a save rewrites the file in a fixed layout and keeps snapshots of the bytes before and after. Display only: it changes what the pages show and nothing else.

## Data foundation (ADR 0033)

- **Dataset**: a row of the `dataset` catalog: one source, product and method with its own cadence, `expected_by`, cursor type, dictionary and convention versions. It extends the ADR 0015 dataset ids of the "Dataset registry" (which stay source-native units and refresh groups) and is the unit that "Freshness" judges against `expected_by`.
- **Observation envelope**: the column set every observation table shares: `row_version_id`, `observation_id`, `dataset_id`, `event_time`, `published_at`, `received_at`, `recorded_at`, `source_revision`, `seq`, `supersedes_id`, `is_deleted`, `quality_flags`, `ingest_run_id`, `payload_hash`.
- **As-of**: the state of the data as known at time T, chosen by `recorded_at <= T` and the largest `seq`. It is not the SEC filed-date "As-of rule", and not the stored as-of of a dataset in "Freshness".
- **Capture**: one planned fetch of a snapshot dataset under one `capture_id`; the identity of an observation set. The slot (`session_date` + `snapshot_kind`) is not.
- **`frozen_eod`**: the `snapshot_kind` of the quarantined frozen end-of-day option capture (IBKR market data type 2, taken after the intended session). Nothing downstream reads it; it is not a `close_1600` row and never merges with one.
- **`quote_basis`**: the column of `option_daily` that says what a quote is. `ibkr_frozen` only when the contract's observed `market_data_type` is 2; NULL for every other row.
- **Owner input version**: one saved version (`input_version`) of an owner file or input, stored in `owner_input`; pages record the version they used.
- **Current table**: the silver table holding, for each observation key, the winning row version, maintained at write time (for example `bar_daily_current`).
- **Compatibility facade**: a module that answers an old store's public reads from the new tables, off by default behind one switch, with the old code as the fallback; it is not the new table's own reader.

## Automatic sector (ADR 0035)

- **Automatic sector**: the "Sector (GICS-like)" of an instrument computed from the data layer, not from the hand map `[sectors]`: owner override, then issuer resolution, then code map (SEC `sic`, JPX 33業種), else Unknown. One of 11 GICS level-1 names or "Fund: broad index"; Unknown is an unresolved classification state, not a sector name. Product routing (funds) comes first.
- **Issuer resolution**: a `resolutions_v1` row that assigns a sector to one issuer whose code is mixed (SIC 5331, 7370, 7372, 7389; JPX 3650, 5250, 9050), with evidence and issuer identity. Without it (and without an override) a mixed code is Unknown.
- **Fund bucket**: "Fund: broad index", the 12th sector bucket, holding broad index funds at their exposure multiplier. It has the same 25%-of-NLV warning limit as a sector (ADR 0035, owner decision 2026-10-28, item 3), and the Unknown total U is added to it like to the 11 sectors.
- **Rule (d) (Unknown exposure)** (`accounts/concentration.py`, `check_concentration`; ADR 0035 point 7): active only under `sector_source = "auto"` and for a candidate new short put (a candidate of no contracts is not one; a covered call or closing order never reaches it). One projected state of the account (held positions, applicable open orders, the candidate; each counted once) gives each name an exposure e = principal x leverage in base through `to_base`; principal is the cap's own (shares at market value, puts with each put credit spread at its width, see Sector cap and issuer cap measure); nothing nets. **U** is the sum of e over the names the cap cannot file (Unknown), each once, the candidate included when it is Unknown. For each of the 12 buckets (11 sectors and "Fund: broad index", empty ones too) projected_k = known_k + U, in `ConcentrationCheck.bucket_projected`; `warnings` holds `SECTOR_UNKNOWN_EXPOSURE:<bucket>` when projected_k is more than the sector cap x NLV (equal passes). A name whose leverage has no evidence (`AutoSector.leverage_evidenced` False: a fund without a `funds_v1` row) is never counted at 1x: `UNKNOWN_LEVERAGE:<symbol>` is a warning and the buckets it feeds have no figure. A warning never blocks or refuses. Reason precedence: if known_k alone is more than the cap in some bucket, the ordinary sector-cap reason is the one (`SECTOR_CAP_EXCEEDED`) and no `SECTOR_UNKNOWN_EXPOSURE` is given; otherwise `warnings` holds `SECTOR_UNKNOWN_EXPOSURE:<bucket>` once, for the breached bucket of the highest projected total (then name ascending), and `ConcentrationCheck.unknown_exposure_text` names it with U, the projected total, the cap and up to five Unknown symbols (distinct, positive exposure, exposure descending then symbol ascending), then "and N more". Both codes have Chinese text in `reports/labels.py`. The 10% issuer check is separate.
- **Sector cap and issuer cap measure** (owner decision 2026-11-02): a put credit spread, held (`put_exposure_at_width`) or candidate (`PutCandidate.long_strike`), counts at its width, the Spread loss amount `(short strike − long strike) × multiplier × contracts`, credit never subtracted; a short put without a long leg counts its strike × multiplier × contracts. The spanning cap, `put_risk` and the cash report keep their measures. Every outcome is a warning.
- **Sector source**: the top-level key `sector_source` of the limits file; it says where the sector cap reads sectors from: `"hand"` (the default and today's behaviour: the `[sectors]` map, every name at multiplier 1) or `"auto"` (the Automatic sector, with the owner's `[sectors]` entry as the override). Any other value, including `"AUTO"`, `""` and a number, fails at load; rollback is setting it back to `"hand"`. It is read in `accounts/limits.py`, never in `accounts/params/`. With `"auto"` a name counts toward its sector as its exposure times the resolver's Exposure multiplier (a 2x single-stock fund doubles, an inverse fund adds 0 to every sector, "Fund: broad index" is one bucket under the same sector cap); a name the resolver leaves Unknown follows the unmapped path unchanged (`SECTOR_UNMAPPED:<symbol>`, no sector figure, status UNKNOWN). The datasets `sec_company` and `jpx_sector33` feed rules (ADR 0015): while one is stale or never fetched, its market's names resolve as Unknown. **Migration rule**: every hand `[sectors]` entry stays as an owner override when the key is switched to `"auto"`; the drift display (`sector-diff`, 自动行业对照) shows where an entry differs from the automatic value, and deleting an entry hands that name to the automatic sector. Activating the key in the owner's limits file is the owner's act.
- **Exposure multiplier**: the leverage a fund's principal is multiplied by to give its exposure (exposure = principal x leverage): 1 for a plain fund, the stated leverage for a leveraged one, 0 for a verified inverse one. Needs product evidence in `funds_v1`; without it the leverage is unresolved and flags new risk with a warning and makes the Unknown total U unknown, so every bucket warns; never a default of 1 and never left out of U.
- **`sector-diff`**: the read-only command `option-trader sector-diff [--account-db] [--market-db] [--limits] [--day]` that prints, for every name held in each account (a stock or the underlying of an option with a non-zero quantity, in the newest stored snapshot of the alias; followed names have no account link and are not in it), `alias`, `symbol`, `hand` (the limits file's `[sectors]` value, or an owner override, or `-`), `auto` (the Automatic sector without the override, or `-`), its `source` and raw `code`, the `multiplier` (Exposure multiplier) and a verdict: `agree` (both present and equal), `differ` (both present, unequal), `hand only`, `auto only`, `both unknown`. `--day` (default today, UTC) is the day the master classifications are read on. It opens the databases read-only, reads the packaged `sectors/params` tables, writes nothing and makes no network call.
- **自动行业对照 (automatic-sector comparison block)**: the block drawn at the 行业上限未生效 lines of the sell-put list when the report is built by `report-build` with the market database present. Per account it lists the held names whose verdict is `differ` (mark `≠`), `hand only` (`仅手工`) or `auto only` (`仅自动`) with both sectors, and one footnote with the table version, the source tables (SEC SIC, JPX 33業種) and the as-of day; absent when every name agrees. Display only: no rule, status, hold or cap reads the Automatic sector, and the 行业未映射 labels are unchanged.

## Market weather gauge/2 (ADR 0026, amendment 2026-10-28)

- **收盘价来源 (close source)**: the label on a weather input saying where the date's close came from, `IBKR` (`bar_daily`) or `FRED` (`series_point`). It differs from ADR 0033 "Dataset", which is the catalog row of one source and method: a close source is chosen per date and per input, and several datasets can feed one verdict.
- **来源不一致 (source mismatch)**: the diagnostic flag set when the IBKR and FRED closes of one date differ by more than the provisional tolerance (0.05 index points for VIX and VIX3M, 0.50 for SPX). It flips no verdict and does not change the selected source. It differs from ADR 0033 "As-of", which picks one state of one dataset by `recorded_at`; this compares two datasets on the same date.
- **盘中试算 phase 2 (intraday preview, phase 2)**: a later preview of the weather from a close before the provisional calculation eligibility (20 minutes after the index's session close). Not built by the gauge/2 amendment. It differs from ADR 0033 "As-of", which is a time cutoff of stored data, not a calculation on a not-yet-eligible bar.
- **VIX / SPX units**: VIX and VIX3M are in index points; the SPX trend input is the price return index without dividends (FRED `SP500`), in index points.

## Home 期权摘要 figures

All amounts are USD, household group only (a non-member's account appears nowhere). "The month" is the US Eastern month of `day`, the latest covered day of the household's accounts. Aggregates keep full precision and are rounded once for display: `value` to the cent half up, `text` in whole dollars half up, minus sign U+2212; a signed amount that rounds to $0 but is not zero keeps its cents (`−$0.40`); zero is `$0`, never `−$0`. A missing or partial value is a named gap, never a zero.

- **Sold this month (本月卖出)**: structures with side `sold_puts` or `sold_calls` (every kind, closed or not, JPY included) whose earliest sold lot was filled in the month by the US Eastern date of its Flex `dateTime` (not the broker trade day). A structure first sold in an earlier month and enlarged later counts in the earlier month only. A roll counts its new structure.
- **Premium this month (本月权利金)**: the `household.monthly_premium_received` figure, signed. A roll adds the new opening only; closing cost is not netted, so it is not a profit.
- **Open premium (未平仓结构的开仓权利金)**: the sum of `net_opening_cash` of sold-side structures that are not closed: the original opening cash of every lot, fees included, closed lots of a still-open structure included. It is not the premium of the remaining contracts and not a value. JPY is divided by the stored DEXJPUS of the session day (`USD = JPY / rate`, the newest value on or before the day, at most 10 days old). Known limit: a short leg closed with the long kept is followed as the recogniser calls it; if it calls the rest a bought position, open premium leaves it out. A negative total is a gap.
- **Max loss (最大亏损风险)**: over the open structures of the newest complete snapshot of each gateway, the sum of the per-structure maximum losses, not the household's joint maximum. A naked put is `(strike − credit) × multiplier × contracts`; a naked call or synthetic short is unbounded (text `无上限`, `value` the bounded sum); a covered call, collar, synthetic long, undefined or unknown-terms structure has no figure and is counted (text `至少 $X`). Accounts are taken one by one: no cross-account cover, and stock never covers a short put.
- **Series**: six calendar months ending with the month. A past month is covered for an account only when a stored Flex statement span of that account runs from on or before the month's first day to at least the day after its last day; continuity inside a span is not checked. A month with any account left out is a gap.
- **≈USD**: a display-only US-dollar approximation of a JPY amount, the amount divided by the USD.JPY rate stored with the same snapshot (or the hourly USD.JPY reading, named when used), rounded half up to whole dollars and shown with a leading ≈. Never summed with USD cells and read by no rule, gate or size.

## Open-report blocks (interface revision `open-blocks-r1`)

`report_page` schema_version 2 adds six optional blocks for the open reports (US open, JP open); a close page leaves them out. The shape is `src/option_trader/viewmodels/report_open.py`; the readers and builders that fill it are later issues. Board 3 of design v2.2 is the reference.

- **Four tiles (四格)**: exactly four, in this order; each has a typed figure, a tone and an optional note. 今天的机会 is a count of the candidate rows of the report (note: how many are worth a look), unit `count`, source day the session day of the report. 本周到期 is a count of open short groups whose expiry falls in the calendar week of the session day (note: the nearest expiry), unit `count`, source day the day of the newest complete positions snapshot. 本月权利金 is the signed US-dollar `household.monthly_premium_received` of the US Eastern month of the session day (note: the part already closed), unit `usd_signed`, source day as the Home 期权摘要 figure. 被指派后现金 is the US-dollar cash left if every short put is assigned (note: its share of cash, whole percent), unit `USD`, source day the day of the newest complete positions snapshot. A tile whose figure cannot be made carries a `Gap` in the figure's place.
- **持仓提醒 (holding reminders)**: rows of one sentence, a tone and an optional same-origin link to the position; the rows to look at come first.
- **候选 (candidates)**: groups by sector, each a list of candidate rows; a **待定/看名单 (pending / watch list)** row is a name that is not a candidate yet, its text says why (for example the valuation range is missing); a covered call and a put spread are at most one each, with their own typed figures (strike, premium, annualised return, max loss). Option prices carry their quote time.
- **市场一行 (market line)**: one sentence and up to six labelled typed figures.
- **账户 meter (account meter)**: a label, a typed value and `percent`, the share of the account's limit that the value uses, whole percent 0 to 100 (`share_percent`); the tone is Python's.
- **Gap convention of these blocks** (as the earlier blocks): a block with nothing is `state="gap"` with at least one named `Gap` and no content, never a zero; `ok` has content and no gap; `partial` has content and at least one gap. - **Covered-call verdict** (`strategies/call_verdict.py`; display only, stored nowhere, computed at page build, offer rows only): the tag on a covered-call card, US (`verdict_cc_v1.toml`, excess over the cash rate) and JP (`verdict_cc_jp_v1.toml`, the lower of the excesses over the two JP rates). Precedence: stale, reject, unknown, a PASS without a usable rate or excess is 待定 (`RATE_MISSING`), a PASS with an excess below `marginal_excess_points` (percentage points; equal is not marginal) is 勉强 (`EXCESS_MARGINAL`), otherwise 值得看. Marks (results, ex-dividend, near spot, non-preferred) never change it.
