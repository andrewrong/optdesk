# option_candidates

## Option candidates

- `optioncandidates.CandidateStores` and `optioncandidates.NewOptionCandidatesJobFunc` (implemented by `internal/jobs/optioncandidates.New`; design `docs/design/option-candidates.md`, Decisions 1, 4, 5, 9, 13, 15). The job `option_candidates` is anchored at `session_close` plus 30 minutes on `XNYS`.
- For each US stock entry of the watch list it reads the newest chain day of `option_eod`, the terms (in chunks of 500 ids), the earnings rows, the newest account captures of the aliases `A` and `B` and the series `DGS3MO`, converts floats to decimal text (shortest round-trip text, then half-even to 4 places), calls `Evaluate` with `compute.KindShortPut` and writes the rows through `store.CandidateWriter` under a new run id (one UUID text per `Run`).
- A nil `Earnings`, `Accounts` or `Series` reader gives `unknown` gates, not an error. A store error is a failed run whose error text starts `optioncandidates: <code>` (`read_session_days_failed`, `read_chain_catalog_failed`, `read_rate_failed`, `read_chain_failed`, `read_earnings_failed`, `read_account_failed`, `write_candidates_failed`). A JP entry gives no row; the job names it, and a US entry with no chain, through `Skipped() []string`.
- `CandidateStores` carries a `Catalog store.OptionEodCatalog` besides the stores of the design: it is how the job finds the instrument id and the newest chain day of each symbol (with `Names`).
- `New` holds no dividend reader and imports no `notify`: the margin cushion and the assignment cash never go to Telegram.
- Contract tests: `internal/jobs/optioncandidates/types_test.go` `TestOptionCandidatesJobFuncContract`; `internal/jobs/optioncandidates` `TestRerunIsNewRun`, `TestOnlyUSEntries`, `TestStoreErrorFailsRun`, `TestJPEntryNamedNotSilent`, `TestNilEarningsIsUnknown`, `TestAliasesAandBAreSeparate`, `TestTermsChunkedAt500`, `TestStaleChainDay`, `TestSessionDaysSupplied`, `TestScheduleIsCloseAfterEod`, `TestFloatToDecimal`, `TestRateRatio`.
- `optioncandidates.NewOptionCandidatesCallsFunc` (implemented by `internal/jobs/optioncandidates.NewWithCalls`; design `docs/design/option-candidates.md#Interface`, entry 11) is the same job as `New` (same `job.toml`, schedule, plan and `Skipped`; `NewOptionCandidatesJobFunc` and `New` are unchanged) plus a `dividend.Reader` of `internal/store/dividend`. A nil dividend reader is a constructor error; a nil `Earnings`, `Accounts` or `Series` still gives `unknown` gates.
- In one run it writes the `short_put` rows as `New` does, then the `covered_call` and `held_call_guard` rows under the same run id, in one `InsertCandidates` call. It reads the chain day for both rights, calls `Dividends(ctx, id, from, day)` with `from` 14 calendar days before the run day, takes the last row (no row gives a nil `Dividend`, so the guard is `unknown`), and sets `next_ex_date` and `ex_amount` of a call or guard row from a `present` row. `OpenShortIDs` are the sorted ids of the short `OPT` calls on the underlying in the newest capture of aliases A and B.
- A dividend read error is a failed run with the new code `read_dividend_failed`; nothing is written. Ranks are per kind: `rank` is set on the `ok` rows of `short_put` among puts and on the `ok` rows of `covered_call` among covered calls; `held_call_guard` rows have none.
- Contract tests: `internal/jobs/optioncandidates/types_test.go` `TestOptionCandidatesCallsFuncContract`; `internal/jobs/optioncandidates` `TestCallsNeedDividendReader`, `TestPutsUnchangedByCalls`, `TestHeldCallGuardRows`, `TestRanksArePerKind`.

## option_candidates settings version

- `optioncandidates.SettingsVersionFunc` (`func(ctx context.Context, at time.Time) (int64, error)`; the shape of `ownerset.Reader.Version`; design `docs/design/settings-form.md` Decision 13): the version of the owner settings in force at an instant.
- `optioncandidates.WithSettingsVersion(job contracts.Job, f optioncandidates.SettingsVersionFunc) (contracts.Job, error)` sets it on a job built by `New` or `NewWithCalls`; any other job is an error. A run calls `f` once with the run's instant (the clock's `now`) and writes the value as `SettingsVersion` on every row of the run, for every kind (`short_put`, `covered_call`, `held_call_guard`). With no function, or an error from it, the rows carry nil, the run goes on and one `slog` line with `component jobs status degraded` is logged. The job resolves no parameter and imports no `store/ownerset`.
- Contract tests: `internal/jobs/optioncandidates/types_test.go` `TestSettingsVersionFuncContract`; `internal/jobs/optioncandidates/job_test.go` `TestRunWritesOneSettingsVersionOnEveryRow`, `TestNoSettingsFuncWritesNil`, `TestSettingsFuncErrorWritesNilAndRuns`.

## option_candidates need

- `job.toml` carries two `[[need]]` rows (design `docs/design/option-capture-need.md`, Decision 2): `short_put` (group `watch:options`, rights `["P"]`, ref `buy_price`, low `0.80`, high `1.05`, expiry `all`) and `covered_call` (group `watch:options`, rights `["C"]`, ref `spot`, low `1.00`, high `1.05`, expiry `all`). Every number is trial; each row's comment names its rule (Decision 6 rule 1 and Decision 11 of `option-candidates.md`).
- `optioncandidates.Needs() ([]jobs.Need, error)` returns the rows in file order with `MinDTE` and `MaxDTE` from `[params]` and an empty `TradingClass`. An error names the key, never a value, for an empty strategy or group, a ref other than `buy_price` or `spot`, rights that are not `C` and `P`, `low` not below `high`, or an expiry other than `all` or `monthly`. `optioneod.Covers` accepts a need only when the stock capture holds it.
- Contract tests: `internal/jobs/optioncandidates/need_test.go` `TestNeedsAreTheTwoRows`, `TestNeedsTakeDTEFromParams`, `TestNeedsRefuseABadRow`; `internal/jobs/need_test.go` `TestEveryDeclaredNeedIsInsideTheCapture`; `index_rows_test.go` `TestIndexUnderlyingRowsAreNotCandidates`.
