# option_candidates

## Option candidates

- `optioncandidates.CandidateStores` and `optioncandidates.NewOptionCandidatesJobFunc` (implemented by `internal/jobs/optioncandidates.New`; design `docs/design/option-candidates.md`, Decisions 1, 4, 5, 9, 13, 15). The job `option_candidates` is anchored at `session_open` plus 5 minutes on `XNYS` and its `After()` is `option_open` (design `docs/design/candidates-at-open.md#5`): the run starts when a turn of the `option_open` pull ends. A run on a pull with a key not ok starts marked `upstream_missing` and is repeated by the runner when the pull is ok.
- For each US stock entry of the watch list it reads the newest chain day of `option_eod`, the terms (in chunks of 500 ids), the earnings rows, the newest account captures of the aliases `A` and `B` and the series `DGS3MO`, converts floats to decimal text (shortest round-trip text, then half-even to 4 places), calls `Evaluate` with `compute.KindShortPut` and writes the rows through `store.CandidateWriter` under a new run id (one UUID text per `Run`).
- A nil `Earnings`, `Accounts` or `Series` reader gives `unknown` gates, not an error. A store error is a failed run whose error text starts `optioncandidates: <code>` (`read_session_days_failed`, `read_chain_catalog_failed`, `read_rate_failed`, `read_chain_failed`, `read_earnings_failed`, `read_account_failed`, `write_candidates_failed`). A JP entry gives no row; the job names it, and a US entry with no chain, through `Skipped() []string`.
- `CandidateStores` carries a `Catalog store.OptionEodCatalog` besides the stores of the design: it is how the job finds the instrument id and the newest chain day of each symbol (with `Names`).
- `New` holds no dividend reader and imports no `notify`: the margin cushion and the assignment cash never go to Telegram.
- Contract tests: `internal/jobs/optioncandidates/types_test.go` `TestOptionCandidatesJobFuncContract`; `internal/jobs/optioncandidates` `TestRerunIsNewRun`, `TestOnlyUSEntries`, `TestStoreErrorFailsRun`, `TestJPEntryNamedNotSilent`, `TestNilEarningsIsUnknown`, `TestAliasesAandBAreSeparate`, `TestTermsChunkedAt500`, `TestStaleChainDay`, `TestSessionDaysSupplied`, `TestScheduleIsSessionOpenPlusFive`, `TestAfterIsOptionOpen`, `TestFloatToDecimal`, `TestRateRatio`.
- `optioncandidates.NewOptionCandidatesCallsFunc` (implemented by `internal/jobs/optioncandidates.NewWithCalls`; design `docs/design/option-candidates.md#Interface`, entry 11) is the same job as `New` (same `job.toml`, schedule, plan and `Skipped`; `NewOptionCandidatesJobFunc` and `New` are unchanged) plus a `dividend.Reader` of `internal/store/dividend`. A nil dividend reader is a constructor error; a nil `Earnings`, `Accounts` or `Series` still gives `unknown` gates.
- In one run it writes the `short_put` rows as `New` does, then the `covered_call` and `held_call_guard` rows under the same run id, in one `InsertCandidates` call. It reads the chain day for both rights, calls `Dividends(ctx, id, from, day)` with `from` 14 calendar days before the run day, takes the last row (no row gives a nil `Dividend`, so the guard is `unknown`), and sets `next_ex_date` and `ex_amount` of a call or guard row from a `present` row. `OpenShortIDs` are the sorted ids of the short `OPT` calls on the underlying in the newest capture of aliases A and B.
- A dividend read error is a failed run with the new code `read_dividend_failed`; nothing is written. Ranks are per kind: `rank` is set on the `ok` rows of `short_put` among puts and on the `ok` rows of `covered_call` among covered calls; `held_call_guard` rows have none.
- Contract tests: `internal/jobs/optioncandidates/types_test.go` `TestOptionCandidatesCallsFuncContract`; `internal/jobs/optioncandidates` `TestCallsNeedDividendReader`, `TestPutsUnchangedByCalls`, `TestHeldCallGuardRows`, `TestRanksArePerKind`.

## option_candidates settings version

- `optioncandidates.SettingsVersionFunc` (`func(ctx context.Context, at time.Time) (int64, error)`; the shape of `ownerset.Reader.Version`; design `docs/design/settings-form.md` Decision 13): the version of the owner settings in force at an instant.
- `optioncandidates.WithSettingsVersion(job contracts.Job, f optioncandidates.SettingsVersionFunc) (contracts.Job, error)` sets it on a job built by `New` or `NewWithCalls`; any other job is an error. A run calls `f` once with the run's instant (the clock's `now`) and writes the value as `SettingsVersion` on every row of the run, for every kind (`short_put`, `covered_call`, `held_call_guard`). With no function, or an error from it, the rows carry nil, the run goes on and one `slog` line with `component jobs status degraded` is logged. The job resolves no parameter and imports no `store/ownerset`.
- Contract tests: `internal/jobs/optioncandidates/types_test.go` `TestSettingsVersionFuncContract`; `internal/jobs/optioncandidates/job_test.go` `TestRunWritesOneSettingsVersionOnEveryRow`, `TestNoSettingsFuncWritesNil`, `TestSettingsFuncErrorWritesNilAndRuns`.

## option_candidates need

- `job.toml` carries two `[[need]]` rows (design `docs/design/option-capture-need.md`, Decision 2): `short_put` (group `watch:options`, rights `["P"]`, ref `buy_price`, low `0.80`, high `1.05`, expiry `all`) and `covered_call` (group `watch:options`, rights `["C"]`, ref `spot`, low `1.00`, high `1.05`, expiry `all`). Every number is trial; each row's comment names its rule (Decision 6 rule 1 and Decision 11 of `option-candidates.md`).
- `optioncandidates.Needs() ([]jobs.Need, error)` returns the rows in file order with `MinDTE` and `MaxDTE` from `[params]` and an empty `TradingClass`. An error names the key, never a value, for an empty strategy or group, a ref other than `buy_price` or `spot`, rights that are not `C` and `P`, `low` not below `high`, or an expiry other than `all` or `monthly`. `optioneod.Covers` accepts a need only when the stock capture holds it.
- Contract tests: `internal/jobs/optioncandidates/need_test.go` `TestNeedsAreTheTwoRows`, `TestNeedsTakeDTEFromParams`, `TestNeedsRefuseABadRow`; `internal/jobs/need_test.go` `TestEveryDeclaredNeedIsInsideTheCapture`; `index_rows_test.go` `TestIndexUnderlyingRowsAreNotCandidates`.

## option_candidates shadow

- `optioncandidates.CandidateStores` gains two optional fields (design `docs/design/strategy-framework.md#6 job-shadow`, Decisions 1, 3, 4 and 6): `Strategy strategy.Strategy` and `Results strategyresult.Writer`. Both nil is the job of today; with only one set, no call is made.
- With both set, `Run` of `New` and of `NewWithCalls` builds a `strategy.Snapshot` per underlying from the same values as the `compute.CandidateInput` (`Issuer`, `UnderlyingID`, and `Context` with `earnings_state` and `next_earnings` from the row's earnings), calls `Strategy.Evaluate`, then `Strategy.Rank` over the evaluations of the run, converts each to a `strategyresult.Row` (`Legs`, `Checks`, `Facts` as JSON text with sorted keys; `RunID`, `ComputedAt` and `SettingsVersion` those of the `short_put` rows of the run, after the run id and the settings stamp) and calls `InsertResults` once, after the old insert. Covered-call and held-call-guard rows are never in `Results`.
- The shadow never fails a run and never changes its `JobResult`. A failed result insert, or a panic of the strategy, is logged with `slog` at error level with the code and `run_id`, never an error text or a row value. A run whose old insert failed does not run the shadow.
- `New` is an error when `Strategy.ID()` has no `[[need]]` row in `job.toml`; a `[[need]]` row without a registered strategy (`covered_call`) is not.
- The old table `candidates.option_candidate` keeps being written and is the one read until the cutover.
- Contract tests: `internal/jobs/optioncandidates/shadow_test.go` `TestShadowRowsEqualOldRows`, `TestShadowRunsInBothJobs`, `TestShadowFailureDoesNotFailTheRun`, `TestNilStrategyIsTodaysJob`, `TestShadowFailureIsLoggedAtErrorWithRunID`, `TestShadowRowsCarryTheSettingsVersionOfTheOldRows`; `need_test.go` `TestEveryStrategyHasANeedRow`.

## option_candidates source

- `optioncandidates.CandidateStores` gains one optional field `Open openquote.OptionOpenReader` (design `docs/design/candidates-at-open.md#1`, Decisions 1 to 4, 8 and 13). Nil is the eod-only job of today: `New` and `NewWithCalls` accept it, no mark is added and `Skipped()` is as before. The schedule is the one stated at the top of this file.
- `job.toml` has a `[source]` table: `open_min_rows = 1`, `need_high = "1.05"` (equal to the `high` of the `short_put` `[[need]]` row) and `oi_lookback_sessions = 3`. A manifest error names the key and never its value.
- Source rule, per underlying and run, for both rights (`NewWithCalls` takes its chain from the same function): `Open.OptionOpen(ctx, day)` is read once per run, rows are grouped by `UnderlyingID` and only currency `USD` is kept. Rows of status 7 (`deferred`) and 4 (`not_listed`) are dropped; status 3, 5, 6, 8 and 9 stay with `QuoteOK` false; `QuoteOK` is true for status 1 and for status 2 with a bid and an ask (a partial row has empty delta and IV). Strike, expiry and right come from `Terms.OptionTerms`; an open row with no terms is dropped and counted in the log line. The open chain of an underlying is usable when at least `open_min_rows` put rows of `min_dte` to `max_dte` days are quotes and, with a buy price, have a strike at most `need_high` times it; then `ChainDay` is the run day and the spot is the `UnderlyingPrice` of its rows.
- An underlying that is not usable, a read error of `Open` (one `slog` warning, `component jobs`, `status degraded`, never a failed run), a day with no open row and a nil `Open` take the `option_eod` chain of the newest chain day on or before the run day, as today. With a non-nil `Open` and at least one open row (or a read error) every row of such an underlying carries the mark `eod_fallback` and `Skipped()` names the underlying and the chain day it fell back to. A day with no open row at all gives no mark and no line: it is the job of today. One `slog` info line per run (with a non-nil `Open`) counts `open`, `eod_fallback`, `none`, `open_rows_without_terms` and, per underlying on the open, the usable puts at or below `need_high` times the buy price.
- Open interest: for a put of the open the job takes the open interest of the newest `option_eod` row of the same instrument (with a value above 0) within the last `oi_lookback_sessions` session days, sets it on the quote and appends the mark `oi_prior_session`. A put with none has open interest 0: no block and the mark `no_open_interest`.
- The marks `eod_fallback` and `oi_prior_session` are new.
- Contract tests: `internal/jobs/optioncandidates/source_test.go` `TestSourceNeedHighEqualsNeedRow`, `TestNilOpenIsTheJobOfToday`, `TestSourceOpenChosenByDataset`, `TestSourceDropsDeferredAndNotListed`, `TestSourcePartialWithBidAskIsQuote`, `TestSourceFallsBackPerUnderlying`, `TestSourceBuyPriceBandDecidesUsable`, `TestOpenReadErrorFallsBackAndWarns`, `TestSourceOpenRowWithoutTermsIsDropped`, `TestSourceNoTermsAtAllFallsBack`, `TestFallbackRowsCarryMarkAndSkippedLine`, `TestOpenInterestJoinedFromEod`, `TestNoEodRowGivesNoOpenInterestMark`, `TestCallsJobUsesSameSourceAsPuts`.
- Buy source marks (design `docs/design/buy-price-source.md#6`, Decisions 6 and 7): after the rule, the job appends `buy_from_valuation` (`buypriceapi.MarkValuation`) for an entry whose `BuySource` is `valuation`, and `buy_from_hand` (`buypriceapi.MarkHand`) for `hand` or for an empty source, to the marks of every row of an entry with a price (short puts and covered calls). An entry with no price adds neither mark. The job is the only writer of the two marks; no store field is added. A row stored before this design has a price and neither mark, which a page reads as `hand`. Tests: `internal/jobs/optioncandidates/buysource_test.go` `TestBuySourceMarkValuation`, `TestBuySourceMarkHand`, `TestBuySourceEmptySourceIsHand`, `TestBuySourceNoMarkWithoutPrice`, `TestBuySourceOnCoveredCalls`, `TestBuySourceKeepsOtherMarks`, `TestBuySourceStoredOnPutRun`.

## option_candidates rule version

- `rule_version` of every row is `optioncandidates.RuleVersion` (`version.go`, now `put_v2`), an exported constant. The data check compares it with the stored `rule_version` of the newest run of each kind (design `docs/design/data-check-2.md`, Decisions 1 and 2). A change of what the rule's gates or tiers write bumps it and adds a row `version: digest` to `ruleDigests` in `version_test.go`; the old rows stay.
- Contract test: `internal/jobs/optioncandidates/version_test.go` `TestRuleVersionDigest` converts the fixed rows of `testdata/rule_digest_input.json` and fails with "bump RuleVersion and add the new digest" when the version has no digest row or the digest differs.
