# underlying_resolve

## underlying_resolve

- `underlyingresolve.New(b, s, clk)` builds the job without a registrar: no company data is written (`internal/jobs/underlyingresolve/job.go`; design `docs/design/underlyings.md`, Decision 7).
- `underlyingresolve.NewWithRegistrar(b broker.Broker, s underlying.Settler, reg stockreg.StockRegistrar, clk clock.Clock) (contracts.Job, error)` (`register.go`; design `docs/design/instrument-company.md`, Decisions 8b and 9a) builds the same job and calls `reg.RegisterStock` after a `Settle` that wrote a con_id above zero (`written` true). It makes no call for `not listed`, `ambiguous`, or a `Settle` that wrote nothing. A nil registrar is refused.
- A registrar error makes the result's `Err` Retryable and never undoes the settled con_id (the name counts as `Usable`; the registration is repeated the next time the name is registered, not by the job, which asks only pending names). A Permanent refusal (a registration whose `cik` and `edinet_code` match two issuer rows) is logged with its key and reason and leaves the result unchanged.
- `underlyingresolve.RegistrationOf(row underlying.Row) stockreg.Registration` is the one mapping from a row of `settings.underlying` to a registration; the job and `ot underlying import` use it. `ConID`, `Kind`, `Symbol`, `Market`, `Name` and `Currency` are the row's; `PrimaryExchange` is the row's `Exchange` unless it is `SMART` (then empty); `MIC` is empty and `CalendarID` is `XNYS` (the values every job passes today). `Issuer`: `cik:<10 digits>` gives `Issuer{Name, CIK, Country: "US"}`, `edinet:<code>` gives `Issuer{Name, EdinetCode, Country: "JP"}`, an empty or malformed text gives nil. A Tokyo name `7203` is registered as symbol `7203`, market `JP`, with no suffix.
- Tests: `internal/jobs/underlyingresolve/register_test.go` `TestRegistrationOfIssuer`, `TestRegistrationOfTokyoName`, `TestRegistrationOfSmartExchangeIsNoPrimary`, `TestSettledConIDIsRegistered`, `TestNoRegistrationWithoutAConID`, `TestNoRegistrationWhenSettleWroteNothing`, `TestRegistrarErrorIsRetryableAndKeepsSettle`, `TestRegistrarPermanentRefusalLeavesResult`, `TestNewCallsNoRegistrar`, `TestNewWithRegistrarRequiresRegistrar`.
