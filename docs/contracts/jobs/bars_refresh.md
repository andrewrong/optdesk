# bars_refresh

## bars_refresh

- `barsrefresh.NewBarsRefreshFunc` (implemented by `internal/jobs/barsrefresh.New`; design `docs/design/market-bars.md`, Decisions 4, 6, 7 and 12): `func(broker.Broker, broker.HistoryClient, store.InstrumentResolver, bar.PriceBarWriter, bar.PriceBarReader, []WatchEntry, clock.Clock) (contracts.Job, error)`. The writer given to `New` must also be a `bar.PriceBarUpserter` at `Run`.
- `barsrefresh.LoadManifest(data)` and `barsrefresh.DefaultManifest()` read `internal/jobs/barsrefresh/job.toml`. Keys: `version`, `id` (`bars_refresh`), `backfill_days` (400), `daily_days` (5), `restate_tolerance` (`"0.00001"`, a decimal text read as an exact `*big.Rat`), `adjusted_cadence_days` (7), `backfill_names_per_run` (10), `deepen_days` (1825), `deepen_piece_days` (730), `deepen_names_per_run` (0 is no cap), four `[[index]]` tables (SPX, NDX, RUT, N225: `symbol`, `sec_type`, `exchange`, `currency`, `con_id` 0). The index exchanges and symbols are proposals the owner sets; the loader does not check them against IBKR. Seventeen `[[stock]]` tables (5 proxies SPY, TLT, GLD, DBC, USO, 11 SPDR sector funds XLB, XLC, XLE, XLF, XLI, XLK, XLP, XLRE, XLU, XLV, XLY and one industry fund SMH: `symbol`, `label`, `group` `proxy`, `sector` or `industry` (the global assets report ignores the group), `sec_type` `STK`, `exchange` `SMART`, `currency` `USD`, `con_id` 0; SPY first) are given as `Manifest.Stocks`, and `[global_assets]` `benchmark` (`SPY`) as `Manifest.Benchmark`. `New` adds the keys `stock:<symbol>` after the index keys, instrument type `stock`, basis `trades`; a watch entry of SPY (`sym:SPY`) and `stock:SPY` build together. The list is also read by the report block (`docs/design/report-global-assets.md`, Decision 11), so the collector and the page cannot differ.
- Refusals name the key and never the value: a non-positive `backfill_days`, `daily_days`, `adjusted_cadence_days`, `backfill_names_per_run`, `deepen_days` or `deepen_piece_days`; a negative `deepen_names_per_run`; a `restate_tolerance` that is not a decimal; an `[[index]]` without `symbol`, `exchange` or `currency`; a `[[stock]]` without `symbol`, `label`, `exchange` or `currency` (keys `stock.symbol`, `stock.label`, ...), a `stock.group` that is not `proxy`, `sector` or `industry`, a repeated `stock.symbol`, a `global_assets.benchmark` that is not a listed stock; a schedule without `calendar`.
- Schedule: calendar `XNYS`, anchor `session_close`, offset `10m`, `run_bound` `10m`.
- Contract tests: `internal/jobs/barsrefresh/types_test.go` `TestBarsRefreshContract`; `internal/jobs/barsrefresh/manifest_test.go` `TestManifestLoads`, `TestManifestRefusesBadDays`, `TestManifestRefusesBadTolerance`, `TestManifestRefusesIncompleteIndex`, `TestManifestDeepenZeroIsNoCap`, `TestManifestLoadsStocks`, `TestManifestRefusesBadStock`, `TestManifestRefusesUnknownBenchmark`.
- `Run(ctx, day, keys)` (design `docs/design/data-repair-bars.md`, Decisions 6 and 7): before any broker call it asserts the writer to `bar.PriceBarUpserter` and returns a Retryable error naming the missing bar store when it is not (it never falls back to `InsertPriceBars`). A day before the UTC date of the clock is a repair run: the bar query carries `End` = the day and `DurationDays` = `backfill_days` (400), and neither the weekly `adjusted_last` step nor the deepening step runs. A run of today sends no `End` and asks `daily_days` (or `backfill_days` for a first pull, under `backfill_names_per_run`). Bars after the day are dropped; a close that is not a positive decimal is Permanent and nothing of that key's pull is written. Every kept bar goes into one `UpsertPriceBars` call with `writtenBy` = `bars_refresh:<day>`; a deepening piece and the weekly `adjusted_last` window each go through the same call. A differing stored day is replaced, an equal one (`100.50` against `100.5`) is not rewritten, an empty answer replaces nothing. A restatement (a pulled close differs from the stored one beyond `restate_tolerance`) rotates no epoch: the 400-day window is pulled, then the older stored days again in pieces of `deepen_piece_days` ending the day before the oldest pulled day, down to the oldest stored day (an empty piece stops the loop), and all rows go into one `UpsertPriceBars` call after the last pull, so a failed pull writes nothing. A re-run is idempotent.
