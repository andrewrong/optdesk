# option_eod

## option_eod

`optioneod.New(broker.Broker, store.InstrumentResolver, store.OptionEodWriter, clock.Clock)` builds the job `option_eod` (type `optioneod.NewOptionEodFunc`) from its embedded `job.toml`; the design is `docs/design/option-jobs.md`. `Plan` gives one key `{obs.option_eod, SYMBOL}` per `[[underlying]]`, sorted, and a hash of the day and every manifest setting. `option_eod` is the in-session daily chain snapshot (open interest, volume, IV, greeks, bid and ask), not a close snapshot: the `[window]` rule is `in_session` with `open_after_minutes` 90 (default), so the window is the session open plus 90 minutes to the session close (`Window()` answers open offset 90 minutes, close offset 0), and the slot is `schedule` anchor `session_open`, offset `90m`, `run_bound` 16m, which is scheduler timing only: no run is refused for its time of day. A `Run` of one key reads its `day` argument: a `day` other than the date of the clock in the manifest `zone` is `Permanent` with "option_eod: day D is not the current day; a past option chain cannot be collected", calls no port and writes nothing; this holds for every caller, backfill and force included (`TestPastDayRefuses`). Otherwise it takes the underlying's price (`Last`, else `Close`, else `UndPrice` of one `Snapshot(KindSnapshot)`; none is `Retryable`), selects from the chain the nearest `max_expiries` expirations on or after the day and the strikes inside the `strike_band` with at most `max_strikes_per_side` per side (an empty selection is `Permanent`), resolves each pair with `ResolveContract` (a pair not listed gets no row), resolves the instrument ids (the option with the 21-character `osi` identifier) and asks `Snapshot(KindOptionEod)` in chunks of `chunk`, writing the rows of a chunk before the next. The clock is read before every broker call; once `budget_minutes` (default 8) have passed the run stops with `Retryable` and keeps the rows written. All rows of a key in one run carry one deterministic `capture_id`, `uuid.NewSHA1(uuid.NameSpaceOID, day|dataset|subject|slot)`, `slot` being the manifest label `HHMM` (never read from the clock; not a column and not in the plan hash), so a re-run of a slot of a day has the same `capture_id` and meets the same keys: the deployed store drops its rows, and once the store entries have landed it replaces them (`TestCaptureIDStable`, `TestTwoRunsOneCapturePerUnderlying`, `TestSlotMatchesSchedule`, `TestCaptureIDSameForRerun`, `TestRerunSameKeys`, `TestEmptySlotRefused`). `snapshot_kind` comes from the quote: `session` when `MarketDataType` is 1, `off_session` otherwise (2, 3 or 4: frozen or delayed); no calendar is read (`TestSnapshotKindFromQuote`). `freeze_after` is a manifest key that is parsed and hashed only. Status: OK and PARTIAL 1, NO_DATA 2, NO_SUBSCRIPTION, TIMEOUT, ERROR and DISCONNECTED 5 with the IBKR code or the status name as `error_code`. `Returned` is the rows written, `Usable` those of status 1; a writer error is `Retryable` with `Usable` 0. Errors of the broker and the resolver keep their kind, an untyped one is `Permanent`. 

`optioneod.NewWithWatch(broker.Broker, store.InstrumentResolver, store.OptionEodWriter, []jobs.WatchEntry, clock.Clock)` (type `optioneod.NewOptionEodWatchFunc`) builds the same job with the underlyings of the US stock entries of the watch list (`Market` US, `Contract.SecType` STK; the `[[underlying]]` tables of `job.toml` are not used; an empty result or a repeated symbol is an error). Three optional manifest keys apply only to it: `min_dte` and `max_dte` (calendar days from the run day, both inclusive; with `max_dte` set the expiries are all those with DTE in `min_dte..max_dte` instead of the nearest `max_expiries`; `job.toml` sets 21 and 45) and `max_strikes` (default 24). The strikes of an entry with a `BuyPrice` run from `buy_low x BuyPrice` to `max(buy_high x BuyPrice, spot)`, where `buy_low` and `buy_high` are two manifest keys (decimal text, both or neither, positive, low below high, else a manifest error naming the key; `job.toml` and `job_jp.toml` carry `0.80` and `1.05`, marked trial; the comment of `NewOptionEodWatchFunc` in `types.go` names the keys and no longer the numbers; a manifest without the keys keeps the `strike_band` for every entry, with or without a `BuyPrice`; the plan hash covers the two values when set, so the pinned hashes of `TestUSPlanHashPinned` and `TestJPPlanHashUnchanged` changed with this entry), exact decimal arithmetic; when more than `max_strikes` fall in the range the nearest `BuyPrice` are kept, a tie keeping the lower strike; `max_strikes_per_side` does not apply to it. Besides the range, an entry with a `BuyPrice` keeps for every selected expiry the two strikes below and the two at or above the spot (the nearest ones inside `strike_band`), both rights, no strike twice, ordered as before; the near-spot strikes need no manifest key of their own. An entry without a `BuyPrice` keeps the `strike_band`. `optioneod.New` ignores the three keys and behaves as before; a manifest without them has the same plan hash as before. `optioneod.NewWithWatchWindow(broker.Broker, store.InstrumentResolver, store.OptionEodWriter, []jobs.WatchEntry, optioneod.OptionEodWindow, clock.Clock)` (type `optioneod.NewOptionEodWatchWindowFunc`) behaves as `NewWithWatch` and replaces `min_dte`, `max_dte` and `max_strikes` of the manifest with each non-zero field of `optioneod.OptionEodWindow{MinDTE, MaxDTE, MaxStrikes int}`; a zero field keeps the manifest value, and a window with `min_dte` above `max_dte` gives the manifest validation error. `NewWithWatch` is unchanged. `optioneod.NewOptionEodHeldFunc` (the `store.AccountReader` comes after the watch list, before the clock) is declared only; it has no implementation yet.

`optioneod.NewJP(broker.Broker, store.InstrumentResolver, store.OptionEodWriter, clock.Clock)` (type `optioneod.NewOptionEodFunc`) builds the job `option_eod_jp` from its embedded `job_jp.toml`; the design is `docs/design/option-capture-jp.md#Decisions`. It has one underlying, N225 (`IND`, `OSE.JPN`, JPY, `con_id` 14087999), zone `Asia/Tokyo`, window calendar `XTKS` with rule `in_session` and `open_after_minutes` 90, slot `session_open` offset `90m` on `XTKS`, `freeze_after` 1550. It is in session like `option_eod`: a `Run` of a day other than the clock's local date in `Asia/Tokyo` is `Permanent` with the refusal above and writes nothing, and each run has its own `capture_id` and a `snapshot_kind` from its quotes. Two optional top-level manifest keys, absent from `job.toml`; each is added to the plan hash only when set:

- `trading_class`: when set, the chain is the first of that trading class on the underlying's exchange, none is an empty selection, `Permanent`; `job_jp.toml` sets `225`.
- `ledger_dataset`: the dataset id of the plan keys, default `obs.option_eod`; `job_jp.toml` sets `obs.option_eod_jp`; the table written stays `obs.option_eod`.

`optioneod.NewJPWithWatch(broker.Broker, store.InstrumentResolver, store.OptionEodWriter, []jobs.WatchEntry, clock.Clock)` (type `optioneod.NewOptionEodWatchFunc`; design `docs/design/option-capture-jp-stocks.md#2-optioneod-jp-stocks`) builds `option_eod_jp` with N225 and one key per JP stock entry of the watch list (`Market` JP, `Contract.SecType` STK; no such entry gives the job of `NewJP`, a repeated code or a `BuyPrice` that is no number is an error). The plan lists `N225` first, then the codes sorted. A `[stock]` table of `job_jp.toml` gives a stock the rest of its underlying (`mic` XTKS, `calendar_id`, `lot_size` 100, `price_increment`) and its selection (`min_dte` 14, `max_dte` 75, `max_strikes` 12, `max_strikes_per_side` 6); the manifest holds no code and no stock `con_id`, and the `[stock]` values enter the plan hash only when the job holds a stock underlying, so the hash of `NewJP` does not move. The chain of a stock is the first one of `chain_exchange` `OSE.JPN` whose trading class is the code (the class equal to the code, not `trading_class`) and whose multiplier is the `[stock]` `multiplier`, 100; its option instruments carry `option_mic` XOSE and the multiplier as lot size. Expiries and strikes follow the `NewWithWatch` rules with the `[stock]` values (`BuyPrice` range of at most `max_strikes` plus the four spot strikes of each expiry (the `NewWithWatch` rule), else the band with at most `max_strikes_per_side` per side); the 48 counts the range only; strikes stay decimal text. Four absences are a final `NoValue` whose text names the job and the symbol: no chain, or none of the exchange and class, "no listed options"; a chain of the class with another multiplier only, "no chain of multiplier 100"; no expiry in the window or no strike, "no contract in window"; a watch entry with `con_id` 0, "no con_id in watch entry", or an empty currency, "no currency in watch entry" (`con_id` checked first, before any broker call; the entry stays in the plan, and `validate` asks a stock underlying for a symbol only; the currency is not taken from the `[stock]` table). An error of `ListChains` is `Permanent`. N225 keeps its own rules, `Permanent` for a missing chain or an empty selection.

## Version history

- v1: `option_eod`, manifest version 1.
- v1: `option_eod` and `option_eod_jp` are in session: window rule `in_session`, `open_after_minutes`, slot `session_open` 90m, `Run` refuses a day that is not the current day, `capture_id` is per run, `snapshot_kind` is `session` or `off_session`; the lateness window key and the stop-time key are gone.
- v1: the capture id is `uuid.NewSHA1(OID, day|dataset|subject|slot)` (the run start leaves the formula); the manifest has `slot = "HHMM"` (1100 for `option_eod` and `option_eod_index`, 1030 for `option_eod_jp`; a label, never read from the clock; not a column, not in the plan hash; `Run` refuses an empty slot with a Permanent error). A re-run of a slot of today replaces its rows once the store entries have landed; with the deployed store (`ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`) it is dropped instead of doubled. Contracts that left the contract list of a re-run keep their rows.
- v1: `option_eod` and `option_eod_jp` watch entries with a `BuyPrice` keep four strikes around the spot besides the range (spot strikes; no manifest key, no hash change).
- v1: `option_eod` with a watch list, `min_dte`, `max_dte`, `max_strikes` (additive; `optioneod.NewOptionEodWatchFunc`, `optioneod.NewOptionEodHeldFunc`).
- v1: `optioneod.OptionEodWindow`, `optioneod.NewOptionEodWatchWindowFunc`, `optioneod.NewWithWatchWindow` (additive).
